## How Lawyers Get Away With Submitting False Documents

Let's be direct. The opposing lawyer in your custody case submitted a document to the court. That document contains statements that are **demonstrably, provably false**. Not a matter of interpretation. Not a difference of perspective. Statements that are directly contradicted by emails, text messages, court records, police reports, or official documentation you possess.

And nothing happened.

Here is why — and here is how to change that.

### The Four Mechanisms of Lawyer-Enabled Falsehood

**1. The 'Relayed Instructions' Shield**
The lawyer claims they were merely conveying their client's version of events. They didn't "know" the statements were false because they didn't independently verify them. This is the most common defense and it has specific legal limits we will dismantle below.

**2. Strategic Omission**
The lawyer doesn't lie directly. Instead, they selectively present evidence, omit contradicting documents, and frame a one-sided narrative. Technically, this is not "false testimony" — but Belgian deontological rules explicitly prohibit misleading the court through omission.

**3. The 'Client Belief' Framing**
Documents are phrased as "the client believes," "it is the client's recollection," or "according to our client." This linguistic distancing creates plausible deniability. But when the 'belief' directly contradicts documented reality, this framing becomes a vehicle for fraud.

**4. The Assumption of Impunity**
The lawyer assumes you don't have the evidence, the knowledge, or the resources to challenge them. They are betting on your silence.

⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Assuming that because a lawyer is "just doing their job," they cannot be held accountable. Belgian law says otherwise. The duty to the court supersedes the duty to the client when truthfulness is at stake. This is not opinion — this is codified in the Codex Deontologie and reinforced by the CCBE Model Code of Conduct for European Lawyers (2021 Edition).

## The Legal Architecture of Lawyer Accountability in Belgium

Belgium provides **three distinct avenues** to hold a lawyer accountable for submitting false documents or enabling abuse:

| Avenue | Legal Basis | Outcome | Timeline |
|--------|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Disciplinary** | Codex Deontologie (OVB) / Code de déontologie (AVOCATS.BE) | Warning → Reprimand → Suspension (max 1 year) → **Permanent Disbarment** | 6-18 months |
| **Criminal** | Belgian Criminal Code, Arts. 193-214 (valsheid in geschrifte), Art. 458bis (duty to report) | Imprisonment, fines, criminal record | 1-3 years |
| **Civil** | Belgian Civil Code (beroepsfout / faute professionnelle) | Financial damages, compensation | 1-2 years |

These are **not mutually exclusive**. You can pursue all three simultaneously. In fact, a disciplinary complaint strengthens a criminal filing, and both strengthen civil claims.

💡 **Pro Insight**: The strategic value of pursuing multiple avenues simultaneously is not the outcome — it is the **immediate behavioral change** it produces in opposing counsel. A lawyer who learns they are facing a disciplinary complaint, a criminal report, and potential civil liability will dramatically alter their conduct. That is the point.

## Belgian Criminal Code: The Weapons at Your Disposal

### Valsheid in Geschrifte (Forgery in Writing) — Articles 193-214

This is the nuclear option, and it is available to you.

**Article 193** of the Belgian Criminal Code defines forgery in writing (*valsheid in geschrifte*) as the fraudulent alteration of the truth in a document, with the intent to harm. This includes:

- **Fabricating documents** that purport to be authentic
- **Altering existing documents** (adding, removing, or changing content)
- **Creating false declarations** presented as truthful statements of fact
- **Causing another person to record false information** in an authentic document

**Critical detail**: Under Belgian law, a *conclusie* (legal brief/submission) filed by a lawyer is a document within the meaning of Articles 193-214. If that submission contains statements the lawyer **knew or should have known** were false, and those false statements were intended to influence the court's decision, the elements of *valsheid in geschrifte* may be satisfied.

### Penalties Under Current Belgian Law

| Offense | Penalty |
|---------|----------|
| Forgery in private writings (Art. 196) | 6 months – 5 years imprisonment + fine |
| Use of forged documents (Art. 197) | Same as forgery itself |
| Forgery in authentic/public documents (Art. 194-195) | 10 – 15 years imprisonment |

**Important**: Belgium's new Criminal Code, adopted on February 22, 2024 (Books I and II), reorganizes these provisions but **does not weaken** the penalties for document forgery. The reform modernizes the classification system but maintains — and in some areas enhances — sanctions for fraud-related offenses. Source: [ICLG — New Belgian Criminal Code adopted](https://iclg.com/briefing/20841-new-belgian-criminal-code-adopted).

### Gebruik van Valse Stukken (Use of Forged Documents) — Article 197

This is equally important. Even if the lawyer didn't personally forge the document, **using** a document they know to be false constitutes a separate criminal offense under Article 197. Every time the opposing lawyer references, cites, or relies on a false document in court — even one their client provided — they commit a new offense if they had knowledge of its falsity.

🧠 **Mental Model**: Think of it as a chain. The client creates the false document (forgery). The lawyer submits it to the court (use of forged documents). Each link in the chain is independently prosecutable.

### How to File a Criminal Complaint

1. **File with the Procureur des Konings** (King's Prosecutor) at the *parket* (prosecutor's office) of the judicial district where the false document was submitted
2. Alternatively, file a **klacht met burgerlijke partijstelling** (complaint with civil party constitution) before the *onderzoeksrechter* (investigating judge) — this forces an investigation and gives you party status
3. Include: the false document, the contradicting evidence, a timeline, and a clear statement identifying the specific articles of the Criminal Code violated

💡 **Pro Insight**: Filing as a *burgerlijke partij* (civil party) is more powerful than a simple complaint to the Procureur. As a civil party, the investigating judge **must** open an investigation. A simple complaint to the Procureur can be shelved (*geseponeerd*). If you want to guarantee action, constitute yourself as a civil party.

## The Belgian Disciplinary System — In Detail

### The Two Bar Associations

Belgium operates two bar association systems, divided by language community:

- **Orde van Vlaamse Balies (OVB)** — Governs Dutch-speaking lawyers (Flanders and Dutch-speaking Brussels bars). Website: [advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be)
- **AVOCATS.BE** (formerly Ordre des Barreaux Francophones et Germanophones de Belgique, OBFG) — Governs French- and German-speaking lawyers. Website: [avocats.be](https://www.avocats.be)

Every practicing lawyer in Belgium **must** be registered with a local bar (*balie* in Dutch / *barreau* in French), which operates under one of these two umbrella organizations.

### The Codex Deontologie (OVB) — Key Provisions

The OVB's Code of Ethics for Lawyers (updated January 18, 2022) establishes the following **essential duties** (Part I, Chapter I.1):

- **Dignity and integrity** (*waardigheid en integriteit*): A lawyer must behave with dignity and integrity in all professional activities
- **Loyalty to the court** (*loyauteit tegenover de rechtbank*): A lawyer shall not mislead the court or assist in misleading the court
- **Independence of judgment**: A lawyer must exercise independent professional judgment and cannot simply execute client instructions that violate ethical or legal obligations
- **Prohibition on assisting fraud**: A lawyer shall not assist clients in conduct that is fraudulent, illegal, or that constitutes an abuse of the legal process

Source: [CCBE — Belgium OVB Code of Ethics for Lawyers](https://www.ccbe.eu/fileadmin/speciality_distribution/public/documents/National_Regulations/DEON_National_CoC/EN_Belgium_OVB_Code_of_Ethics_for_Lawyers.pdf)

### Disciplinary Sanctions — The Full Spectrum

Per the [Orde van Vlaamse Balies](https://www.advocaat.be/en/practical/i-have-a-problem-with-my-lawyer/what-is-a-disciplinary-procedure), the disciplinary board can impose:

| Sanction | Description | Additional Consequences |
|----------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Waarschuwing** (Warning) | Formal admonishment | Recorded on the lawyer's file |
| **Berisping** (Reprimand) | More serious censure | + Ban from internal bar elections for up to 3 years |
| **Schorsing** (Suspension) | Temporary ban from practicing | Max 1 year + ban from bar elections for up to 5 years |
| **Schrapping** (Deletion/Disbarment) | **Permanent ban** from practicing law | **Forever**. The lawyer can never practice again. |

The investigation costs can also be passed on to the sanctioned lawyer.

⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Many people assume the worst a disciplinary board can do is issue a warning. **Wrong**. Permanent disbarment — *schrapping van het tableau* — means the lawyer loses their profession, their title, and their livelihood. Permanently. This is the maximum lever you can pull, and it is available for repeated or egregious violations such as knowingly submitting false documents.

## Step-by-Step: Filing a Disciplinary Complaint That Cannot Be Ignored

This is the procedural playbook. Follow every step.

### Step 1: Identify the Lawyer's Bar Registration

Determine which local bar the lawyer belongs to:
- Search the OVB lawyer database at [zoekeenadvocaat.be](https://www.zoekeenadvocaat.be) for Flemish lawyers
- Search the AVOCATS.BE database at [avocats.be/annuaire](https://www.avocats.be) for French/German-speaking lawyers
- Note: the lawyer's *balie/barreau*, their full name, and their registration number

### Step 2: Compile Your Evidence Dossier

Your complaint must be evidence-based, not emotional. Prepare:

**For each false statement submitted to the court:**
- The exact quote from the lawyer's submission (*conclusie*)
- The date and case reference number
- The contradicting evidence (original document, timestamped)
- Proof the lawyer had — or should have had — access to the contradicting evidence

**Organize into categories:**
- Category A: **Demonstrably false statements of fact** (dates, events, communications that provably did not occur as stated)
- Category B: **Strategic omissions** (evidence the lawyer possessed but deliberately withheld from the court)
- Category C: **Pattern evidence** (multiple instances showing systematic rather than isolated behavior)

### Step 3: Draft the Formal Complaint

Address it to the **Stafhouder** (Bâtonnier/President of the Bar) of the lawyer's local bar.

**Your complaint MUST include** (per [advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be/en/practical/i-have-a-problem-with-my-lawyer/what-is-a-disciplinary-procedure)):
- The date
- Your full name
- Your signature
- The lawyer's name and bar registration
- Specific deontological rules violated (cite the Codex by chapter/article)
- Factual description of each violation
- Numbered evidence index

**Structure the complaint as follows:**
1. **Introduction**: Your role in the proceedings, the lawyer's role, the court and case reference
2. **Violation Inventory**: Each false document, organized chronologically
3. **Contradicting Evidence**: For each violation, the proof of falsity
4. **Deontological Analysis**: Which specific rules of the Codex Deontologie were violated
5. **Pattern Summary**: Why this is systematic, not accidental
6. **Requested Action**: Explicitly request referral to the Tuchtraad (Disciplinary Board)

### Step 4: Submit by Registered Mail

Send via **aangetekende zending** (registered letter / *envoi recommandé*) to the Stafhouder of the lawyer's local bar. **Keep the proof of delivery** (*ontvangstbewijs*).

### Step 5: The Investigation Process

Per Belgian law and [advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be/en/practical/i-have-a-problem-with-my-lawyer/what-is-a-disciplinary-procedure):

1. The Stafhouder investigates personally or appoints an investigator (*rapporteur*)
2. You will be informed by letter
3. The investigator gathers evidence, interviews you, the accused lawyer, and witnesses
4. **You may provide additional evidence at any time during the investigation**
5. Your statements become part of the official *proces-verbaal* (police report) — you have the right to a copy
6. Decision: referral to the Tuchtraad (Disciplinary Board) or dismissal

### Step 6: If Your Complaint Is Dismissed

**You have the right to appeal.** Per the OVB:
- Send a registered letter to the **Voorzitter van de Tuchtraad** (Chairman of the Disciplinary Board) within **3 months** of the dismissal decision
- If you received no answer within **6 months** of your original complaint, you can escalate directly to the Chairman of the Disciplinary Board (you have 3 months to do so — i.e., up to 9 months after your initial complaint)

### Step 7: Parallel Actions — Amplify the Pressure

Simultaneously with your disciplinary complaint:

1. **Raise the false documents with the presiding judge** — through your own lawyer, submit a *nota* identifying the false statements and the contradicting evidence
2. **File a criminal complaint** for *valsheid in geschrifte* (see Section 11 below)
3. **Notify your own lawyer** that you intend to pursue all available avenues — this signals that you expect your legal team to be equally aggressive

## When Opposing Counsel Enables Alienation, Parentification, and Abuse

This is the section that matters most — and the one Belgian law has the most to say about.

### What Does 'Enabling' Look Like?

The opposing lawyer is not alienating your child. Their client is. But the lawyer becomes **complicit** when they:

- **Advance a legal strategy designed to reduce or eliminate your contact** with your child, knowing the underlying claims are false
- **Submit schedules, declarations, or motions** that contain fabricated or exaggerated allegations of danger, abuse, or unfitness
- **Fail to disclose** information favorable to you when legally required
- **Coach their client** on how to present alienation tactics as legitimate parenting concerns
- **Ignore visible signs** that the child is being parentified, triangulated, or subjected to psychological duress — and continue advancing the alienating parent's agenda

### The Lawyer's Obligation Under Belgian Law

Under the adversarial system, a lawyer's primary duty is to their client. **But this duty has hard, codified limits:**

**1. The Codex Deontologie prohibits assisting in fraud or abuse of process.**
If the lawyer knows their client is fabricating evidence, making false allegations, or using the legal process to facilitate psychological abuse of a child, they are prohibited from advancing that strategy.

**2. The lawyer must exercise independent judgment.**
The CCBE Model Code of Conduct (2021), Article 3.1, states: "A lawyer shall always act in the best interests of the client and shall put those interests before the lawyer's own interests or those of fellow members of the legal profession, provided that the lawyer shall not violate the law or professional rules." Key phrase: **"provided that the lawyer shall not violate the law."**

**3. The duty to withdraw.**
If a client insists on a course of conduct that requires the lawyer to deceive the court, the lawyer **must** withdraw from representation. Continuing to represent under those circumstances is itself a deontological violation.

### The Parentification Problem

When a child is being parentified — forced into adult roles such as emotional caretaker, mediator, message-carrier, or confidant ([toxic-stress research](https://developingchild.harvard.edu/key-concept/toxic-stress/)) — and the opposing lawyer sees this dynamic reflected in their own client's communications, they face an ethical crossroads.

If the lawyer's own client's text messages show the child acting as a go-between, making adult decisions, or expressing fear of a parent in [scripted language that mirrors the alienating parent's exact phrasing](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/basics/parental-alienation) — and the lawyer advances this as evidence of the child's "authentic wishes" — they are no longer passively representing a client. They are **actively weaponizing a child through the legal system**.

This distinction — between passive representation and active engineering — is the standard disciplinary bodies apply. Your evidence must demonstrate the latter.

## Article 458bis: The Duty to Report and Its Strategic Implications

### The Law

**Article 458bis of the Belgian Criminal Code** creates an exception to professional secrecy (*beroepsgeheim*). It permits — but does not mandate — professionals, including lawyers, to break confidentiality and report to the *Procureur des Konings* when:

1. They have examined the victim or received direct confidences
2. There is a **serious and imminent danger** to the physical or **mental** integrity of a minor
3. The professional cannot adequately protect the minor alone or with help

### The Strategic Implication

Article 458bis uses the word "may" (*kan*), not "must" (*moet*). This means Belgian lawyers have the **legal right** to report without violating professional secrecy — but are not strictly **obligated** to do so.

However, there is a critical nuance: while the Criminal Code says "may," the **deontological codes** impose an independent duty of integrity. A lawyer who sees clear evidence that a child is being subjected to psychological abuse — parentification, triangulation, weaponization, psychological duress — and chooses to look away, is in breach of their duty to act with dignity and integrity.

### How to Use This Against Opposing Counsel

In your disciplinary complaint, include a section addressing Article 458bis:

*"The opposing lawyer had direct knowledge, through their own client's communications and court submissions, that [child's name redacted] was being subjected to [specific behaviors — parentification, triangulation, psychological duress]. Despite having the legal authority under Article 458bis of the Belgian Criminal Code to report this danger to the Procureur des Konings, the lawyer chose not only to remain silent but to actively advance the alienating conduct through court submissions dated [dates]. This constitutes a failure of the lawyer's deontological duty to act with integrity and dignity, and a failure to protect a minor when the legal framework expressly authorized them to do so."*

This reframes the lawyer's silence not as a neutral choice but as an **active decision to enable harm**.

## The Crime-Fraud Exception: Dismantling Attorney-Client Privilege

Opposing counsel will inevitably invoke attorney-client privilege (*beroepsgeheim*) as a defense. Here is why that defense fails.

### The Rule

Attorney-client privilege under Belgian law (Article 458 of the Criminal Code) protects communications between lawyer and client from disclosure.

### The Exception

This privilege **does not apply** when:

1. **The lawyer's services are being used to perpetrate a fraud** — including fraud on the court
2. **The client is using the lawyer to facilitate ongoing criminal conduct** — including *valsheid in geschrifte*
3. **Article 458bis applies** — the professional secrecy exception for reporting danger to minors

The [CCBE Charter of Core Principles of the European Legal Profession](https://www.ccbe.eu/NTCdocument/EN_CCBE_CoCpdf1_1382973057.pdf) explicitly states that professional secrecy "does not extend to communications made for the purpose of committing a crime or fraud."

### How to Attack the Privilege Defense

In your complaint, articulate the crime-fraud exception explicitly:

*"The opposing lawyer cannot invoke beroepsgeheim (Article 458 of the Criminal Code) to shield the submission of provably false documents to this court. Professional secrecy does not extend to communications made in furtherance of fraud (the crime-fraud exception), nor to the facilitation of criminal acts including valsheid in geschrifte (Articles 193-214 of the Criminal Code). The following evidence demonstrates that the privileged relationship was used as a vehicle for fraud on the tribunal: [list evidence]."*

## Advanced Adversarial Strategy: Building the Case Against Opposing Counsel

### The Contradiction Matrix

Create a formal document — ideally as an exhibit to your submission — that maps every false statement to its contradicting evidence.

| # | Date | False Statement (Exact Quote) | Source Document | Contradicting Evidence | Source | Lawyer Had Access? |
|---|------|-------------------------------|-----------------|----------------------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | [Date] | "[Exact quote]" | Conclusie dd. [date] | Email dd. [date] showing [truth] | Exhibit A-1 | Yes — CC'd on email |
| 2 | [Date] | "[Exact quote]" | Verzoekschrift dd. [date] | Text messages dd. [date] | Exhibit A-2 | Yes — referenced in prior submission |
| 3 | [Date] | "[Exact quote]" | Conclusie dd. [date] | Police report dd. [date] | Exhibit A-3 | Yes — filed in same dossier |

This matrix does three things:
1. **Eliminates the 'isolated mistake' defense** — a pattern is undeniable
2. **Proves knowledge** — the "Lawyer Had Access?" column demonstrates the lawyer knew or should have known
3. **Creates a court exhibit** that a judge can reference directly in their decision

### The Chronological Contradiction Map

Layer the false statements over a timeline of actual events. When a judge sees that on March 3 the opposing lawyer claimed "the father did not attend the school meeting" and your evidence shows a sign-in sheet with your name on it from that exact meeting — the visual impact is devastating.

### The 'Alarming Contradictions' Standard

In Belgian disciplinary proceedings, the standard is not whether the lawyer 'knew' the statements were false — it is whether **a reasonable lawyer in their position would have recognized the contradictions**. This is a negligence standard, not an intent standard. You don't need to prove the lawyer was malicious. You need to prove they were **unreasonable**.

Document every instance where the contradicting evidence was:
- Available in the court dossier
- Sent to the lawyer directly (via email, letter, or prior submission)
- Part of common knowledge in the proceedings
- So obviously false that any competent lawyer would have questioned it

## The 'Evidence Sandwich' Method — Enhanced

For each false statement, build a three-layer evidence package:

**Layer 1: The False Statement**
- Exact quote from the lawyer's submission
- Document title, date, page number, paragraph
- Screenshot or certified copy

**Layer 2: The Contradicting Evidence**
- The original document (email, text, police report, medical record, school record) that proves the statement is false
- Timestamped, authenticated, with metadata preserved
- If from AntiAlienate: chain of custody documentation, hash verification

**Layer 3: Proof of Knowledge**
- Evidence that the lawyer had access to the contradicting information
- Was the lawyer CC'd on the email? Did they receive the document in discovery? Was it part of a prior submission?
- If the lawyer's own client sent them the contradicting evidence — game over

**Layer 4 (New): The 'So What' Factor**
- How did this false statement affect the court's decision?
- Did the judge rely on it? Did it influence interim measures? Did it affect custody time?
- This connects the misconduct to **concrete harm** — essential for both disciplinary proceedings and civil damages claims

## Criminal Complaints: Valsheid in Geschrifte and Beyond

### Filing the Criminal Complaint

**Option A: Simple Complaint (Klacht)**
- File at the *parket* (prosecutor's office) of the relevant judicial district
- Free of charge
- The Procureur decides whether to prosecute — they can shelve it (*seponeren*)
- Advantage: Simple. Disadvantage: No guarantee of action

**Option B: Civil Party Constitution (Burgerlijke Partijstelling)**
- File before the *onderzoeksrechter* (investigating judge)
- Requires a deposit (*borgstelling*) — typically €250-€1,000 depending on the case
- The investigating judge **must** open an investigation
- You become a party with rights to information about the investigation's progress
- Advantage: Guarantees investigation. Disadvantage: Cost

💡 **Pro Insight**: If you want to guarantee that a criminal investigation is opened, **always** choose Option B. The *klacht met burgerlijke partijstelling* is the most powerful procedural tool available to a private citizen in Belgian criminal law. It takes the decision out of the Procureur's hands.

### What to Include in Your Criminal Filing

1. Identification of the suspect (opposing lawyer)
2. Description of the criminal acts, referencing specific articles of the Criminal Code
3. The false documents (certified copies)
4. The contradicting evidence (certified copies)
5. A chronological timeline
6. A clear statement: "I hereby constitute myself as civil party and request the appointment of an investigating judge"

### Additional Criminal Offenses to Consider

| Offense | Article | Application |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Valsheid in geschrifte | Arts. 193-197 | False documents submitted to court |
| Gebruik van valse stukken | Art. 197 | Using/referencing documents known to be false |
| Poging tot oplichting | Arts. 496-497 | If the false documents were used to obtain a legal benefit (custody, money) |
| Meineed (by the client) | Art. 215 | If the client signed false declarations under oath |
| Deelneming | Arts. 66-69 | If the lawyer participated in or facilitated the client's criminal conduct |

## Civil Liability: Suing the Opposing Lawyer for Damages

### The Legal Basis

Under Belgian law, a lawyer can be held civilly liable for *beroepsfout* (professional fault) under the general rules of civil liability (formerly Articles 1382-1383 of the Civil Code, now Articles 6.2 and following of the new Civil Code Book 6).

### Requirements

1. **Fault** (*fout/faute*): The lawyer acted below the standard of a reasonably competent lawyer
2. **Damage** (*schade/dommage*): You suffered quantifiable harm (legal costs, lost custody time, psychological damage)
3. **Causal link** (*oorzakelijk verband/lien causal*): The false documents directly contributed to the harm

### Quantifiable Damages

- **Additional legal costs** incurred to rebut false evidence
- **Lost custody time** caused by interim measures based on false evidence
- **Psychological harm** to you and your children, documented by a therapist
- **Lost income** if false allegations affected employment
- **Expert fees** (handwriting analysis, forensic IT, psychological assessments) required to disprove false claims

⚠️ **Common Mistake**: Many targeted parents believe they cannot sue the opposing lawyer because they are not the lawyer's client. **This is incorrect under Belgian law.** A third party can bring a civil liability claim against a lawyer for professional misconduct that caused them harm. The lawyer's duty of care extends beyond their own client to include the proper administration of justice.

## CCBE and European Standards: The Continental Framework

The [Council of Bars and Law Societies of Europe (CCBE)](https://www.ccbe.eu/) sets the overarching ethical framework for lawyers across Europe, including Belgium.

### CCBE Model Code of Conduct (2021 Edition) — Key Provisions

**Preamble**: "Lawyers have a central position in the administration of justice. They defend citizens' rights by assisting and representing them, and liaise between citizens and the courts. In this capacity, they hold a key position in ensuring the trust of the public in actions of the courts."

**Article 2.2 — Trust and Personal Integrity**: "Relationships of trust can only exist if a lawyer's personal honour, honesty and integrity are beyond doubt."

**Article 4.2 — Truthfulness**: "A lawyer shall never knowingly give false or misleading information to the court. They shall not put forward any evidence they know to be false."

**Article 4.4 — Relations with Third Parties**: In dealings with third parties, a lawyer must not provide false information or use means that are unfair.

These standards apply to all Belgian lawyers as both the OVB and AVOCATS.BE are member organizations of the CCBE.

Source: [CCBE Model Code of Conduct for European Lawyers, 2021](https://www.ccbe.eu/fileadmin/speciality_distribution/public/documents/DEONTOLOGY/DEON_CoC/EN_DEONTO_2021_Model_Code.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I file a disciplinary complaint while my family case is still active?
Yes. There is no requirement to wait. In fact, filing during the case puts the opposing lawyer on notice that their conduct is being documented and scrutinized. Per [advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be), the Stafhouder can even initiate proceedings independently if they become aware of misconduct.

### Will this make me look like a 'difficult parent'?
This is a legitimate strategic concern. Discuss with your own lawyer. An evidence-based disciplinary complaint — backed by documented false statements and contradicting proof — is not 'difficult parent' behavior. It is forensic accountability. Judges in Belgium distinguish between emotional complaints and documented professional misconduct.

### What if the Stafhouder dismisses my complaint?
You can appeal to the **Voorzitter van de Tuchtraad** (Chairman of the Disciplinary Board) by registered letter within 3 months of the dismissal. If you receive no response within 6 months of your original complaint, you can also escalate directly. Source: [advocaat.be — What is a disciplinary procedure?](https://www.advocaat.be/en/practical/i-have-a-problem-with-my-lawyer/what-is-a-disciplinary-procedure)

### Can a lawyer be permanently disbarred in Belgium?
Yes. **Schrapping** (deletion from the tableau) is permanent — the lawyer can never practice law again. Per the OVB, this is the most severe disciplinary sanction and is reserved for egregious violations. Source: [advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be/en/practical/i-have-a-problem-with-my-lawyer/what-is-a-disciplinary-procedure)

### What is the difference between klacht and burgerlijke partijstelling?
A *klacht* (complaint) is a report to the Procureur des Konings who decides whether to investigate. A *burgerlijke partijstelling* (civil party constitution) is filed before an investigating judge who **must** open an investigation. The latter guarantees action but requires a deposit.

### Can I pursue disciplinary, criminal, AND civil action simultaneously?
Yes. These are independent proceedings. A disciplinary finding can strengthen your criminal and civil cases. There is no requirement to choose one avenue.

### What about the new Belgian Criminal Code?
The Belgian Parliament adopted the reformed Criminal Code on February 22, 2024 (Books I and II). Forgery provisions are reorganized but penalties for document fraud are maintained and in some cases enhanced. The core principles remain the same.

### How long do I have to file?
Disciplinary: varies by bar association but file immediately. Criminal: general statute of limitations for *valsheid in geschrifte* is 5 years from the last use of the false document. Civil: generally 5 years from knowledge of the damage under the new Civil Code Book 6.

### What if the lawyer is from a different EU country?
The CCBE's European framework applies. You can file a complaint with both the lawyer's home bar association and the Belgian bar where they are practicing under the 'double deontology' principle — the lawyer must comply with both their home country's rules and Belgium's. Source: [Balie Brussel — Rules of professional conduct for foreign lawyers](https://www.baliebrussel.be/en/im-a-foreign-lawyer/ethical-and-professional-rules/)

### Can the disciplinary board award me compensation?
No. The Tuchtraad/Conseil de discipline can only acquit or impose sanctions (warning through disbarment). For compensation, you need a separate civil court action. However, a disciplinary finding is powerful evidence in a civil damages claim.

## Final Takeaway

Lawyers are not above the law. They are officers of the court, bound by codified ethical duties and the Belgian Criminal Code. When they submit provably false documents, they commit offenses ranging from deontological violations to criminal forgery.

In Belgium, the system provides every tool you need:

- **The Stafhouder** investigates and can refer to the Tuchtraad
- **The Tuchtraad** can impose sanctions up to **permanent disbarment**
- **The Procureur des Konings** can prosecute for valsheid in geschrifte
- **The Onderzoeksrechter** must investigate when you constitute yourself as civil party
- **The Civil Courts** can award financial damages for professional misconduct
- **Article 458bis** opens the door for reporting danger to children
- **The CCBE framework** provides European-level accountability

The opposing lawyer is betting on your silence. They are betting you don't know these procedures exist. They are betting you'll be too emotional, too overwhelmed, or too focused on your children to hold them accountable.

They are wrong.

Document everything. Organize forensically. File strategically. And remember: a lawyer who submits false documents is not 'zealously advocating' — they are committing fraud on the tribunal. The Belgian legal system has mechanisms to address this. Use them.

Facts over feelings. Evidence that speaks.
