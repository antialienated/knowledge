# Press Kit — DRAFT (staged for Adam's review)

This folder contains pre-launch press materials for the AntiAlienate knowledge-base public-release announcement. **Nothing here is distributed externally yet.** All materials are staged for review and edit before any outreach goes out.

## Contents

### Releases (three angles to pick from / combine)

* **[release-v1.md](release-v1/)** — Initial launch release. "Largest open-source PA knowledge base."
* **[release-v2-evidence-launch.md](release-v2-evidence-launch/)** — Evidence-corpus angle. "10-page primary-source evidence base." Strongest standalone news angle for trade press (Above the Law, Bloomberg Law, BBC Family Court, Above the Law).
* **[release-v3-knowledge-base-comprehensive.md](release-v3-knowledge-base-comprehensive/)** — Comprehensive angle. "26 cases, 18 jurisdictions, 10 evidence pages, 12 playbooks." Strongest for general legal/family-law press and for organisational endorsements ([PASG](https://pasg.info/), advocacy orgs, professional associations).

### Supporting materials

* **[one-pager.md](one-pager/)** — single-page fact sheet for journalist quick-read
* **[influencer-outreach.md](influencer-outreach/)** — Tier 1-4 list with verified emails, personalised email template + critic-variant template
* **[launch-copy.md](launch-copy/)** — pre-written copy for HackerNews / Reddit / X / LinkedIn

## Recommended sequence

1. **Adam review** — read and edit all three releases; pick which to use (could send v2 to trade press + v3 to general / advocacy press, or use one as the master)
2. **Fill in `[TO FILL]` placeholders** — media contact, name, email, phone
3. **Tier-1 influencer outreach** — send personalised emails 48h before public launch
4. **Public launch day**:
5. HN post in US morning window (Tue–Thu)
6. Reddit posts 1–2 hours later
7. X/Twitter thread same window
8. LinkedIn post same day or next morning
9. **Day 2–3**: assess traction; trigger paid wire (EIN Presswire ~$100 or PRWeb ~$400) if organic warrants
10. **Week 2**: follow-up to non-responding Tier-1 influencers

## Strategy notes

* **Three releases ≠ three launches.** They're three angles to fit the audience. Pick one as the primary and send tailored variants to specialist outlets.
* **Organic before paid** — HN/Reddit pickup justifies paid wire spend; paid wire without organic underneath is mostly SEO trail
* **Influencer endorsement > press pickup** — a single retweet from Bernet, [Warshak](https://warshak.com/family-bridges/), or Silberg compounds for years
* **Truthful claims only** — every numerical claim in v2 and v3 must be verifiable from the live repo at time of release
* **No paid placements in journalism outlets** — the open-source ethos forbids it and would taint future earned-media credibility

## What's NOT in this kit yet

* Image assets / logos / repo screenshots — to add before launch
* Demo video or screencast — optional but high-leverage
* Translation of releases into FR/DE/NL/ES — defer to post-launch
* Founder bio — to add when Adam confirms attribution preferences
* Knowledge base live at antialienate.com/knowledge (old subdomain 301-redirects here)
