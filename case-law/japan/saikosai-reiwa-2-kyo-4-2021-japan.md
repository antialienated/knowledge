# 最高裁判所第一小法廷 令和3年3月29日決定 令和2年(許)第4号 子の監護に関する処分（面会交流）申立て却下審判に対する抗告審の取消決定に対する許可抗告事件 — 集民第265号113頁

**Neutral citation:** 最一小決令和3・3・29 集民265号113頁 ([Supreme Court of Japan](https://www.courts.go.jp/english/), First Petty Bench, decision of 29 March 2021, Reiwa 2 (kyo) No. 4)  
**Court:** 最高裁判所第一小法廷 (Supreme Court of Japan, First Petty Bench), on permitted appeal (許可抗告) from the Osaka High Court, 令和1(ラ)1140, decision of 29 November 2019  
**Decided:** 2021-03-29  
**Panel:** 池上政幸 (裁判長裁判官) / 小池裕 / 木澤克之 / 山口厚 / 深山卓也 (裁判官) — 裁判官全員一致の意見 (unanimous)  

## Why this case matters

This is the most recent decision published on the Supreme Court of Japan's official case-law database (courts.go.jp 裁判例検索) that rules on a 面会交流 (visitation/contact) application, and — as of 23 September 2026 — no High Court decision on 面会交流 refusal, and no appellate decision at all applying the 2024 Civil Code amendments (Act No. 33 of 2024, joint parental authority, in force 1 April 2026), has yet been published there. The case concerns the maternal grandparents of a young child whose mother had died: they had shared in the child's care and sought a family-court order for contact after the father, who now cared for the child alone, did not provide it. The Osaka High Court had opened the door by analogy to Civil Code article 766; the Supreme Court closed it, holding that only the child's parents may apply for a custody-disposition ruling on contact, that de facto care does not equate a third party to a parent, and that the best-interests clause of article 766(1) is not a source of standing. The decision matters for the alienation debate because it fixes the outer boundary of judicially enforceable contact in Japan at the parent–child relationship: extended-family alienation, however real, has no procedural vehicle under article 766, and any widening is for the legislature. It is a decision about standing, not about the merits of contact refusal, and it does not use the language of 片親疎外 (parental alienation).

## Procedural history

The father married Ｂ, the daughter of the respondents, in November 2012; the child Ａ was born in 2016. The father lived with Ｂ, the child and the respondents at the respondents' home until around January 2017, when he left and the couple separated. From March 2017 the father and Ｂ alternated care of the child every one or two weeks, the respondents assisting Ｂ in her periods of care. Ｂ died in June 2018 and the father has cared for the child since. The respondents (maternal grandparents) applied to the family court, naming the father as respondent, for a ruling fixing visitation and other contact (面会交流) between themselves and the child as a 'disposition concerning the custody of the child' (子の監護に関する処分) under item 3 of Appended Table 2 of the Domestic Relations Case Procedure Act (家事事件手続法別表第2の3の項). The family court (原々審) dismissed the application as unlawful (不適法却下). On appeal, the Osaka High Court (令和1(ラ)1140, decision of 29 November 2019) set aside the family court's ruling and remanded, holding that where a third party other than the parents has in fact cared for the child and has a relationship with the child comparable to that of a parent, and visitation would serve the child's interests, there is scope to allow visitation as a disposition concerning custody by analogous application of Civil Code article 766(1) and (2), so that the application could not be dismissed without examining whether visitation served the child's interests. The father obtained leave to appeal (許可抗告). The First Petty Bench decided on 29 March 2021, quashing the High Court's decision and dismissing the grandparents' appeal against the family court's ruling; the total costs of the appeal proceedings were placed on the respondents. Reported at 集民第265号113頁.

## Holding

(1) Civil Code article 766(1) provides that, on divorce by agreement, matters concerning the custody of the child — including contact between the father or mother and the child — are to be determined by agreement between the parents, and article 766(2) provides that the family court determines those matters where the parents cannot agree; article 766(2) therefore contemplates the family court acting on the application of the parents, who are the parties to the agreement under article 766(1). (2) No provision of the Civil Code or any other statute allows a third party who has in fact cared for the child to apply to the family court to determine such matters, and the fact of care does not equate the third party to a parent for the purposes of such an application. (3) Although the child's interests must be given first priority in determining matters concerning custody (article 766(1), second sentence), that does not provide a basis for allowing a third party to apply. (4) Accordingly a third party other than the parents, even one who has in fact cared for the child, cannot apply to the family court for a ruling fixing contact between that third party and the child as a disposition concerning the custody of the child, whether by direct or analogous application of article 766. (5) Only 'the child's parents and the child's custodian' (家事事件手続法156条4号) may lodge an immediate appeal against a ruling dismissing an application for a disposition concerning custody; the grandparents were neither, so their appeal to the High Court was itself unlawful and is dismissed.

## Verbatim

**理由 4(1), first alinéa** (ja):

> 民法７６６条１項前段は，父母が協議上の離婚をするときは，父又は母と子との面会交流その他の子の監護について必要な事項は，父母が協議をして定めるものとしている。そして，これを受けて同条２項が「前項の協議が調わないとき，又は協議をすることができないときは，家庭裁判所が，同項の事項を定める。」と規定していることからすれば，同条２項は，同条１項の協議の主体である父母の申立てにより，家庭裁判所が子の監護に関する事項を定めることを予定しているものと解される。

*Translation:* The first sentence of Article 766(1) of the Civil Code provides that, when the father and mother divorce by agreement, the matters necessary concerning the custody of the child, including visitation and other contact between the father or mother and the child, are to be determined by agreement between the father and mother. Given that, following on from this, Article 766(2) provides that 'where the agreement under the preceding paragraph cannot be reached, or the parties are unable to consult, the family court shall determine the matters in that paragraph', Article 766(2) is to be construed as contemplating that the family court determines matters concerning the custody of the child upon the application of the father and mother, who are the parties to the agreement under Article 766(1).

<https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-90216.pdf>

**理由 4(1), second alinéa** (ja):

> 他方，民法その他の法令において，事実上子を監護してきた第三者が，家庭裁判所に上記事項を定めるよう申し立てることができる旨を定めた規定はなく，上記の申立てについて，監護の事実をもって上記第三者を父母と同視することもできない。なお，子の利益は，子の監護に関する事項を定めるに当たって最も優先して考慮しなければならないものであるが（民法７６６条１項後段参照），このことは，上記第三者に上記の申立てを許容する根拠となるものではない。

*Translation:* On the other hand, there is no provision in the Civil Code or any other law stating that a third party who has in fact cared for the child may apply to the family court to determine the above matters, and, as regards such an application, the fact of care cannot serve to equate that third party with the father and mother. Moreover, while the interests of the child must be given the highest priority in determining matters concerning the custody of the child (see the second sentence of Article 766(1) of the Civil Code), this does not constitute a basis for permitting such a third party to make such an application.

<https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-90216.pdf>

**理由 4(1), concluding alinéa (裁判要旨)** (ja):

> したがって，父母以外の第三者は，事実上子を監護してきた者であっても，家庭裁判所に対し，子の監護に関する処分として上記第三者と子との面会交流について定める審判を申し立てることはできないと解するのが相当である。

*Translation:* Accordingly, it is appropriate to construe that a third party other than the father and mother, even one who has in fact cared for the child, cannot apply to the family court for a ruling determining visitation and other contact between that third party and the child as a disposition concerning the custody of the child.

<https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-90216.pdf>

**理由 5** (ja):

> そして，子の監護に関する処分の申立てを却下する審判に対して即時抗告をすることができるのは「子の父母及び子の監護者」（家事事件手続法１５６条４号）であるところ，前記２の事実関係によれば，相手方らが本件子の「父母」又は「監護者」のいずれにも当たらないことは明らかである。したがって，原々審判に対する相手方らの抗告は不適法であるから，これを却下することとする。

*Translation:* Further, those who may lodge an immediate appeal against a ruling dismissing an application for a disposition concerning the custody of a child are 'the child's father and mother and the child's custodian' (Article 156, item 4, of the Domestic Relations Case Procedure Act), and on the facts set out in 2 above it is clear that the respondents are neither the 'father and mother' nor the 'custodian' of the child. Accordingly, the respondents' appeal against the ruling of the court of first instance is unlawful and is dismissed.

<https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-90216.pdf>

**主文** (ja):

> 原決定を破棄し，原々審判に対する相手方らの抗告を却下する。抗告手続の総費用は相手方らの負担とする。

*Translation:* The decision of the court below is quashed, and the respondents' appeal against the ruling of the court of first instance is dismissed. The total costs of the appeal proceedings shall be borne by the respondents.

<https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-90216.pdf>

## Outcome

破棄自判 (quashed and decided by the Supreme Court itself): the Osaka High Court's decision of 29 November 2019 (令和1(ラ)1140) is quashed; the grandparents' appeal against the family court's ruling dismissing their visitation application is dismissed as unlawful; total costs of the appeal proceedings on the grandparents. The family court's dismissal of the application therefore stands.

## Comparative jurisprudence

- **最高裁判所第一小法廷 平成25年3月28日決定 平成24(許)41 / 平成24(許)48 / 平成24(許)47 (間接強制 — indirect enforcement of visitation orders)** (JP) — The three Supreme Court decisions of 28 March 2013 on indirect compulsion (間接強制) of 面会交流 orders, listed on courts.go.jp alongside the 2021 decision; they are the apex authorities on enforcing contact against a refusing custodial parent, and together with the 2021 decision they mark the procedural boundaries — who may apply, and how orders are enforced — within which contact-refusal disputes are litigated in Japan.
- **最高裁判所第一小法廷 令和2年4月16日決定 令和1(許)14 (ハーグ条約実施法117条1項の類推適用)** (JP) — Same bench, one year earlier: the Court allowed analogous application of a statute (Hague Implementation Act art. 117(1)) to vary a mediated return agreement in the child's interests. Read together, the two decisions show the Court willing to reason by analogy on remedies but not on standing under Civil Code art. 766.
- **TEN v TEO and another appeal [2020] SGHCF 20** (SG) — [`ten-v-teo-2020-sghcf-20-singapore`](./ten-v-teo-2020-sghcf-20-singapore.md) — Asian common-law counterpart in which contact refusal and alienation were addressed on the merits with expert involvement; contrasts with the Japanese apex court's confinement of the question to statutory standing.
- **H v W [2021] HKCA 733** (HK) — [`h-v-w-2021-hkca-733-hong-kong`](./h-v-w-2021-hkca-733-hong-kong.md) — Decided two months after the Japanese decision; Hong Kong appellate engagement with parental-alienation findings in a relocation context — part of the same 2017–2026 Asian appellate cluster.
- **EWM (Suing as mother and next friend) v VS [2024] KEMC 82 (KLR)** (KE) — [`ewm-v-vs-2024-kemc-82-kenya`](./ewm-v-vs-2024-kemc-82-kenya.md) — Contemporaneous Global-South decision grounding contact in constitutional equal parental responsibility; illustrates the parent-centred conception of contact that the Japanese decision also adopts, there to the exclusion of grandparents.
