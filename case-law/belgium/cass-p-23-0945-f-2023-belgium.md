# Cass. (ch. vac.), 16 août 2023, RG P.23.0945.F, Procureur général près la cour d'appel de Liège c. D. — ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2

**ECLI:** `ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2`  
**Neutral citation:** Cass. 16 août 2023, RG P.23.0945.F  
**Court:** [Cour de cassation](https://www.courdecassation.fr/) de Belgique / Hof van Cassatie van België — chambre des vacations (VAC), on a pourvoi against the arrêt of the cour d'appel de Liège, chambre de la jeunesse, of 5 June 2023  
**Decided:** 2023-08-16  
**Panel:** Erwin Francis (conseiller faisant fonction de président) / Bart Wylleman / Eric de Formanoir (rapporteur) / François Stévenart Meeûs / Sven Mosselmans, conseillers — avocat général Bart De Smet — greffier Tatiana Fenaux  

## Why this case matters

This is the most recent Cour de cassation decision on JUPORTAL (2023–2026) in which a finding of 'aliénation' — here 'aliénation familiale maternelle', from an SOS Enfants psychosocial report — was the trigger for a change of a child's residence. The youth-protection courts of Liège had used the Communauté française's 2018 youth-protection decree to place the child temporarily 'hors de son milieu maternel de vie', i.e. with the father, on the reasoning that this freed her from the loyalty conflict in which the mother placed her. The Cour de cassation, on the prosecution's pourvoi, held that article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, of the decree does not authorise the tribunal de la jeunesse to order a protective placement outside the paternal or maternal living environment: the provision contemplates placement outside the child's living environment as such, and the travaux préparatoires confirm that a parental conflict is not in itself a situation of danger, that constrained intervention in the civil sphere must be strictly limited, and that organising residence and parental authority belongs in principle to the tribunal de la famille. The arrêt matters for parental-alienation practice because it closes off, in French-speaking Belgium, the youth-protection route as a means of transferring residence on an alienation rationale, channelling such disputes back to the family court and its adversarial safeguards. The Court does not assess the validity of the alienation construct itself; the SOS Enfants finding is recorded as a fact of the case and the holding is one of statutory competence.

## Procedural history

The parents of T. N. separated in 2016. In May 2022 the Service de l'Aide à la jeunesse was seised in a context of reciprocal complaints to SOS Enfants, the father alleging maltreatment of the child by the mother and the mother alleging violence by the father towards the child. By judgment of 8 November 2022 the tribunal de la famille fixed the child's principal residence (hébergement principal) with the mother and secondary residence with the father. In December 2022 the SOS Enfants team produced a psychosocial assessment concluding, among other things, to the existence of 'une situation d'aliénation familiale maternelle déjà bien ancrée'. In January 2023 the juge de la jeunesse ordered the child's provisional placement outside the maternal environment; T. N. was placed with her father and, absent fundamental change, the measure was renewed on 10 February 2023, with supervised mother–daughter meetings. The tribunal de la jeunesse, seised by the procureur du Roi under article 51 of the decree of 18 January 2018 (Code de la prévention, de l'Aide à la jeunesse et de la protection de la jeunesse), decided by judgment of 21 March 2023 under article 51, alinéa 1er, 1° and 2°, that the child 'sera hébergée temporairement hors de son milieu maternel de vie en vue de son éducation ou de son traitement' and that the persons concerned would be subject to psychological, social or educational directives or accompaniment, reasoning that placement outside the maternal environment remained necessary to allow the child to escape the loyalty conflict in which her mother placed her. On appeal, the cour d'appel de Liège (chambre de la jeunesse), by arrêt of 5 June 2023, confirmed the judgment 'sous l'émendation que la mesure d'hébergement temporaire de [l'enfant] hors de son milieu de vie maternel en vue de son éducation ou de son traitement est annulée', holding that article 51 does not allow the youth courts to order temporary placement outside the living environment of one particular parent; ruling on the parents' connected civil applications under article 7 of the Law of 8 April 1965, it recalled joint parental authority, domiciled the child with the father, entrusted him with principal residence until the family courts rule, and declined to fix the mother's residence rights while work on the mother–daughter relationship was entrusted to the directeur de la protection de la jeunesse. The Procureur général près la cour d'appel de Liège brought a pourvoi en cassation, limited to the dispositions on the action publique, with a single moyen alleging violation of article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, of the 2018 decree. Conseiller Eric de Formanoir reported; avocat général Bart De Smet concluded. The chambre des vacations rejected the pourvoi on 16 August 2023. The arrêt was entered on JUPORTAL on 22 August 2023.

## Holding

Article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, of the decree of 18 January 2018 of the Communauté française (Code de la prévention, de l'Aide à la jeunesse et de la protection de la jeunesse) — which allows the tribunal de la jeunesse, in exceptional situations, to decide that a child whose health or safety is currently and gravely compromised 'sera hébergé temporairement hors de son milieu de vie en vue de son éducation ou de son traitement' — does not permit the tribunal de la jeunesse to limit that measure to one of the persons composing the child's living environment, for example by ordering placement outside the paternal or the maternal living environment. Neither the text nor the travaux préparatoires disclose such a legislative intention: the substitution of 'milieu de vie' for 'milieu familial de vie' was made to cover children who no longer live within their family, and the exposé des motifs stresses that the tribunal de la famille's competence to organise the children's residence and questions of parental authority is not called into question, that constrained youth-aid intervention in the civil sphere must be strictly limited to situations of danger, that a parental conflict is not in itself a situation of danger, and that only within those conditions could the youth court entrust the child to the other parent. The moyen 'manque en droit'; the pourvoi is rejected.

> En décembre 2022, l'équipe SOS Enfants a réalisé un bilan psychosocial qui conclut, notamment, à l'existence « d'une situation d'aliénation familiale maternelle déjà bien ancrée ». The youth judge then ordered, in January 2023, the child's provisional placement outside the maternal environment. T. N. was placed with her father and, in the absence of any fundamental change, the youth judge renewed that measure on 10 February 2023. The youth judge's orders also provided for supervised mother–daughter meetings.

**II, n° 2** (fr):

> A l'appui de cette décision, le tribunal de la jeunesse a considéré que l'hébergement de l'enfant hors de son milieu de vie maternel restait nécessaire, dès lors qu'il permet à l'enfant d'échapper au conflit de loyauté dans lequel sa mère le place, de se centrer sur elle-même et ses propres besoins, et de s'épanouir.

*Translation:* In support of that decision, the youth court considered that the child's placement outside her maternal living environment remained necessary, since it allows the child to escape the loyalty conflict in which her mother places her, to centre on herself and her own needs, and to flourish.

<https://juportal.be/content/ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2/FR>

**III. La décision de la Cour, n° 6 (first two alinéas)** (fr):

> L'intention prêtée par le moyen au législateur ne ressort pas du texte de l'article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, du décret, puisque cette disposition n'énonce pas que le tribunal de la jeunesse est autorisé à limiter la mesure de l'hébergement temporaire hors du milieu de vie de l'enfant, à l'une ou l'autre des personnes qui composent ce milieu de vie, par exemple en décidant que l'enfant sera hébergé hors de son milieu de vie paternel ou hors de son milieu de vie maternel. Cette intention ne ressort pas davantage des travaux préparatoires du décret.

*Translation:* The intention which the ground of appeal attributes to the legislature does not emerge from the text of article 51, paragraph 1, 2°, of the decree, since that provision does not state that the youth court is authorised to confine the measure of temporary placement outside the child's living environment to one or other of the persons who make up that living environment, for example by deciding that the child will be placed outside his or her paternal living environment or outside his or her maternal living environment. Nor does that intention emerge from the decree's preparatory works.

<https://juportal.be/content/ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2/FR>

**III, n° 6 (quoting Doc. parl., session 2016-2017, 467, n° 1, p. 47)** (fr):

> « il ne s'agit pas de remettre en cause la compétence du tribunal de la famille pour organiser l'hébergement des enfants et pour les questions liées à l'autorité parentale », que, « comme le rappellent le Conseil communautaire et le ministère public dans leurs avis, l'ingérence de l'aide contrainte dans le domaine civil doit être strictement limitée aux situations de danger, un conflit parental ne constituant pas en soi une situation de danger au sens du décret », que, « de plus, cette ingérence doit être fondée sur le fait que l'absence de décision civile ou le caractère inapplicable de la décision civile contribue à mettre l'enfant en danger et être limitée dans le temps », et que « ce n'est donc que dans le respect de ces conditions que le tribunal de la jeunesse pourrait décider, sur la base de l'article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, de confier l'enfant à l'autre parent »

*Translation:* 'there is no question of calling into doubt the family court's competence to organise the children's residence and to deal with questions relating to parental authority'; 'as the Community Council and the public prosecutor recall in their opinions, the interference of compulsory assistance in the civil sphere must be strictly limited to situations of danger, a parental conflict not constituting in itself a situation of danger within the meaning of the decree'; 'moreover, that interference must be founded on the fact that the absence of a civil decision, or the unenforceable character of the civil decision, contributes to placing the child in danger, and must be limited in time'; and 'it is therefore only in compliance with those conditions that the youth court could decide, on the basis of article 51, paragraph 1, 2°, to entrust the child to the other parent'.

<https://juportal.be/content/ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2/FR>

**III, n° 6 (last alinéa)** (fr):

> Contrairement à ce que le moyen soutient, l'extrait précité des travaux préparatoires ne permet pas de considérer que le législateur ait eu l'intention, alors que l'article 51, alinéa 1er, 2°, du décret ne le prévoit pas, d'autoriser le tribunal de la jeunesse à prononcer une mesure protectionnelle d'hébergement temporaire en dehors du milieu de vie paternel ou maternel de l'enfant. Le moyen manque en droit.

*Translation:* Contrary to what the ground of appeal maintains, the above-cited extract from the preparatory works does not allow it to be considered that the legislature intended — when article 51, paragraph 1, 2°, of the decree does not so provide — to authorise the youth court to order a protective measure of temporary placement outside the child's paternal or maternal living environment. The ground fails in law.

<https://juportal.be/content/ECLI:BE:CASS:2023:ARR.20230816.VAC.2/FR>

## Outcome

Pourvoi rejeté; frais (42,90 EUR) à charge de l'État. The arrêt of the cour d'appel de Liège (chambre de la jeunesse) of 5 June 2023 stands: the protective measure of temporary placement of the child outside her maternal living environment is annulled; on the connected civil claims, joint parental authority is recalled, the child is domiciled with the father, who holds principal residence until the family courts rule in the same sense or otherwise, and the mother's residence rights are left to the ongoing work entrusted to the directeur de la protection de la jeunesse.

## Comparative jurisprudence

- **Cass. 28 avril 2010, RG P.10.0409.F, ECLI:BE:CASS:2010:ARR.20100428.2, Pas. 2010, n° 292** (BE) — Precedent cited in the JUPORTAL fiche of the 2023 arrêt on the measure of temporary placement outside the child's 'milieu familial de vie' under article 38, § 3, 2°, of the abrogated decree of 4 March 1991; the 2023 arrêt carries the same restrictive reading into the 2018 Code.
- **Cass. 6 mars 2025, RG C.24.0157.F, M. c. L., ECLI:BE:CASS:2025:ARR.20250306.1F.5** (BE) — The most recent Cour de cassation arrêt located on JUPORTAL concerning non-execution of family-court residence (hébergement) decisions under article 387ter of the ancien [Code civil](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGITEXT000006070721/) — the civil-law remedy route for contact frustration, as opposed to the youth-protection route closed by the 2023 arrêt. Only its metadata (rôle, ECLI, date, chamber, parties' initials) and the presence of 'hébergement' and '387ter' in its text were verified for this entry; its reasoning was not reviewed and it does not appear among JUPORTAL results for the phrase 'aliénation parentale'.
- **Hof van beroep Gent, 24 februari 2026, 2025/FA/628, ECLI:BE:HBGNT:2026:ARR.20260224.1** (BE) — Most recent Belgian appellate decision on JUPORTAL using the Dutch term 'oudervervreemding' (2026): in a [Hague Convention 1980](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=24) return case, the Gent court found it not proven that the child's objection to return resulted from 'externe conditionering en van oudervervreemding of emotionele of andere beïnvloeding' and gave weight to the child's views. Belgian courts thus treat the construct as a factual allegation to be proven, not as a presumption.
- **[Cassazione](https://www.cortedicassazione.it/), Sez. I Civile, ordinanza n. 9691 del 24 marzo 2022** (IT) — [`cassazione-9691-2022-italy`](./cassazione-9691-2022-italy.md) — Italian apex critique of alienation-based removals of a child from the resident mother; the Belgian 2023 arrêt reaches a comparable practical outcome (annulment of the residence transfer) but on grounds of statutory competence rather than scientific validity.
- **[BVerfG](https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/), Beschluss 1 BvR 1076/23 vom 17.11.2023** (DE) — [`bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023`](./bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023.md) — Decided three months after the Belgian arrêt; German constitutional review of an alienation-based custody decision on fundamental-rights grounds — the doctrinal counterpoint to Belgium's competence-based approach within the EU6 group.
- **Hoge Raad 15 februari 2005, ECLI:NL:HR:2005:AR8250** (NL) — [`hoge-raad-2005-ar8250-netherlands`](./hoge-raad-2005-ar8250-netherlands.md) — Neighbouring Dutch-language apex treatment of contact frustration through the criminal law; together with the Belgian arrêt illustrates how the Benelux apex courts locate alienation-type disputes in specific statutory channels rather than in a clinical construct.
