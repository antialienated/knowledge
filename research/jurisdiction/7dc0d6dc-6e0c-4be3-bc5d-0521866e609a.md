# Saxony / Freistaat Sachsen (Free State of Saxony)

Saxony (German: Freistaat Sachsen 'Free State of Saxony') is an Eastern German civil-law constituent Land of the Federal Republic of Germany — structurally distinctive globally as one of three German Länder operating formal 'Free State' (Freistaat) designation (Bavaria + Saxony + Thuringia — historical Free State designation deriving from post-WWI Weimar Republic 1918 abolition of constitutional monarchy framework + post-1990 German Reunification re-adoption, distinct from rest-of-German-Länder which operate Land designation), as the only modern German Land formally re-established via the German Reunification framework 3 October 1990 with continuous pre-WWII Free State of Saxony 1918-1933 + GDR Land Saxony 1949-1952 + GDR-Bezirk-system 1952-1990 + post-1990 Free State of Saxony 1990+ identity continuous framework (Saxony was abolished as Land by GDR Bezirke reorganisation 25 July 1952 + restored as Land 14 October 1990 — only modern German Land with continuous Free State of Saxony historical-cultural-political identity framework spanning Weimar + GDR + reunified Germany), as the only modern German Land with formal Sorbian-language-recognition framework (Upper Sorbian Hornjoserbsce Slavic Western Slavic language formally recognised in Cantonal Constitution of Saxony 1992 Article 6 + Sorbian Areas Act Sächsisches Sorbengesetz 1999 — Sorbian as recognised minority language of Saxony, distinct from rest-of-German-Länder which operate German-language-only frameworks), and as the central jurisdiction of the most devastating modern Allied firebombing campaign on German soil (Dresden firebombing 13-15 February 1945 — Allied firebombing campaign with ~25,000 civilian deaths, structurally distinctive globally as pivotal late-WWII Allied bombing controversy framework). Saxony historically operated under Wettin Dynasty 928-1918 + Kingdom of Saxony 1806-1918 + Free State of Saxony 1918-1933 + Nazi German Gau-system 1933-1945 + post-1945 Soviet Zone of Occupation 1945-1949 + German Democratic Republic Land 1949-1952 + GDR Bezirke 1952-1990 + Free State of Saxony 1990+. Saxony comprises ~18,449 km² with a population of ~4.0 million. Family-law framework operates under the federal German civil-law framework (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch BGB 1896 Family Law portion + German Family-Law Reform Act 2024) applied uniformly across German Länder. The Saxon Oberlandesgericht is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Federal Court of Justice of Germany. Saxony is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Germany is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 27 September 1990 effective 1 December 1990) — Saxony is governed by German Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
