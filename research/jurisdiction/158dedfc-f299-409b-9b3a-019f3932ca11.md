# New Zealand (Aotearoa New Zealand)

New Zealand is a common-law jurisdiction whose Family Court (Te Kōti Whānau) operates under the Care of Children Act 2004 (CoCA) and the Family Court Act 1980. The CoCA replaced the Guardianship Act 1968 establishing the modern parenting-order framework with welfare-and-best-interests-of-the-child paramount standard (s 4). The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court of New Zealand and Court of Appeal have addressed contact-refusal cases applying CoCA welfare-and-best-interests standard. Distinctive features include Māori tikanga and Treaty of Waitangi (Te Tiriti o Waitangi) considerations under CoCA s 5(f). The Family Violence Act 2018 (replacing Domestic Violence Act 1995) reformed DV-protection framework — invoked in DV-PA-allegation intersection cases. New Zealand's Family Court 2014 reforms (under Family Court Reform Act 2013) restructured procedural framework toward Family Dispute Resolution.
