# Brazil (República Federativa do Brasil)

Brazil is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (Varas de Família) operate under the Código Civil arts. 1.630 to 1.638 (poder familiar), Estatuto da Criança e do Adolescente (ECA, Lei n° 8.069/1990), and the Lei n° 12.318/2010 da Alienação Parental — the world's first statutory codification of 'alienação parental' as a juridical concept. The Superior Tribunal de Justiça (STJ) has applied the statute in cases such as REsp 1.629.994/RJ (2017) and REsp 1.722.030/PR (2019) while the Conselho Federal de Psicologia (CFP) Resolução CFP n° 13/2022 has expressed institutional caution against PAS-syndromic framing — placing Brazil in a structurally distinctive position with statutory recognition but professional-regulator critique.
