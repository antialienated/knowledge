# IGAD Agreement 1996 (Intergovernmental Authority on Development)

The Agreement Establishing the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD Agreement) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-IGAD-treaty framework adopted by the 7 founding Horn-of-Africa states in Nairobi — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Horn-of-Africa regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal Horn-of-Africa framework (IGAD Agreement signed 21 March 1996 in Nairobi Kenya + effective 25 November 1996 + IGADD Intergovernmental Authority on Drought and Development predecessor founded 16 January 1986 in Djibouti — formal foundational IGAD treaty framework establishing comprehensive IGAD organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-African-treaty frameworks which operate on different geographic scopes, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Horn-of-Africa regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Horn-of-Africa framework + formed in 1986 as IGADD drought-response framework + transformed to IGAD development framework 1996 + only modern African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal South-Sudan-mediation framework via IGAD-Plus framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Eritrea-membership-suspended-self-suspension framework since April 2007), as the central jurisdiction of the formal IGAD South Sudan Mediation framework (IGAD-led South Sudan Civil War mediation framework + Agreement on the Resolution of the Conflict in the Republic of South Sudan ARCSS signed 17 August 2015 in Addis Ababa Ethiopia + Revitalized Agreement on the Resolution of the Conflict in the Republic of South Sudan R-ARCSS signed 12 September 2018 in Addis Ababa Ethiopia — formal foundational IGAD South-Sudan-mediation framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional-state-level mediation framework with operational mediation framework via IGAD-Plus framework including AU + UN + US + UK + Norway + China framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal IGAD Climate Prediction and Applications Centre ICPAC framework (IGAD Climate Prediction and Applications Centre ICPAC established 1989 in Nairobi Kenya providing formal Horn-of-Africa climate-prediction framework — formal foundational Horn-of-Africa climate-prediction framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Horn-of-Africa regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional climate-prediction framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal IGAD CEWARN Conflict Early Warning and Response Mechanism framework (IGAD Conflict Early Warning and Response Mechanism CEWARN established 2002 providing formal Horn-of-Africa conflict-early-warning framework — formal foundational Horn-of-Africa conflict-early-warning framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Horn-of-Africa regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional conflict-early-warning framework). The IGAD Agreement is binding upon 8 IGAD member states as of 2026 (Djibouti + Eritrea + Ethiopia + Kenya + Somalia + South Sudan + Sudan + Uganda) with Eritrea-self-suspended-since-April-2007 framework — comprehensive Horn-of-Africa regional coverage framework. The Agreement is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal IGAD CEWARN + IGAD Migration Policy frameworks operate substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Horn-of-Africa cross-border-children's-rights framework + IGAD Migration Action Plan framework. The IGAD Assembly of Heads of State and Government + IGAD Council of Ministers + IGAD Secretariat + IGAD CEWARN + IGAD ICPAC are the formal IGAD bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
