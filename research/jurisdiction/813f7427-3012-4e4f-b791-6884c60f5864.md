# Sápmi (Sámi homeland / Sámiid Ruoktu)

Sápmi (Northern Sámi: Sápmi 'Sámi homeland' / also Sámiid Ruoktu) is a transnational Arctic civil-law Indigenous homeland territory spanning four sovereign states (Norway + Sweden + Finland + Russia — distinct from rest-of-modern-indigenous-territorial-frameworks operating within single-state framework) — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern indigenous homeland with formal multi-state parliamentary representation (three formally-recognised Sámi parliaments: Sámediggi Finland 1973 + Sámediggi Norway 1989 + Sametinget Sweden 1993 — only modern indigenous people globally with three formally-recognised national-level indigenous parliaments under three different sovereign-state frameworks, with non-recognition framework in Russian Sápmi sub-territory due to Russian Federation 2007 Indigenous Federal-Targeted-Programme deviation from Nordic framework), as the only modern transnational territory operating formal Sámi-language (Saami language family) official-administrative framework across multiple states (Northern Sámi + Inari Sámi + Skolt Sámi + Lule Sámi + Pite Sámi + Southern Sámi + Ume Sámi + Kildin Sámi + Akkala Sámi + Ter Sámi 10-language Sámi-language family framework — formal recognition of Northern Sámi as official language of Sámi areas under Norwegian Sámi Language Act 1990 + Finnish Sámi Language Act 2003 + Swedish Minority Languages Act 2009), as the only modern Arctic-circle-spanning indigenous territory with continuous formal historical framework predating modern sovereign state formation frameworks (Sámi pre-historic reindeer-herding-fishing-hunting framework continuous since ~10,000 BCE Younger Stone Age framework — distinct from rest-of-modern-indigenous-territorial-frameworks which operate post-colonial frameworks), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Sámi land-rights-recognition framework via the Norwegian Finnmark Act 2005 (Norwegian Finnmark Act of 17 June 2005 — formal recognition of Sámi land-rights over Finnmark county, only modern Nordic land-rights-recognition framework). The Sápmi territory historically operated under pre-historic Sámi framework ~10,000 BCE + Norse Viking expansion 9th-11th centuries + Sámi taxation by Norwegian + Swedish + Russian Crown frameworks 13th-17th centuries + Strömstad Treaty 1751 framework establishing Lapp Codicil (Sámi cross-border reindeer-herding-rights framework — first international treaty on indigenous rights globally) + 19th-century Norwegianisation + Swedification + Finnicisation + Russification assimilation policies + 20th-century Sámi-indigenous-rights-movement framework + 1973 Finland Sámi Delegation + 1989 Norway Sámediggi + 1993 Sweden Sametinget + 2005 Norway Finnmark Act + 2017 Finland Truth and Reconciliation Commission. Sápmi comprises ~390,000 km² with a population of ~80,000-100,000 Sámi. Family-law framework operates under each respective sovereign-state framework (Norway + Sweden + Finland + Russia) applied uniformly across Sámi areas within each state. The Norway Supreme Court (Norway Sápmi) + Sweden Supreme Court (Sweden Sápmi) + Finland Supreme Court (Finland Sápmi) + Russia Supreme Court (Russia Sápmi) are the apex courts. Sápmi is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
