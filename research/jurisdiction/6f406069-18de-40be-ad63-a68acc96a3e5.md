# Cambodia (Kingdom of Cambodia / ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា)

Cambodia is a Southeast Asian civil-law constitutional monarchy whose family-law framework operates under the Civil Code 2007 (effective 2011) Book V (Family) and the Law on Marriage and Family 1989. Parental authority and child custody are governed by Civil Code arts. 1034-1054. The Supreme Court of Cambodia (តុលាការកំពូល) is the apex court for civil and criminal matters; the Constitutional Council (ក្រុមប្រឹក្សាធម្មនុញ្ញ) operates separate constitutional review. Family-law matters are heard at first instance in the Court of First Instance under specialised family-procedure rules. Psychology profession is regulated through the Ministry of Health framework; no unified federal-statutory psychology regulator exists. Cambodia is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label; courts operate substantively under the welfare-of-the-child standard codified in Civil Code art. 1037.
