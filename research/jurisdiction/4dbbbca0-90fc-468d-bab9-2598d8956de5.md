# Bir Tawil (بير طويل / Tall Well)

Bir Tawil (Arabic: بير طويل Bīr Ṭawīl 'Tall Well') is a North African mixed unclaimed sui generis territory between Egypt and Sudan — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern terra nullius territory on Earth not claimed by any formal sovereign state (~2,060 km² territory between Egypt and Sudan — not formally claimed by Egypt + not formally claimed by Sudan + not formally claimed by any other state, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern uninhabited unclaimed sovereign-state-level terra nullius framework, distinct from Antarctica + International-Waters frameworks operating under multilateral treaty regimes), as the central jurisdiction of the most distinctive modern Egypt-Sudan border-dispute framework (Egypt-Sudan border 1899 Anglo-Egyptian Sudan Condominium framework vs. 1902 Anglo-Egyptian administrative-line framework — formal Egypt-Sudan border-line discrepancy framework where mutual claim to Hala'ib Triangle ~20,580 km² disputed territory by both states creates structural inconsistency requiring neither state to claim Bir Tawil to maintain mutual Hala'ib Triangle claim consistency), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern micro-nationalist-claim-frameworks (multiple unrecognised micro-nationalist claims since 2014 including 'Kingdom of North Sudan' established 2014 by US individual + 'Kingdom of the Two Niles' + 'Yellow Empire' + 'Free Republic of Verdis' + others — distinct from rest-of-modern-sovereign-state framework where micro-nationalist claims operate on islands or other peripheral territories), and as one of only two modern formal terra nullius territories globally (alongside parts of Antarctica under Antarctic Treaty System 1959 — distinct multilateral framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-territorial framework which operates formal sovereign-state claims). Bir Tawil historically operated under Anglo-Egyptian Sudan framework 1899-1956 + Egypt-Sudan border-dispute framework 1956+ + no formal sovereign-state-level claim 1899-present 126+ years. Bir Tawil comprises ~2,060 km² with a population of 0 (uninhabited). Family-law framework operates under non-applicability (no formal sovereign state claim — no formal family-law framework). The Apex court is non-applicable (no formal sovereign state framework). Bir Tawil is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
