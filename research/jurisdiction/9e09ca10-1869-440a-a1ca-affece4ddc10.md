# Republic of Tatarstan (Республика Татарстан / Татарстан Җөмһүрияте)

The Republic of Tatarstan (Russian: Республика Татарстан Respublika Tatarstan / Tatar: Татарстан Җөмһүрияте Tatarstan Cömhüriyäte) is a Volga Russian civil-law constituent republic of the Russian Federation — structurally distinctive globally as the second-largest Russian sub-national republic by population (~3.9 million — second-largest of all 21 Russian republics after Bashkortostan ~4.0 million), as the only Russian sub-national republic to refuse to sign the original Russian Federation Treaty 1992 (Russian Federation Treaty signed 31 March 1992 — Tatarstan + Chechnya were only two Russian republics refusing initial signature, with Tatarstan subsequently signing bilateral Russian-Tatarstan Power-Sharing Treaty 15 February 1994 as substitute framework — distinct from rest-of-Russian-republics framework), as the only Russian sub-national republic with formal post-Soviet bilateral power-sharing treaty framework (Russian-Tatarstan Power-Sharing Treaty 1994 effective 15 February 1994 + Russian-Tatarstan Power-Sharing Treaty 2007 effective 26 June 2007 - 2017 expired — only modern Russian sub-national republic with formal bilateral power-sharing treaty framework, expired 24 July 2017 with no replacement framework as of 2026), and as the central jurisdiction of historical Kazan Khanate 1438-1552 framework (Kazan Khanate established 1438 by Ulugh Muhammad as Golden Horde successor state + Kazan Khanate conquest by Ivan IV the Terrible 2 October 1552 — only modern Russian sub-national republic with continuous Golden-Horde-successor-state historical-cultural-political-Islamic framework). Tatarstan historically operated under Volga Bulgaria 7th-13th centuries + Golden Horde 1242-1438 + Kazan Khanate 1438-1552 + Russian Empire 1552-1917 + Soviet Tatar ASSR 1920-1990 + Republic of Tatarstan 1990+ (with formal sovereignty declaration 30 August 1990). Tatarstan comprises ~67,847 km² with a population of ~3.9 million (~53% Tatar + ~39% Russian + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Russian civil-law framework (Russian Family Code 1995 + Russian Civil Code 1994) applied uniformly across Russian sub-national territories. The Supreme Court of Tatarstan is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation. Tatarstan is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Russia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 28 July 2011 effective 1 October 2011) — Tatarstan is governed by Russian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
