# OIC Charter 1972 / Revised 2008 (Organisation of Islamic Cooperation)

The Charter of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC Charter) is a global supranational meta-framework foundational-OIC-treaty framework adopted by the founding Muslim-majority states in Jeddah — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Muslim-world supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal Muslim-majority-states framework + second-largest international organisation by membership globally after UN framework (OIC Charter original signed 4 March 1972 in Jeddah Saudi Arabia + OIC Revised Charter signed 14 March 2008 in Dakar Senegal + effective 14 March 2008 — formal foundational OIC treaty framework establishing comprehensive OIC organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate without religious specificity, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Muslim-world supranational binding-treaty framework + second-largest international organisation by membership globally after UN with 57 member states framework + founded 25 September 1969 in Rabat Morocco at Rabat Summit in response to Al-Aqsa Mosque arson 21 August 1969 framework + only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam framework + only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Independent Permanent Human Rights Commission IPHRC framework via 2008 Revised Charter + only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Islamic Solidarity Fund framework + only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Islamophobia framework adopted 15 March 2022 UN International Day to Combat Islamophobia framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OIC Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam framework (Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam CDHRI adopted 5 August 1990 in Cairo Egypt at 19th Islamic Conference of Foreign Ministers + replaced by OIC Declaration on Human Rights 28 November 2020 in Niamey Niger — formal foundational OIC human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam framework + formal Islamic-Sharia-based human-rights framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OIC Independent Permanent Human Rights Commission IPHRC framework (OIC Independent Permanent Human Rights Commission IPHRC established 30 June 2011 at OIC Council of Foreign Ministers Astana Kazakhstan + first meeting 24 February 2012 in Jeddah Saudi Arabia providing formal OIC human-rights monitoring framework — formal foundational OIC human-rights monitoring framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Islamic-Sharia-based human-rights monitoring framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal OIC International Islamic Court of Justice framework (Statute of the International Islamic Court of Justice adopted 29 January 1987 at 5th Islamic Summit Conference Kuwait + not yet operational due to insufficient ratifications as of 2026 — formal foundational OIC-Islamic-judicial framework adopted but not operational, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Islamic-Court-statute-adopted-but-not-operational framework). The OIC Charter is binding upon 57 OIC member states as of 2026 + 5 OIC observer states + 5 OIC observer institutions — comprehensive Muslim-majority-states coverage framework + Syria suspended-since-August-2012 + Egypt suspended-from-Arab-League-and-OIC-2013-2015 framework. The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal OIC Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Islamic-Sharia-based family-law framework + Muslim-majority-states cross-border-children's-rights framework.
