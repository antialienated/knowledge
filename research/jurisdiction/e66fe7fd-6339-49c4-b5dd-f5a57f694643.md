# WHO Constitution 1946 (World Health Organization)

The Constitution of the World Health Organization (WHO Constitution) is a global supranational meta-framework health-rights binding-treaty instrument adopted by the International Health Conference in New York — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal multilateral binding-treaty framework operating specifically on health-rights framework (WHO Constitution adopted 22 July 1946 in New York + effective 7 April 1948 — formal universal multilateral binding-treaty framework establishing comprehensive health-rights framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate on broader general-rights frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal health-as-fundamental-human-right framework via Preamble framework 'The enjoyment of the highest attainable standard of health is one of the fundamental rights of every human being without distinction of race, religion, political belief, economic or social condition' + 7 April World Health Day commemoration framework + first UN specialized agency operating exclusively on health framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal WHO Preamble Health-as-Fundamental-Right framework (WHO Constitution Preamble framework providing 'Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity' + 'The enjoyment of the highest attainable standard of health is one of the fundamental rights of every human being' — formal foundational health-as-fundamental-right framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal health-as-fundamental-right framework with formal comprehensive health-definition framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal International Health Regulations IHR 2005 framework (International Health Regulations IHR 2005 adopted 23 May 2005 by 58th World Health Assembly Resolution WHA58.3 + effective 15 June 2007 providing formal global health-security framework via Public Health Emergency of International Concern PHEIC framework — formal foundational global health-security framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal global health-security PHEIC framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal WHO Pandemic Agreement 2025 framework (WHO Pandemic Agreement adopted 20 May 2025 by 78th World Health Assembly providing formal pandemic prevention + preparedness + response framework — formal foundational pandemic framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal pandemic-specific framework). The WHO Constitution is binding upon 194 WHO member states + 2 associate members as of 2026 — comprehensive universal coverage framework. The Constitution is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal WHO ICD-11 framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal mental-health-condition-classification framework where ICD-11 index entry 'parental alienation' was added 2018 + removed 2020 + child-maltreatment QE52 framework with mental-health-care related codes. The World Health Assembly + WHO Executive Board + WHO Director-General + WHO Regional Offices are the formal WHO bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies. The WHO Constitution is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though WHO ICD-11 classification framework operates active framework on 'parental alienation' classification framework.
