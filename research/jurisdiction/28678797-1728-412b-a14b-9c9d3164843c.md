# Japan (日本国 / Nihon-koku)

Japan is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (家庭裁判所 Katei Saibansho) operate under the Minpō (民法 Civil Code) arts. 766-839 and the Personal Status Litigation Act. Japan operated a sole-custody (単独親権 tandoku shinken) framework as default until the Civil Code Partial Amendment 2024 (Reiwa 6) introduced joint custody (共同親権 kyōdō shinken) effective 2026. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court (Saikō Saibansho) has not endorsed PAS as a syndrome but addresses contact-refusal (面会交流拒否 menkai-kōryū-kyohi) within the welfare-of-the-child (子の福祉 ko-no-fukushi) standard. Japan is internationally distinctive for its 1980 Hague Abduction Convention accession (delayed until 2014) and persistent international-abduction concern.
