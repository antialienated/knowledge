# Saba (The Unspoiled Queen)

Saba (popularly 'The Unspoiled Queen' in English-language framework) is a Caribbean Leeward Islands civil-law Dutch special municipality (bijzondere gemeente) within the Caribbean Netherlands (BES Islands: Bonaire + Sint Eustatius + Saba) — structurally distinctive globally as the smallest formally-administered Dutch sub-national jurisdiction by area (~13 km² — smallest special-municipality framework in the Kingdom of the Netherlands, smaller than rest-of-Dutch-Caribbean special-municipalities Bonaire ~294 km² + Sint Eustatius ~21 km²), as the only Dutch sub-national jurisdiction operating on an extinct stratovolcano (Mount Scenery 877m — highest point in the entire Kingdom of the Netherlands including European Netherlands, structurally distinctive as the only modern Dutch sub-national jurisdiction with formal volcanic-topographic framework, Saba is essentially a single volcanic island with no flat areas), as one of the most distinctive modern formal-equality LGBT-rights frameworks in the Caribbean (Saba conducted the first same-sex marriage in the entire Caribbean region 9 October 2012 — first legally-recognised same-sex marriage in the Caribbean under Dutch Federal same-sex marriage framework 2001, distinct from rest-of-Caribbean which continued sole-different-sex marriage frameworks), and as a distinctive Atlantic-historical jurisdiction (Saba operated continuous English-language framework in formal-administrative + community-cultural usage despite Dutch sovereignty — distinct from rest-of-Dutch-Caribbean frameworks where Papiamento + Dutch dominate, due to historical English/Scottish/Irish settlement). Saba operated under Dutch West India Company framework 1640s onwards + occasional French + British alternations 17th-19th centuries, operated as part of Netherlands Antilles 1954-2010, and was integrated as Dutch special municipality 10 October 2010 following Netherlands Antilles dissolution framework. Saba comprises ~13 km² with a population of ~1,900. Family-law framework operates under the federal Dutch civil-law framework (Dutch Civil Code 1992 Book 1 Family Law portion) applied uniformly across Dutch sub-national jurisdictions. The Court of First Instance (Saba Branch) is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Common Court of Justice of Aruba + Curaçao + Sint Maarten + BES + Supreme Court of the Netherlands. Saba is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. The Netherlands is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 12 June 1990 effective 1 September 1990) — Saba is governed by Dutch Hague-Convention-1980 framework via Caribbean Netherlands status.
