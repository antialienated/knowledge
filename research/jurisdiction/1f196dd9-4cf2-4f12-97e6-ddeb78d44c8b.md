# ASEAN Charter 2007 (Charter of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations)

The Charter of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-ASEAN-treaty framework adopted by the 10 ASEAN member states in Singapore — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Southeast Asia regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal ASEAN-Way consensus + non-interference framework (ASEAN Charter signed 20 November 2007 in Singapore + effective 15 December 2008 + ASEAN founded 8 August 1967 in Bangkok via Bangkok Declaration — formal foundational ASEAN treaty framework granting legal-personality framework to ASEAN organisation framework + 40-year delayed treaty-form codification framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on formal binding-treaty framework from outset, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal ASEAN-Way consensus + non-interference framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal 40-year delayed treaty-form codification framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal ASEAN-Way Consensus + Non-Interference framework (ASEAN Charter Article 2 framework providing 'respect for the independence, sovereignty, equality, territorial integrity and national identity of all ASEAN Member States' + 'non-interference in the internal affairs of ASEAN Member States' + 'consultation and consensus' — formal foundational ASEAN-Way framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal ASEAN-Way consensus + non-interference framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal AICHR ASEAN Intergovernmental Commission on Human Rights framework (AICHR established 23 October 2009 by ASEAN Foreign Ministers Meeting + ASEAN Human Rights Declaration adopted 18 November 2012 in Phnom Penh Cambodia — formal foundational ASEAN-regional-human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Southeast Asia regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional human-rights framework + only modern regional human-rights framework operating without formal individual-petition or court framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal ACWC ASEAN Commission on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children framework (ACWC established 7 April 2010 by ASEAN Foreign Ministers Meeting — formal foundational ASEAN-regional-women-children-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Southeast Asia regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional women + children rights framework with PA-intersection framework). The ASEAN Charter is binding upon 10 ASEAN member states + 1 observer state Timor-Leste pending accession 2025-2026 as of 2026 — comprehensive Southeast Asia regional coverage framework. The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal ACWC framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Southeast-Asia-regional women + children rights framework. The ASEAN Summit + ASEAN Coordinating Council + ASEAN Community Councils + AICHR + ACWC + ASEAN Secretariat are the formal ASEAN bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
