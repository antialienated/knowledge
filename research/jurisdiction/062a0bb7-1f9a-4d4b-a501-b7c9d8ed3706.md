# Colombia (República de Colombia)

Colombia is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (Juzgados de Familia + Comisarías de Familia) operate under the Código Civil arts. 250-323 (patria potestad / responsabilidad parental), the Código de la Infancia y la Adolescencia (CIA, Ley 1098 of 2006), and the Constitución Política 1991. The Constitution art. 44 establishes constitutionally-grounded child-rights framework declaring children's rights as fundamental and prevailing. The statutory framework is silent on the 'alienación parental' label as a diagnostic category but multiple legislative proposals codifying alienación parental have been debated. The Corte Constitucional has issued substantive jurisprudence on interés superior del niño (constitutional rank under bloque de constitucionalidad incorporating UN CRC). The Instituto Colombiano de Bienestar Familiar (ICBF) institutionally informs child-protection framework. Multiple Corte Constitucional sentences (T-557/2011, T-955/2013) address contact-refusal cases applying constitutional child-rights framework.
