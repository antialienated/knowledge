# Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (दादरा और नगर हवेली और दमन और दीव)

Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (दादरा और नगर हवेली और दमन और दीव in Hindi) is a Western Indian mixed common-law/Portuguese-civil-law-remnant Indian Union Territory comprising the merged Dadra and Nagar Haveli (interior tribal-area enclave bordering Maharashtra and Gujarat) + Daman (coastal town bordering Gujarat) + Diu (small coastal island bordering Gujarat) — structurally distinctive globally as the only Indian Union Territory formed via the formal merger of two previously distinct Indian Union Territories (Dadra and Nagar Haveli + Daman and Diu merged into single Union Territory effective 26 January 2020 under the Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union Territories) Act 2019), as the only Indian Union Territory operating Portuguese civil-law remnant framework (Portuguese Codigo Civil 1867 framework applicable to Goa-Dadra-Daman-Diu Portuguese India 1510-1961 — preserved post-Operation Vijay 1961 annexation by India under the Goa, Daman and Diu Administration Act 1962 and Indian Government framework), as the central jurisdiction of three distinct Portuguese India enclaves with three different annexation histories (Dadra and Nagar Haveli: nationalist takeover 1954 + integration 1961; Daman and Diu: Operation Vijay 1961 India-Portugal armed action), and as the only Indian Union Territory with all-Portuguese-India 1510-1961 colonial framework (distinct from Puducherry's French India framework 1674-1962). The Union Territory comprises ~603 km² with a population of ~590,000. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Indian common-law + Portuguese civil-law-remnant framework with Codigo Civil 1867 applicability via Indian post-1961 legal-succession framework. The Bombay High Court (Goa/Daman/Diu Permanent Bench) is the apex domestic appellate court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of India. The Union Territory is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. India is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is governed by the Indian non-ratification status.
