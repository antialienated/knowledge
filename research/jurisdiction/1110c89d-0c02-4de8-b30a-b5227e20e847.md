# France (République française)

France is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (juge aux affaires familiales, JAF) operate under the Code civil arts. 371-1 to 373-2-13 (autorité parentale) and the Code de procédure civile. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label as a diagnostic category; the Cour de cassation has not endorsed PAS as a syndrome but applies l'intérêt supérieur de l'enfant (best-interests-of-the-child standard) to contact-refusal cases. The Haute Autorité de Santé (HAS) has not validated 'aliénation parentale' as a clinical diagnosis. France sits in the institutional-caution camp, with the Conseil supérieur du travail social and Défenseure des droits expressing critique of PA-labelled deflection of domestic-violence allegations.
