# Cabinda Province (Cabinda / Cabinda Enclave)

Cabinda Province (Portuguese: Cabinda — also known as Cabinda Enclave) is a Central African civil-law constituent province of the Republic of Angola — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Angolan sub-national territory not contiguous with main Angola (~7,283 km² Angolan exclave separated from main Angola by the Republic of the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo ~60 km wide DRC Bas-Congo corridor framework — formal Angolan exclave framework, distinct from rest-of-Angolan-sub-national-territorial framework), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most economically distinctive modern African sub-national oil-production frameworks (Cabinda contains ~60-65% of Angolan oil production + ~25-30% of Sub-Saharan African oil production at peak ~700,000 barrels/day output framework — structurally distinctive globally as the most economically-substantial sub-national oil-production framework on the African continent), as the central jurisdiction of one of the longest-running modern African separatist insurgency frameworks (Front for the Liberation of the Enclave of Cabinda FLEC 1963-2026 63+ years — continuous Cabinda separatist insurgency framework 1963-present, distinct framework from FNLA + MPLA + UNITA Angolan-mainland frameworks during Angolan War of Independence 1961-1975 + Angolan Civil War 1975-2002, with 1 August 2006 Memorandum of Understanding for Peace + National Reconciliation in the Province of Cabinda partial-resolution framework but FLEC-FAC fragment continuing armed insurgency), as the only modern African sub-national territory with formal pre-Angolan-independence-treaty-framework (Treaty of Simulambuco 1 February 1885 — Portuguese-Cabinda Treaty of Protectorate framework establishing Cabinda as separate Portuguese protectorate distinct from Portuguese Angola framework 1885-1956, only formally-integrated into Portuguese Angola 1956 via Portuguese Ministry of Overseas Cabinda-Angola integration framework). Cabinda Province historically operated under Kingdom of Loango + Kingdom of Ngoyo + Kingdom of Kakongo pre-16th century - 19th century + Portuguese Treaty of Simulambuco 1885 protectorate framework + Portuguese Cabinda 1885-1956 + Portuguese Angola Cabinda integration 1956-1975 + Angolan Cabinda 1975+ via Alvor Agreement 15 January 1975. Cabinda Province comprises ~7,283 km² with a population of ~720,000 (~95% Cabindans + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Angolan civil-law framework (Angolan Family Code 11/2024 + Angolan Civil Code 2024) applied uniformly across Angolan sub-national territories. The Court of Appeal of Cabinda is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of Angola. Cabinda is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Angola is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Cabinda is governed by Angolan non-Hague-Convention-1980 framework. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
