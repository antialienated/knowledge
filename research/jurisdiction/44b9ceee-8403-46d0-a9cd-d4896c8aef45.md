# Australia (Commonwealth of Australia)

Australia is a federal common-law jurisdiction whose family courts (Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia, FCFCOA, established 2021) operate under the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth). The Family Law Amendment Act 2023 substantially restructured the best-interests-of-the-child framework, repealing the presumption of equal shared parental responsibility (s 61DA) and the related compulsory consideration of equal/substantial-and-significant time. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Family Court of Australia and FCFCOA have historically applied PA-related analysis through case-law (e.g., Re W [1995], McCall and Clark [2009]) within the BIC framework. The Australian Law Reform Commission (ALRC) Family Law Report 135 (2019) and AIFS research have informed institutional-critique register against PAS-syndromic framing — particularly in DV-intersection cases.
