# UN Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations (VCDR 1961)

The UN Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations (VCDR) is a global supranational meta-framework diplomatic-relations binding-treaty instrument adopted by the UN Conference on Diplomatic Intercourse and Immunities in Vienna — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal multilateral binding-treaty framework operating specifically on diplomatic-relations framework (VCDR adopted 18 April 1961 in Vienna Austria + effective 24 April 1964 — formal universal multilateral binding-treaty framework establishing comprehensive diplomatic-relations framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate on substantive treaties operating within state framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal diplomatic-immunity framework + codification of customary international diplomatic-relations law framework + most-ratified UN universal treaty framework with 193 state parties matching UN membership), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 22 Premises Inviolability framework (VCDR Article 22 framework providing 'The premises of the mission shall be inviolable. The agents of the receiving State may not enter them, except with the consent of the head of the mission' — formal foundational diplomatic-premises-inviolability framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal absolute diplomatic-premises-inviolability framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 29 Personal Inviolability framework (VCDR Article 29 framework providing 'The person of a diplomatic agent shall be inviolable. He shall not be liable to any form of arrest or detention. The receiving State shall treat him with due respect and shall take all appropriate steps to prevent any attack on his person, freedom or dignity' — formal foundational diplomatic-personal-inviolability framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal absolute diplomatic-personal-inviolability framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 31 Diplomatic Immunity from Jurisdiction framework (VCDR Article 31 framework providing 'A diplomatic agent shall enjoy immunity from the criminal jurisdiction of the receiving State. He shall also enjoy immunity from its civil and administrative jurisdiction' — formal foundational diplomatic-immunity-from-jurisdiction framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal absolute diplomatic-immunity-from-criminal-jurisdiction framework). The VCDR is binding upon 193 state parties matching UN membership as of 2026 — universal coverage framework + most-ratified UN universal treaty framework. The Convention is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Article 31 diplomatic-immunity-from-jurisdiction framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border diplomatic-immunity-blocking-family-court-jurisdiction framework where diplomatic-staff-parent-alienator cases occur. The ICJ + national-level apex courts are the formal judicial bodies; UN Secretariat operates as formal Implementation Framework body. The VCDR is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label.
