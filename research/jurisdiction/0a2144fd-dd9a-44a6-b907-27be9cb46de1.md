# Hamburg / Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg (Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg)

Hamburg (German: Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg 'Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg') is a Northern German civil-law constituent Land of the Federal Republic of Germany — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern German Land with continuous Hanseatic League (Hansa / Hanse) framework historical-cultural-political lineage operating in formal current-Land-name designation (Freie und Hansestadt — only modern jurisdiction globally with formal Hanseatic prefix in current sub-national jurisdiction name, alongside Bremen as 'Freie Hansestadt Bremen' — Hamburg's Hansa membership 1241+ continuous through Hanseatic League dissolution 1862 + formal Free Hanseatic City designation continuous through Imperial Free City framework 1410-1806 + Free City of Hamburg 1815-1933 + Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg 1949+), as one of three German Stadtstaaten (city-state Länder operating simultaneously as Land + municipality — Hamburg + Berlin + Bremen, distinct from rest-of-German-Länder territorial framework), as the second-largest German port + central jurisdiction of the largest port of Continental Europe (Port of Hamburg — handling ~7.7 million TEU containers 2024, second-largest port in Europe after Rotterdam + third-largest in Europe by tonnage), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most economically distinctive modern German sub-national maritime-commercial frameworks (Hamburg historically dominant Hansa-League commercial framework + modern North-Sea-Baltic-Sea logistics framework + Hamburg-Altona-rail-network framework). Hamburg historically operated under Hanseatic League 1241-1862 + Imperial Free City 1410-1806 + Free City of Hamburg 1815-1933 + Nazi German Gau-system 1933-1945 (suffering Operation Gomorrah firebombing 24 July - 3 August 1943 with ~37,000 civilian deaths) + post-1945 British Zone of Occupation 1945-1949 + Federal Republic of Germany 1949+. Hamburg comprises ~755 km² with a population of ~1.85 million. Family-law framework operates under the federal German civil-law framework (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch BGB 1896 Family Law portion + German Family-Law Reform Act 2024) applied uniformly across German Länder. The Hanseatic Oberlandesgericht is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Federal Court of Justice of Germany. Hamburg is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Germany is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 27 September 1990 effective 1 December 1990) — Hamburg is governed by German Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
