# Czechia (Česko / Czech Republic)

Czechia is a civil-law unitary republic whose family-law framework was substantially modernised by the New Civil Code (Občanský zákoník, Law 89/2012) in force 1 January 2014. Parental responsibility (rodičovská odpovědnost) is governed by §§ 858-880 of the New Civil Code. The Supreme Court (Nejvyšší soud, Brno) is the apex civil-court of cassation; the Constitutional Court (Ústavní soud, Brno) operates a separate constitutional-review jurisdiction. Family-law matters are heard first-instance by Okresní soud (District Courts) with appeals to Krajský soud (Regional Courts). Psychology profession is regulated under Law 96/2004 on non-medical health professions and the Czech Psychological Society (Českomoravská psychologická společnost / ČMPS) operates ethics oversight. Czechia is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label; courts operate substantively under the nejlepší zájem dítěte (best-interests-of-the-child) standard. The Constitutional Court has produced jurisprudence on parental-contact enforcement engaging the European Convention positive-obligations framework.
