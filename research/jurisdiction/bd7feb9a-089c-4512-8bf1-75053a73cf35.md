# Singapore (Republic of Singapore / 新加坡共和国)

Singapore is a common-law jurisdiction with parallel Syariah Court framework for Muslim personal-status matters under the Administration of Muslim Law Act (AMLA). Family courts operate under the Family Justice Courts (established 1 October 2014 under the Family Justice Act 2014) consolidating Family Court, Youth Court, Family Division of the High Court. The Women's Charter 1961 (extensively amended) governs civil marriage and divorce; AMLA governs Muslim personal-status. The Guardianship of Infants Act and Women's Charter Part X establish welfare standard. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Court of Appeal of Singapore has addressed contact-refusal cases applying welfare standard.
