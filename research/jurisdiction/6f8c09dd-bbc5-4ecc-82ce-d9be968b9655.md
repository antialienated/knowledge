# Pacific Islands Forum 1971 + 2005 Agreement Establishing PIF

The Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-Pacific-Islands-Forum framework adopted by the 7 founding Pacific states in Wellington — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Pacific regional supranational framework operating on formal Pacific-Ocean framework + only modern regional supranational framework operating formal
