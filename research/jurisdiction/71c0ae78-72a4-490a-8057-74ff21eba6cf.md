# Berlin (Land Berlin / Bundeshauptstadt)

Berlin (German: Land Berlin / Bundeshauptstadt 'Federal Capital') is a Northeastern German civil-law constituent Land of the Federal Republic of Germany + German federal capital — structurally distinctive globally as the only post-WWII jurisdiction divided continuously for 44 years (1945-1989) under formal four-power Allied occupation framework (US + UK + France + Soviet Union — Yalta Conference 4-11 February 1945 + Potsdam Conference 17 July - 2 August 1945 + Berlin Declaration 5 June 1945 + Four Powers Status of Berlin 12 September 1944, four-power-occupation framework continuous 1945-1990 only ended by Treaty on the Final Settlement with Respect to Germany 12 September 1990), as the central jurisdiction of the most distinctive modern divided-city Cold War framework (Berlin Wall Berliner Mauer 13 August 1961 - 9 November 1989 — 155 km concrete wall + barbed-wire framework dividing West Berlin from East Berlin + East Germany, with ~140 confirmed deaths attempting Berlin Wall crossings), as one of three German Stadtstaaten (city-state Länder operating simultaneously as Land + municipality — Berlin + Hamburg + Bremen, distinct from rest-of-German-Länder territorial framework), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern German federal-capital-city frameworks (Berlin became German federal capital via German federal decision of 20 June 1991 effective 1999 — Bonn-to-Berlin Capital Move framework, only modern federal capital established via formal post-Cold-War-reunification political decision). Berlin was historically the capital of Brandenburg-Prussia 1701-1871 + capital of German Empire 1871-1918 + capital of Weimar Republic 1918-1933 + capital of Nazi Germany 1933-1945 + post-WWII four-power occupation 1945-1990 (with West Berlin operating as de facto West German territory + East Berlin as East German capital 1949-1990), reunified 3 October 1990, and re-established as German federal capital 1999. Berlin comprises ~891 km² with a population of ~3.7 million. Family-law framework operates under the federal German civil-law framework (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch BGB 1896 Family Law portion + German Family-Law Reform Act 2024) applied uniformly across German Länder. The Kammergericht (Berlin's Higher Regional Court) is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Federal Court of Justice of Germany. Berlin is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Germany is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 27 September 1990 effective 1 December 1990) — Berlin is governed by German Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
