# Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region (内蒙古自治区 / ᠥᠪᠥᠷ ᠮᠣᠩᠭᠣᠯ ᠤᠨ ᠥᠪᠡᠷᠲᠡᠭᠡᠨ ᠵᠠᠰᠠᠬᠤ ᠣᠷᠣᠨ)

The Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region (内蒙古自治区 Nèi Měnggǔ Zìzhìqū in Chinese / ᠥᠪᠥᠷ ᠮᠣᠩᠭᠣᠯ ᠤᠨ ᠥᠪᠡᠷᠲᠡᠭᠡᠨ ᠵᠠᠰᠠᠬᠤ ᠣᠷᠣᠨ Öbör Mongγol-un öbertegen Jasaqu Orun in Traditional Mongolian Script) is a Northern Chinese civil-law Chinese autonomous-region constituent unit of the People's Republic of China — structurally distinctive globally as the oldest Chinese autonomous-region in modern Chinese state formation history (Inner Mongolia Autonomous Government established 1 May 1947 — preceding the founding of the People's Republic of China by 2 years 5 months 1 October 1949, making Inner Mongolia the precedent template for all subsequent Chinese autonomous-region frameworks), as the third-largest Chinese province-level division by area (~1,183,000 km² — ~12.3% of Chinese land area, third after Xinjiang + Tibet), as the only modern Chinese sub-national territory operating Traditional Mongolian Script ᠮᠣᠩᠭᠣᠯ ᠪᠢᠴᠢᠭ in formal government usage (Inner Mongolia is the only modern jurisdiction globally where Traditional Mongolian Script — the vertical script descended from Old Uyghur framework — is used in official government documentation, as Mongolia itself uses Cyrillic-script Mongolian since 1946 Mongolian script reform), and as the central jurisdiction of the 2020 Inner Mongolian-language education protests (August-September 2020 protests against Chinese Government policy reducing Mongolian-language instruction in Inner Mongolian schools — pivotal cultural-political-identity contestation framework). Inner Mongolia historically operated under multiple Mongol Khanate frameworks (Mongol Empire 1206-1368 + Northern Yuan 1368-1635 + Qing Inner Mongolian league framework 1635-1912), was contested 1912-1947 between Republic of China + Mongolian People's Republic (1924-1992) + various Inner Mongolian self-government frameworks, established Inner Mongolia Autonomous Government 1 May 1947, and was formally incorporated into People's Republic of China 1949. Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region comprises ~1,183,000 km² with a population of ~24 million (~4.2 million Mongolian + ~19 million Han + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Chinese civil-law framework (Chinese Marriage Law 1980 + Chinese Civil Code 2020 Family Law portion Articles 1040-1118) applied uniformly across Chinese autonomous regions. The Inner Mongolia High People's Court is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme People's Court of China. Inner Mongolia is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. China is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Inner Mongolia is governed by the Chinese non-ratification status.
