# CARICOM Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas 2001 (Caribbean Community)

The Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas Establishing the Caribbean Community Including the CARICOM Single Market and Economy (CARICOM Revised Treaty) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-CARICOM-treaty framework adopted by the 15 founding Caribbean states in Nassau — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Caribbean regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal Caribbean-Community framework + Anglophone-Caribbean-with-Suriname-Haiti-mixed-membership framework (CARICOM Revised Treaty signed 5 July 2001 in Nassau Bahamas + effective 1 January 2006 + original Treaty of Chaguaramas signed 4 July 1973 in Chaguaramas Trinidad and Tobago founding Caribbean Community — formal foundational revised CARICOM treaty framework establishing comprehensive CARICOM organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on different geographic frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Caribbean regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Caribbean-Community framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Anglophone-Caribbean-with-Suriname-Haiti-mixed-membership framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal small-island-developing-states SIDS framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal CARICOM Single Market and Economy CSME framework (CARICOM Single Market launched 1 January 2006 + CSME framework providing formal Caribbean-regional single-market framework — formal foundational Caribbean-regional single-market framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Caribbean regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal single-market framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Caribbean Court of Justice CCJ framework (Agreement Establishing the Caribbean Court of Justice signed 14 February 2001 in Bridgetown Barbados + effective 23 July 2002 + CCJ inaugurated 16 April 2005 in Port of Spain Trinidad and Tobago providing formal CARICOM judicial framework operating Original Jurisdiction framework on CSME + Appellate Jurisdiction framework as final court of appeal replacing JCPC for 4 CARICOM states — formal foundational CARICOM judicial framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Caribbean regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal hybrid Original-Jurisdiction-on-CSME-plus-Appellate-Jurisdiction-replacing-JCPC framework + first regional court globally operating formal hybrid international-court-plus-domestic-final-court-of-appeal framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal CARICOM ICJ Climate Change Advisory Opinion framework (CARICOM-led Vanuatu UN General Assembly Resolution 77/276 of 29 March 2023 requesting ICJ advisory opinion on climate-change-obligations-of-states + ICJ delivered Advisory Opinion 23 July 2025 — formal foundational CARICOM-led climate-change-international-obligations framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional-led ICJ climate-change-advisory-opinion framework). The CARICOM Revised Treaty is binding upon 15 CARICOM member states + 5 CARICOM associate members as of 2026 (Antigua and Barbuda + Bahamas + Barbados + Belize + Dominica + Grenada + Guyana + Haiti + Jamaica + Montserrat + St. Kitts and Nevis + St. Lucia + St. Vincent and the Grenadines + Suriname + Trinidad and Tobago) — comprehensive Caribbean regional coverage framework. The Treaty is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal CARICOM CCJ Appellate Jurisdiction framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Caribbean-regional cross-border-children's-rights framework via CCJ appellate-jurisdiction framework.
