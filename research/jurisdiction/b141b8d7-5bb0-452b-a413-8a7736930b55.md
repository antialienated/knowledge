# AfCFTA 2018 (African Continental Free Trade Area)

The Agreement Establishing the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-AfCFTA-treaty framework adopted by the 44 founding African states in Kigali — structurally distinctive globally as the largest free trade area globally by member states + only modern African continental supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal continental free-trade framework (AfCFTA Agreement signed 21 March 2018 in Kigali Rwanda by 44 founding African states + effective 30 May 2019 + trading-operational 1 January 2021 — formal foundational AfCFTA treaty framework establishing comprehensive African continental free-trade framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on regional or sub-continental scopes, structurally distinctive globally as the largest free trade area globally by member states with 54 of 55 AU member states framework + only modern African continental supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal continental free-trade framework + 1.4 billion population coverage framework + USD 3.4 trillion combined GDP framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Eritrea-only-non-signatory framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal English-French-Arabic-Portuguese-Spanish-Swahili six-language framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal AfCFTA Protocol on Trade in Goods + Trade in Services frameworks (AfCFTA Protocol on Trade in Goods + Protocol on Trade in Services adopted 21 March 2018 providing formal continental free-trade-in-goods + free-trade-in-services framework — formal foundational AfCFTA trade-in-goods + trade-in-services framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African continental supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal continental trade-in-goods + trade-in-services framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal AfCFTA Dispute Settlement Mechanism framework (AfCFTA Protocol on Rules and Procedures on the Settlement of Disputes adopted 21 March 2018 providing formal AfCFTA dispute-settlement mechanism via Dispute Settlement Body DSB + AfCFTA Appellate Body framework following WTO Dispute Settlement Understanding model — formal foundational AfCFTA dispute-settlement framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African continental supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal WTO-DSU-model continental dispute-settlement framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal AfCFTA Phase II + Phase III Protocols framework (AfCFTA Phase II Protocols on Investment + Intellectual Property Rights + Competition Policy adopted 19 February 2023 at 36th AU Summit + AfCFTA Phase III Protocol on Digital Trade + Protocol on Women + Youth in Trade adopted 2024-2025 — formal foundational AfCFTA Phase II + Phase III framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African continental supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal continental investment + IP + competition + digital-trade + women-youth-in-trade framework). The AfCFTA Agreement is binding upon 54 of 55 AU member states as of 2026 (Eritrea-only-non-signatory) with 47 ratifications as of 2026 — comprehensive African continental coverage framework. The Agreement is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal AfCFTA Protocol on Women + Youth in Trade framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal African continental women + youth + cross-border-children's-rights framework. The AfCFTA Council of Ministers + AfCFTA Senior Trade Officials Committee + AfCFTA Secretariat + AfCFTA Dispute Settlement Body DSB are the formal AfCFTA bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies. The AfCFTA Agreement is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
