# Arab League Charter 1945 (Charter of the League of Arab States)

The Charter of the League of Arab States (Arab League Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-Arab-League-treaty framework adopted by the founding 7 Arab states in Cairo — structurally distinctive globally as the oldest modern Middle East regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on pan-Arab framework (Arab League Charter signed 22 March 1945 in Cairo Egypt + effective 10 May 1945 — formal foundational Arab League treaty framework establishing comprehensive Arab League organisation framework predating UN Charter by three months, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which postdate UN Charter, structurally distinctive globally as the oldest modern Middle East regional supranational binding-treaty framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework predating UN Charter framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Arab Charter on Human Rights 2004 framework (Arab Charter on Human Rights adopted 22 May 2004 by Arab League Summit in Tunis Tunisia + effective 15 March 2008 + Arab Human Rights Committee established 2009 — formal foundational Arab-regional-human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Middle East regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional human-rights framework + only modern regional human-rights framework operating without formal individual-petition or court framework via Arab Human Rights Committee state-state reporting framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Arab Court of Human Rights framework (Statute of the Arab Court of Human Rights adopted 7 September 2014 by Arab League Council in Cairo Egypt + not yet operational due to insufficient ratifications as of 2026 — formal foundational Arab-regional-human-rights-court framework adopted but not operational, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal court-statute-adopted-but-not-operational framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Riyadh Convention 1983 framework (Riyadh Arab Agreement for Judicial Cooperation adopted 6 April 1983 in Riyadh Saudi Arabia + effective 22 October 1985 — formal foundational Arab-regional cross-border judicial-cooperation framework with PA-intersection framework). The Arab League Charter is binding upon 22 Arab League member states as of 2026 — comprehensive Arab regional coverage framework (Syria reinstated 2023 after 12-year 2011 suspension). The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Arab Charter on Human Rights 2004 framework + Riyadh Convention 1983 framework operate substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Arab-regional cross-border-children's-rights framework. The Arab League Council + Arab League Secretariat + Arab Human Rights Committee are the formal Arab League bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies. The Arab League Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
