# Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM / بڠسامورو)

The Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM / بڠسامورو in Jawi-script Bangsamoro) is a Southern Philippine mixed common-law/Islamic-law autonomous region of the Republic of the Philippines — structurally distinctive globally as the only Philippine autonomous region with formal Islamic-law family-law framework (Code of Muslim Personal Laws of the Philippines 1977 Presidential Decree 1083 + Bangsamoro-Specific Shari'ah Courts framework + Code of Muslim Personal Laws integration with BARMM Organic Law framework — distinct from rest-of-Philippines' Catholic-tradition civil-law family-law framework), as the only Philippine autonomous region established via formal peace-agreement framework derived from internal armed conflict resolution (Bangsamoro Organic Law Republic Act 11054 effective 27 January 2019 implementing Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro 27 March 2014 between Philippines Government + Moro Islamic Liberation Front MILF — ending 1968-2014 Moro insurgency framework with ~120,000-150,000 cumulative deaths), as the central jurisdiction of the most sustained modern Philippine internal armed conflict (Moro National Liberation Front MNLF founded 1972 + Moro Islamic Liberation Front MILF founded 1977 + Abu Sayyaf Group ASG founded 1991 — 46-year Moro insurgency framework 1968-2014), and as a distinctive jurisdiction within the Philippines for the Moro-cultural-political-identity-vs-Filipino-Catholic-cultural-political-assimilation tension (historic Sulu Sultanate 1457-1915 + Maguindanao Sultanate 1520-1888 + Spanish-Moro Wars 1565-1898 + American-Moro Wars 1899-1913 + Marcos-era 1972 Jabidah massacre catalysing Moro insurgency framework). BARMM was established 21 January 2019 via plebiscite under Bangsamoro Organic Law replacing the prior Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM 1990-2019) — BARMM comprises ~36,826 km² with a population of ~4.4 million across Lanao del Sur + Maguindanao del Norte + Maguindanao del Sur + Basilan + Sulu + Tawi-Tawi provinces + 63 Cotabato barangays + Cotabato City. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Philippine common-law framework + Bangsamoro-specific Code of Muslim Personal Laws 1977 PD 1083 Shari'ah-courts family-law framework. The Bangsamoro Sharia District Courts operate as primary courts for Islamic family-law matters; the Sandiganbayan Cotabato Branch + Court of Appeals Philippines are intermediate appellate courts; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Philippines. Bangsamoro is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. The Philippines is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 8 March 2016 effective 1 June 2016).
