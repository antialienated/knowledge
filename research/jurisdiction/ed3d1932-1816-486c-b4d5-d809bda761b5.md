# UN Charter 1945 (Charter of the United Nations)

The UN Charter 1945 (formally Charter of the United Nations adopted by the UN Conference on International Organization in San Francisco) is a global supranational meta-framework foundational-UN-treaty framework adopted by the UN Conference on International Organization — structurally distinctive globally as the foundational modern UN international treaty framework underlying all subsequent modern UN human-rights + international-law + international-criminal-law + private-international-law frameworks (UN Charter signed 26 June 1945 in San Francisco + effective 24 October 1945 — formal foundational UN treaty framework establishing comprehensive UN organisation framework + ICJ Statute, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate as substantive treaties subsequent to + underlying the UN Charter framework, structurally distinctive globally as the foundational modern UN international treaty framework underlying all subsequent modern UN human-rights + international-law + international-criminal-law + private-international-law frameworks), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 1 UN purposes framework (UN Charter Article 1 framework providing 'The Purposes of the United Nations are: 1. To maintain international peace and security... 2. To develop friendly relations among nations based on respect for the principle of equal rights and self-determination of peoples... 3. To achieve international co-operation in solving international problems of an economic, social, cultural, or humanitarian character, and in promoting and encouraging respect for human rights and for fundamental freedoms for all without distinction as to race, sex, language, or religion' — formal foundational UN purposes framework, structurally distinctive globally as the foundational modern UN purposes framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Chapter VII Article 39-51 collective-security framework (UN Charter Chapter VII Articles 39-51 framework providing collective-security framework via UN Security Council + Article 41 sanctions framework + Article 42 military-action framework + Article 51 self-defense framework — formal foundational collective-security framework, structurally distinctive globally as the foundational modern collective-security framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 103 UN-Charter-supremacy framework (UN Charter Article 103 framework providing 'In the event of a conflict between the obligations of the Members of the United Nations under the present Charter and their obligations under any other international agreement, their obligations under the present Charter shall prevail' — formal foundational UN-Charter-supremacy framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern international treaty framework operating formal supremacy framework). The UN Charter is binding upon all 193 UN member states + observer states — universal coverage framework. The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Article 1 UN purposes framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border-children's-rights-framework underlying all subsequent modern international + regional + sub-national children's-rights frameworks. The UN General Assembly + UN Security Council + International Court of Justice ICJ + UN Secretariat + UN Trusteeship Council + UN Economic and Social Council ECOSOC are the formal UN principal organ bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
