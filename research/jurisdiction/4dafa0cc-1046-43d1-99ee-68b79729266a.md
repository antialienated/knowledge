# Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria (AANES / Rojava / ڕۆژاوا)

The Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria (AANES — formally Rojava ڕۆژاوا 'West' in Kurdish, also Northern Syria Democratic Federation) is a Northeastern Syrian mixed civil-law/customary-law de facto autonomous region of Syria — structurally distinctive globally as the only sub-state-level Kurdish jurisdiction operating formal Democratic Confederalism framework (Apo Öcalan-Bookchin Democratic Confederalism framework — distinct council-democracy-libertarian-municipalism political-organisational framework, distinct from rest-of-Kurdish-jurisdiction frameworks in Iraq, Turkey, Iran which operate conventional nation-state autonomy frameworks), as the only modern Syrian sub-national territory established via post-2011 Syrian Civil War framework with formal multi-ethnic Kurdish-Arab-Syriac-Christian-Turkmen self-governance framework (2014 Charter of the Social Contract of the Cantons + 2016 Federation of Northern Syria + 2018 Constitution of the Federation of Northern Syria-Rojava — distinct from Iraqi Kurdistan's mono-ethnic Kurdish framework), as the central jurisdiction of the most militarily distinctive modern Kurdish self-governance jurisdiction (Syrian Democratic Forces SDF + People's Defence Units YPG + Women's Defence Units YPJ — central role in 2014-2019 international anti-ISIS coalition framework with US-led international military support framework), and as the only modern de facto autonomous region operating formal gender-equality co-governance framework (formal requirement for male+female co-chair leadership at all administrative levels — Jineolojî 'science of women' Kurdish-feminist theoretical framework, distinct from rest-of-Kurdish-jurisdiction frameworks). Rojava historically operated under Ottoman vilayet framework + French Mandate Syria 1920-1946 + Syrian Republic frameworks 1946-2012, achieved de facto autonomy 19 July 2012 following Syrian Civil War 2011-onwards withdrawal of Syrian Government forces (Rojava Revolution framework), established the Federation of Northern Syria 2016, ratified the Constitution of the Federation of Northern Syria-Rojava 2018, and continues to operate as de facto autonomous region without formal constitutional recognition by Syrian Government. AANES comprises ~50,000 km² with a population of ~2 million across Cizîrê + Euphrates + Afrin + Manbij + Tabqa + Raqqa + Deir ez-Zor cantons. Family-law framework operates under a dual customary-Kurdish framework + AANES Family-Law Code framework + residual Syrian-Civil-Code framework. The People's Defence Court is the primary court framework; final appellate jurisdiction is contested between AANES Appellate Court + Syrian Federal Court. AANES is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Syria is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — AANES is governed by Syrian non-ratification status + de facto AANES Family-Law Code framework.
