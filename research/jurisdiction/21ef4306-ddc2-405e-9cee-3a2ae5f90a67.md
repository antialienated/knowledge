# Anguilla

Anguilla is a Caribbean common-law British Overseas Territory whose family-law framework operates under the Children Act 2014 (drawing on English Children Act 1989 model), the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Act, and the Status of Children Act. Parental responsibility and child custody are governed by Children Act 2014 Part II. The Court of Appeal of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (sitting for Anguilla) is the apex domestic appellate court; final appellate jurisdiction was retained with the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council in London. Family-law matters are heard at first instance in the High Court of Justice and Magistrates' Court. Psychology profession is regulated through the Anguilla Ministry of Health framework. Anguilla is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label; courts operate substantively under the welfare-of-the-child principle codified in Children Act 2014 s. 3.
