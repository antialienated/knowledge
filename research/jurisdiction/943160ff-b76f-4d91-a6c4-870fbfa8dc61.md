# Chechen Republic (Чеченская Республика / Нохчийн Республика)

The Chechen Republic (Russian: Чеченская Республика Chechenskaya Respublika / Chechen: Нохчийн Республика Noxçiyn Respublika) is a North Caucasus civil-law/Sharia-customary constituent republic of the Russian Federation — structurally distinctive globally as the central jurisdiction of two modern formal Russian-Chechen Wars (First Chechen War 11 December 1994 - 31 August 1996 + Second Chechen War 26 August 1999 - 16 April 2009 — cumulative ~150,000-250,000 deaths, only modern post-Soviet Russian sub-national territory subject to two formal full-scale wars with Russian Federation), as the only Russian sub-national republic with continuous de facto independence period 1991-1999 followed by Russian reintegration via armed force (Chechen Republic of Ichkeria 1991-2007 — declared independence 1 November 1991, de facto independent 1991-1999 + 1999-2007 nominal-government-in-exile-and-insurgency framework, only modern post-Soviet Russian sub-national territory with formal continuous independence-declaration framework), as the central jurisdiction of the most distinctive modern Russian sub-national de facto dual-legal-framework operating Russian civil-law + Sharia/Adat customary-law framework (Kadyrov era post-2007 framework — formal dual-framework operation despite formal constitutional secularism, distinct from rest-of-Russian-Federation operating uniform secular civil-law framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Chechen-Ingush Deportation 1944-1957 historical framework (Soviet Operation Lentil 23 February 1944 — Stalin Soviet government deportation of ~478,000 Chechen + ~96,000 Ingush people to Central Asia + Siberia with ~25-50% mortality framework, formally recognised as Soviet genocide by European Parliament Resolution 26 February 2004). Chechnya historically operated under Chechen Imamate 1828-1859 (Imam Shamil Caucasian Imamate framework) + Russian Empire 1859-1917 + Soviet Mountain ASSR 1921-1924 + Soviet Chechen Autonomous Oblast 1922-1934 + Soviet Chechen-Ingush ASSR 1934-1944 + Soviet Deportation 1944-1957 (with formal abolition of Chechen-Ingush ASSR 1944) + restored Chechen-Ingush ASSR 1957-1991 + Chechen Republic of Ichkeria 1991-1999 + First Chechen War 1994-1996 + Second Chechen War 1999-2009 + Chechen Republic Russian Federation 2003+. Chechnya comprises ~17,300 km² with a population of ~1.5 million. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Russian civil-law framework (Russian Family Code 1995 + Russian Civil Code 1994) + Kadyrov-era de facto Sharia/Adat customary-law family-law framework. The Supreme Court of Chechen Republic is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation. Chechnya is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Russia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 28 July 2011 effective 1 October 2011) — Chechnya is governed by Russian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
