# Sarawak (Bumi Kenyalang / 砂拉越)

Sarawak (Malay: Bumi Kenyalang 'Land of the Hornbills' / 砂拉越 Shālāyuè in Chinese) is a Bornean Malaysian mixed common-law/Adat-customary-law constituent state of the Federation of Malaysia — structurally distinctive globally as the largest Malaysian state by area (~124,450 km² — ~37.5% of Malaysian land area, larger than the entire Peninsular Malaysia combined ~131,598 km²), as the only Malaysian state with formal autonomy framework under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 (MA63) including formal immigration-control autonomy (Sarawak operates independent immigration controls under Immigration Ordinance 1953 Sarawak Cap. 51 — distinct from Peninsular Malaysia, requiring Sarawak immigration clearance for Peninsular Malaysians + foreign nationals entering Sarawak), as a distinctive jurisdiction within Malaysia for the formal recognition of Native Customary Rights (NCR) Adat framework (Sarawak Land Code 1958 + Native Courts Ordinance 1992 — formal Adat-customary-law framework distinct from rest-of-Malaysia's secular common-law framework), and as the only Malaysian state with continuous Brooke 'White Rajah' framework 1841-1946 (Sarawak Kingdom under James Brooke + Charles Brooke + Charles Vyner Brooke 1841-1946 + Japanese occupation 1941-1945 + British Crown Colony 1946-1963 + Federation of Malaysia 16 September 1963). Sarawak comprises ~124,450 km² with a population of ~2.9 million. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Malaysian common-law framework + Sarawak-specific Native Courts Adat-customary-law family-law framework for indigenous communities. The Sarawak High Court (located in Kuching) is the apex domestic appellate court — operates under the Kuching High Court framework with parallel Native Court Adat-framework jurisdiction; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Court of Appeal of Malaysia and Federal Court of Malaysia. Sarawak is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Malaysia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Sarawak is governed by the Malaysian non-ratification status.
