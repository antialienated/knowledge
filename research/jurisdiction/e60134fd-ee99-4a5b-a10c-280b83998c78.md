# ECOWAS Revised Treaty 1993 (Economic Community of West African States)

The Revised Treaty of the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS Revised Treaty) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-ECOWAS-treaty framework adopted by the 16 ECOWAS member states in Cotonou — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern West African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal West-African common-market framework + English-French-Portuguese trilingual framework (ECOWAS Revised Treaty signed 24 July 1993 in Cotonou Benin + effective 23 August 1995 + ECOWAS founded 28 May 1975 via Treaty of Lagos — formal foundational ECOWAS treaty framework establishing comprehensive ECOWAS organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on formal monolingual frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern West African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal English-French-Portuguese trilingual treaty framework + only modern African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal customs-union framework via ECOWAS Common External Tariff CET adopted 25 October 2013 + effective 1 January 2015), as the central jurisdiction of the formal ECOWAS Court of Justice framework (ECOWAS Court of Justice ECCJ established 1991 by Protocol on the Community Court of Justice + Supplementary Protocol 2005 granting jurisdiction over human-rights violations in any ECOWAS member state — formal foundational ECOWAS judicial framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern West African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal individual-petition human-rights court framework without exhaustion-of-domestic-remedies requirement framework + first regional African court operating formal individual-petition framework without exhaustion-of-domestic-remedies framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal ECOWAS Protocol on Free Movement framework (ECOWAS Protocol on Free Movement of Persons signed 29 May 1979 in Dakar Senegal providing formal right of entry + residence + establishment framework — formal foundational West-African free-movement framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal free-movement-of-persons framework with operational ECOWAS passport framework + ECOWAS travel-certificate framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal ECOWAS Democratic Suspension framework (ECOWAS Protocol on Democracy and Good Governance signed 21 December 2001 in Dakar Senegal + ECOWAS Niger 2023 + Burkina Faso 2022 + Mali 2021 + Guinea 2021 suspension framework — formal foundational ECOWAS democratic-suspension framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern African regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal democratic-suspension framework with operational suspension framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework with formal Sahel Trio Burkina-Faso-Mali-Niger withdrawal framework effective 29 January 2025). The ECOWAS Revised Treaty is binding upon 12 ECOWAS member states as of 2026 (down from 15 after Sahel Trio Burkina Faso + Mali + Niger withdrawal effective 29 January 2025 + Cape Verde + Côte d'Ivoire + Gambia + Ghana + Guinea + Guinea-Bissau + Liberia + Nigeria + Senegal + Sierra Leone + Togo + Benin) — substantial West African regional coverage framework. The Treaty is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal ECOWAS Court of Justice framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal ECOWAS-regional cross-border-children's-rights framework via individual-petition framework. The ECOWAS Authority of Heads of State and Government + ECOWAS Council of Ministers + ECOWAS Commission + ECOWAS Court of Justice + ECOWAS Parliament are the formal ECOWAS bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
