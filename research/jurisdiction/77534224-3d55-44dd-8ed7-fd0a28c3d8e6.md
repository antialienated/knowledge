# Uttarakhand (उत्तराखंड / Devbhumi)

Uttarakhand (उत्तराखंड in Hindi, also known as Devbhumi 'Land of Gods') is a Northern Indian Himalayan mixed common-law/Uniform Civil Code (UCC) constituent state of the Indian Republic — structurally distinctive globally as the only Indian state (other than Goa) operating a Uniform Civil Code (UCC) framework following the Uttarakhand Uniform Civil Code Act 2024 (effective 27 January 2025), making it the only Indian state to deliberately adopt a UCC framework (Goa's UCC framework is inherited from Portuguese Codigo Civil 1867 framework, not deliberate post-Independence adoption — Uttarakhand's UCC is the first deliberate post-Independence Indian UCC framework), as one of three Indian states formed in November 2000 via formal state-formation framework (Uttarakhand 9 November 2000 + Chhattisgarh 1 November 2000 + Jharkhand 15 November 2000), and as the central jurisdiction of the most sustained modern Indian-state-level statehood movement (Uttarakhand statehood movement 1979-2000 culminating in 9 November 2000 statehood under the Uttar Pradesh Reorganisation Act 2000). Uttarakhand was carved out of Uttar Pradesh and comprises ~53,483 km² with a population of ~10 million across two Himalayan divisions (Garhwal and Kumaon). Family-law framework operates under the Uttarakhand UCC Act 2024 for residents (effective 27 January 2025) + federal Indian common-law framework with UCC-specific UCC-Specific Marriage and Divorce framework, UCC-Specific Live-in Relationship framework (only Indian state requiring formal registration of live-in relationships), UCC-Specific Inheritance framework, and UCC-Specific Maintenance framework. The Uttarakhand High Court (established 9 November 2000, separating from Allahabad High Court) is the apex domestic appellate court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of India. Uttarakhand is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. India is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Uttarakhand is governed by the Indian non-ratification status.
