# African Union Constitutive Act 2000

The African Union Constitutive Act is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-AU-treaty framework adopted by the OAU Assembly of Heads of State and Government in Lomé Togo — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on Africa-wide continental framework with formal Article 4(h) right-of-intervention framework (AU Constitutive Act adopted 11 July 2000 in Lomé Togo + effective 26 May 2001 + AU launched 9 July 2002 in Durban South Africa replacing Organisation of African Unity OAU established 25 May 1963 — formal foundational AU treaty framework establishing comprehensive AU organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate without formal right-of-intervention framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Article 4(h) right-of-intervention-in-grave-circumstances framework + only regional organisation operating formal continental-coverage framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 4(h) Right of Intervention framework (AU Constitutive Act Article 4(h) framework providing 'the right of the Union to intervene in a Member State pursuant to a decision of the Assembly in respect of grave circumstances, namely: war crimes, genocide and crimes against humanity' — formal foundational right-of-intervention framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal right-of-intervention framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights Banjul Charter framework (Banjul Charter adopted 27 June 1981 in Nairobi Kenya + effective 21 October 1986 + African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights ACHPR established 1987 + African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights AfCHPR established 1998/2006 — formal foundational Africa-regional-human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal two-tier ACHPR + AfCHPR Africa-regional-human-rights framework + only modern regional human-rights framework formally including peoples' rights framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child ACRWC framework (ACRWC adopted 11 July 1990 in Addis Ababa Ethiopia + effective 29 November 1999 + African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child ACERWC established 2001 — formal foundational Africa-regional-children's-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional children's-rights framework with PA-intersection framework). The AU Constitutive Act is binding upon 55 AU member states as of 2026 — comprehensive Africa-continental coverage framework. The Act is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal ACRWC framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Africa-regional cross-border-children's-rights framework. The AU Assembly + AU Executive Council + AU Commission + Pan-African Parliament + African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights AfCHPR are the formal AU bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
