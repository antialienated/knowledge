# African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights (Banjul Charter 1981)

The African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights (popularly the Banjul Charter) is a Sub-Saharan African supranational meta-framework human-rights binding-treaty instrument adopted by the Organisation of African Unity (OAU — succeeded by the African Union AU) — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional binding-treaty framework operating formal combined individual + collective peoples' rights framework (African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights adopted 27 June 1981 + effective 21 October 1986 — formal African regional binding-treaty framework establishing comprehensive human-rights framework including formal Article 19-24 peoples' rights framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-human-rights frameworks which operate on individual-rights framework without formal peoples'-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional human-rights binding-treaty framework operating formal peoples'-rights framework), as the third modern regional binding-treaty framework operating both formal human-rights binding-treaty framework + dedicated judicial-body framework (Banjul Charter + African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights established via Protocol of 10 June 1998 effective 25 January 2004 + African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights established 1987 — formal African regional court framework + dedicated commission framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-human-rights frameworks which operate without formal regional-court framework, structurally distinctive globally as one of three modern regional human-rights frameworks with formal regional-court framework alongside ECHR + IACtHR), as the central jurisdiction of the formal African Article 18 family-rights + Article 19-24 peoples'-rights framework (Banjul Charter Article 18 framework on Family + Articles 19-24 peoples'-rights framework — formal foundational family-rights + peoples'-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional human-rights binding-treaty framework operating formal combined family-rights + peoples'-rights framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child ACRWC 1990 complementary framework (Banjul Charter Article 18 framework + ACRWC 1990/1999 framework operating complementary children's-rights + family-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional binding-treaty framework operating formal complementary children's-rights binding-treaty framework). The Banjul Charter is binding upon 55 of 55 African Union member states + 1 of 2 Western Saharan Arab Democratic Republic SADR state party framework — universal African coverage framework. The Convention is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Article 18 family-rights framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework. The African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights + African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights are the apex judicial + monitoring bodies.
