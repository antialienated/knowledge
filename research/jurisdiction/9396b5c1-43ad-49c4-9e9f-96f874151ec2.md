# SCO Charter 2002 (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation)

The Charter of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-SCO-treaty framework adopted by the 6 founding Eurasian states in Saint Petersburg — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Eurasian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal Eurasian-strategic-framework + China-Russia-co-anchor framework (SCO Charter signed 7 June 2002 in Saint Petersburg Russia by China + Kazakhstan + Kyrgyzstan + Russia + Tajikistan + Uzbekistan + effective 19 September 2003 + Shanghai Five predecessor founded 26 April 1996 in Shanghai China — formal foundational SCO treaty framework establishing comprehensive SCO organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on bilateral-only frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Eurasian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Eurasian-strategic-framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal China-Russia-co-anchor framework + most-populous regional supranational binding-treaty framework with ~3.4 billion population coverage framework spanning Eurasia geographic framework after India + Pakistan accession 2017), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Three Evils framework (SCO Charter Article 1 framework providing formal SCO mandate on Three Evils framework: terrorism + separatism + extremism — formal foundational Three-Evils framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Three-Evils framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal SCO Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure RATS framework (SCO Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure RATS established 2004 in Tashkent Uzbekistan providing formal SCO counter-terrorism framework — formal foundational SCO counter-terrorism framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional anti-terrorist structure framework with operational counter-terrorism database framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal SCO Membership Expansion framework (India + Pakistan joined SCO 9 June 2017 at Astana Summit + Iran joined SCO 4 July 2023 at Tehran Summit + Belarus joined SCO 4 July 2024 at Astana Summit expanding SCO from original 6 to 10 member states with population coverage framework expanding from ~1.5 billion to ~3.4 billion framework — formal foundational SCO expansion framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal three-stage-expansion framework from 6 founding to 8 with India-Pakistan to 9 with Iran to 10 with Belarus framework). The SCO Charter is binding upon 10 SCO member states + 2 SCO observer states + 14 SCO dialogue partners as of 2026 (Belarus + China + India + Iran + Kazakhstan + Kyrgyzstan + Pakistan + Russia + Tajikistan + Uzbekistan) — comprehensive Eurasian regional coverage framework. The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal SCO Three Evils framework operates limited intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal Eurasian-regional security framework not directly addressing family-law-issues framework. The SCO Heads of State Council + SCO Heads of Government Council + SCO Secretariat + SCO RATS Executive Committee are the formal SCO bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
