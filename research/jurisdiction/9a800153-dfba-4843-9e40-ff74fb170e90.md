# Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic (Naxçıvan Muxtar Respublikası / Нахичеванская Автономная Республика)

The Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic (Azerbaijani: Naxçıvan Muxtar Respublikası / Russian: Нахичеванская Автономная Республика Nakhichevanskaya Avtonomnaya Respublika) is a Caucasian civil-law constituent autonomous republic of the Republic of Azerbaijan — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Azerbaijani sub-national territory not contiguous with main Azerbaijan (~5,500 km² Azerbaijani exclave separated from main Azerbaijan by the Republic of Armenia ~30 km wide Zangezur corridor — formal Azerbaijani exclave framework, distinct from rest-of-Azerbaijani-sub-national territorial framework, requiring formal cross-border transit through Iran or Turkey for surface connection to main Azerbaijan), as the only modern autonomous republic established by international-treaty framework rather than internal-constitutional framework (Treaty of Moscow 16 March 1921 + Treaty of Kars 13 October 1921 between Soviet Russia + Turkey + South Caucasus Soviet republics — formal Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic framework established by Soviet-Turkish-mediated treaty framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-autonomous-republic frameworks established via internal-constitutional framework, with formal Turkish guarantee of Nakhchivan autonomy framework continuous 1921-present 104+ years), as the central jurisdiction of the most distinctive modern Azerbaijani-Iranian land-border framework (Nakhchivan-Iran border ~179 km — Azerbaijan's only formal land border with Iran, structurally distinctive globally as the only formal Azerbaijani land border with Iran), and as one of only two modern Turkic-speaking exclave-frameworks globally (alongside Russian Kaliningrad Oblast exclave-framework — distinct framework). Nakhchivan territory historically operated under Persian + Arab Caliphate + Atabegs of Azerbaijan + Safavid Persian + Qajar Persian frameworks + Treaty of Turkmenchay 1828 Russian-Persian transfer + Russian Empire 1828-1917 + Soviet Nakhchivan ASSR 1924-1990 + Soviet Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic 1990-1991 + Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic 1991+. Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic comprises ~5,500 km² with a population of ~460,000 (~99% Azerbaijani Turkic-speakers). Family-law framework operates under the federal Azerbaijani civil-law framework (Azerbaijani Family Code 1999 + Azerbaijani Civil Code 1999) applied uniformly across Azerbaijani sub-national territories. The Supreme Court of Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of Azerbaijan. Nakhchivan is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Azerbaijan is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 22 January 2020 effective 1 April 2020) — Nakhchivan is governed by Azerbaijani Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
