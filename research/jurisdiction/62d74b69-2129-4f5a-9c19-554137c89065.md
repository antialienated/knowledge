# Poland (Rzeczpospolita Polska)

Poland is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (sąd rejonowy — wydział rodzinny i nieletnich) operate under the Kodeks rodzinny i opiekuńczy (KRO) of 1964 and the Kodeks postępowania cywilnego (KPC). The statutory framework is silent on the 'alienacja rodzicielska' label as a diagnostic category. The Sąd Najwyższy has not endorsed PAS as a syndrome but applies dobro dziecka (welfare-of-the-child) standard under KRO art. 56. The Rzecznik Praw Dziecka (Children's Rights Ombudsperson) and the Komitet Ochrony Praw Dziecka have expressed institutional caution toward PAS-syndromic framing. Poland's 2020 attempted withdrawal from the Istanbul Convention (subsequently retained under Tusk Government from December 2023) reflects political-context tension affecting DV-protection framework intersection with custody disputes. The Hague Abduction Convention (ratified 1992) operates with EU 2019/1111 Brussels IIb regulation framework. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
