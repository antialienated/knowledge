# Republika Srpska (Република Српска / Republic of Srpska)

Republika Srpska (Cyrillic: Република Српска / English: Republic of Srpska) is a Balkan civil-law constitutionally-recognised entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina (one of two constitutionally-recognised entities under the Dayton Accord 1995 framework: Republika Srpska + Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, with Brčko District operating as condominium third entity) — structurally distinctive globally as the only sub-state-level entity constituted via a formal post-war Western-mediated peace agreement (Dayton Accord 14 December 1995 — formal peace agreement ending Bosnian War 1992-1995 between Bosnia and Herzegovina + Croatia + Federal Republic of Yugoslavia, mediated by US Secretary of State Richard Holbrooke + signed Paris 14 December 1995, formally annexed as Annex IV of the Dayton Accord Bosnia and Herzegovina Constitution — only modern sub-state-level entity globally with constitution annexed to a peace agreement), as the only modern sub-state-level entity with formal pre-Dayton-Accord-1995 war-period self-government framework (Republika Srpska self-government framework 9 January 1992 - 14 December 1995 — 3-year 11-month armed-conflict-period self-government framework, contested by Bosnia and Herzegovina + international community as unilateral framework prior to Dayton Accord formalisation), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most internationally-scrutinised modern post-Cold-War European internal armed conflicts (Bosnian War 1992-1995 with ~100,000 deaths + Srebrenica genocide 11-22 July 1995 with ~8,372 Bosniak male deaths — only formal genocide on European soil since WWII recognised by International Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia ICTY 2001 + International Court of Justice 2007 + UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/77/77 2024), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most contested modern sub-state-level secessionist frameworks (Republika Srpska 2008+ secessionist contestation framework + 2024 secession referendum threats framework — ongoing constitutional contestation framework between Republika Srpska government + Bosnia and Herzegovina federal framework). Republika Srpska territory historically operated under Roman + Byzantine + Ottoman 1463-1878 + Austro-Hungarian 1878-1918 + Kingdom of Yugoslavia 1918-1941 + WWII Independent State of Croatia 1941-1945 + Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia 1945-1992 + Bosnian War period 1992-1995 + Dayton Accord 1995+ frameworks. Republika Srpska comprises ~24,857 km² with a population of ~1.15 million. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Bosnia and Herzegovina civil-law framework + Republika Srpska-specific Family Law of Republika Srpska 2002 framework. The Supreme Court of Republika Srpska is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction is contested between Constitutional Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina + Republika Srpska courts. Republika Srpska is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Bosnia and Herzegovina is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 23 August 1991 effective 1 December 1991 via succession from SFRY) — Republika Srpska is governed by Bosnia and Herzegovina Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
