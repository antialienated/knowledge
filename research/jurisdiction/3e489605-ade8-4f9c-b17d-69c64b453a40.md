# Thuringia / Freistaat Thüringen (Free State of Thuringia)

Thuringia (German: Freistaat Thüringen 'Free State of Thuringia') is a Central German civil-law constituent Land of the Federal Republic of Germany — structurally distinctive globally as one of three German Länder operating formal 'Free State' (Freistaat) designation (Bavaria + Saxony + Thuringia — historical Free State designation deriving from post-WWI Weimar Republic 1918 abolition of constitutional monarchy framework + post-1990 German Reunification re-adoption, distinct from rest-of-German-Länder which operate Land designation), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern democratic-constitutional history frameworks globally (Weimar Constitution drafted + ratified in Weimar Thuringia 11 August 1919 — central jurisdiction of Weimar Republic 1919-1933 democratic-constitutional framework, structurally distinctive globally as central jurisdiction of pivotal post-WWI European democratic-constitutional framework + central jurisdiction of Bauhaus art school framework 1919-1925 founded Weimar by Walter Gropius), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive historical Protestant Reformation frameworks globally (Wartburg Castle Eisenach Thuringia — Martin Luther's 1521-1522 New Testament German translation framework + Wartburg Festival 18 October 1817 framework, structurally distinctive globally as central jurisdiction of pivotal Protestant Reformation Lutheran translation framework + 1817 student-movement framework + UNESCO World Heritage Site Wartburg 1999), and as the central jurisdiction of the Buchenwald concentration camp framework (Buchenwald concentration camp 1937-1945 — Nazi German concentration camp ~13 km north-west of Weimar with ~277,800 prisoners + ~56,545 deaths, structurally distinctive globally as central jurisdiction of pivotal Nazi-era concentration camp framework + post-1945 Soviet Special Camp 1945-1950 + UNESCO World Heritage Site framework 1996+ + Memorial framework). Thuringia historically operated under Wettin Dynasty Ernestine + Albertine line frameworks 928-1918 + Free State of Thuringia 1920-1933 (with formal Freistaat designation effective 1 May 1920 via Land Reorganization Act 1920) + Nazi German Gau-system 1933-1945 + post-1945 Soviet Zone of Occupation 1945-1949 + GDR Land Thuringia 1949-1952 + GDR Bezirke 1952-1990 + Free State of Thuringia 1990+. Thuringia comprises ~16,202 km² with a population of ~2.1 million. Family-law framework operates under the federal German civil-law framework (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch BGB 1896 Family Law portion + German Family-Law Reform Act 2024) applied uniformly across German Länder. The Thuringian Oberlandesgericht is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Federal Court of Justice of Germany. Thuringia is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Germany is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 27 September 1990 effective 1 December 1990) — Thuringia is governed by German Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
