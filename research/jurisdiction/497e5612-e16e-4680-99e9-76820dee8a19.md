# SAARC Charter 1985 (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation)

The Charter of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-SAARC-treaty framework adopted by the 7 founding South Asian states in Dhaka — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern South Asian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal South-Asia framework + India-Pakistan-bilateral-tension-paralysed framework (SAARC Charter signed 8 December 1985 in Dhaka Bangladesh by Bangladesh + Bhutan + India + Maldives + Nepal + Pakistan + Sri Lanka + effective 8 December 1985 + Afghanistan admitted 3 April 2007 — formal foundational SAARC treaty framework establishing comprehensive SAARC organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate as functional regional integration frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern South Asian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal South-Asia framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal India-Pakistan-bilateral-tension-paralysed framework with no SAARC Summit held since 2014 Kathmandu Summit — 11-year paralysis framework as of 2026 + most-populous regional supranational binding-treaty framework with ~1.95 billion population coverage framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal SAARC Convention on Suppression of Terrorism 1987 framework (SAARC Regional Convention on Suppression of Terrorism adopted 4 November 1987 in Kathmandu Nepal + effective 22 August 1988 + Additional Protocol adopted 6 January 2004 in Islamabad Pakistan — formal foundational South-Asia-regional counter-terrorism framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern South Asian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal counter-terrorism framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal SAARC SAFTA South Asian Free Trade Area framework (SAARC Agreement on South Asian Free Trade Area SAFTA signed 6 January 2004 in Islamabad Pakistan + effective 1 January 2006 providing formal South-Asia-regional free-trade framework — formal foundational South-Asia-regional free-trade framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern South Asian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional free-trade framework operating effectively-non-operational due to bilateral-tensions framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal SAARC Convention on Trafficking in Women and Children 2002 framework (SAARC Convention on Preventing and Combating Trafficking in Women and Children for Prostitution signed 5 January 2002 in Kathmandu Nepal + effective 15 November 2005 — formal foundational South-Asia-regional women + children trafficking framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern South Asian regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional women + children trafficking framework with PA-intersection framework via cross-border-South-Asian children-trafficking-and-parental-alienation framework). The SAARC Charter is binding upon 8 SAARC member states + 9 SAARC observer states as of 2026 (Afghanistan post-Taliban participation framework uncertain framework + SAARC paralysis framework since 2014) — comprehensive South Asia regional coverage framework. The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal SAARC Convention on Trafficking in Women and Children 2002 framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal South-Asia-regional cross-border-children's-rights framework. The SAARC Summit + SAARC Council of Ministers + SAARC Standing Committee + SAARC Secretariat are the formal SAARC bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
