# Republic of Bashkortostan (Республика Башкортостан / Башҡортостан Республикаһы)

The Republic of Bashkortostan (Russian: Республика Башкортостан Respublika Bashkortostan / Bashkir: Башҡортостан Республикаһы Başqortostan Respublikahı) is a Volga-Ural Russian civil-law constituent republic of the Russian Federation — structurally distinctive globally as the largest Russian sub-national republic by population (~4.0 million — largest of all 21 Russian republics by population), as the first Russian sub-national territory to formally establish autonomous status within Russian post-Imperial framework (Bashkir Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic established 20 March 1919 — first Soviet ASSR established in Russia + first formal post-Imperial autonomous sub-national framework, established 9 months prior to next-established Soviet republic Karelo-Finnish ASSR 8 June 1920 + Tatar ASSR 27 May 1920), as the central jurisdiction of historical Bashkir Confederation framework (Bashkir Confederation pre-13th century - 1740s + Bashkir uprisings 1681-1684 + 1704-1711 + 1735-1740 + 1755 + 1773-1774 Pugachev Uprising — only modern Russian sub-national republic with formal continuous Bashkir-uprising framework against Russian Imperial framework), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most economically distinctive modern Russian sub-national oil-refining frameworks (Bashneft + Ufa refineries cluster — third-largest Russian oil-refining-capacity sub-national jurisdiction). Bashkortostan historically operated under Bashkir Confederation pre-13th century - 1740s + Golden Horde 1242-1438 + Kazan Khanate 1438-1552 + Russian Empire 1557-1917 + Bashkir Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic 1919-1990 + Republic of Bashkortostan 1990+ (with formal sovereignty declaration 11 October 1990). Bashkortostan comprises ~142,947 km² with a population of ~4.0 million (~30% Bashkir + ~36% Russian + ~25% Tatar + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Russian civil-law framework (Russian Family Code 1995 + Russian Civil Code 1994) applied uniformly across Russian sub-national territories. The Supreme Court of Bashkortostan is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation. Bashkortostan is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Russia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 28 July 2011 effective 1 October 2011) — Bashkortostan is governed by Russian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
