# UNESCO Constitution 1945 (UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization)

The Constitution of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO Constitution) is a global supranational meta-framework education-science-culture binding-treaty instrument adopted by the London Conference for the Establishment of an Educational and Cultural Organization — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal multilateral binding-treaty framework operating specifically on education + science + culture framework (UNESCO Constitution adopted 16 November 1945 in London + effective 4 November 1946 — formal universal multilateral binding-treaty framework establishing comprehensive UNESCO organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate on substantive treaties on either education or science or culture separately, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal triple-mandate education-science-culture framework + Preamble framework providing 'since wars begin in the minds of men, it is in the minds of men that the defences of peace must be constructed' framework + second UN specialized agency after ILO 1946 framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal 1972 World Heritage Convention framework (Convention concerning the Protection of the World Cultural and Natural Heritage adopted 16 November 1972 + effective 17 December 1975 providing formal World Heritage List + World Heritage in Danger List framework — formal foundational world-heritage framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal world-heritage framework with 1,223 World Heritage sites + 56 World Heritage in Danger sites as of 2026), as the central jurisdiction of the formal 2003 Intangible Cultural Heritage Convention framework (Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage adopted 17 October 2003 + effective 20 April 2006 providing formal Intangible Cultural Heritage Lists framework — formal foundational intangible-cultural-heritage framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal intangible-cultural-heritage framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal 1960 Convention against Discrimination in Education framework (Convention against Discrimination in Education adopted 14 December 1960 + effective 22 May 1962 providing formal education non-discrimination framework — formal foundational education-non-discrimination framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal education-non-discrimination framework). The UNESCO Constitution is binding upon 194 UNESCO member states + 12 associate members as of 2026 — comprehensive universal coverage framework. The Constitution is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal 1960 Convention against Discrimination in Education + Education for All EFA framework operate substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border educational-access-for-children-of-alienated-parent framework where parental-alienator-school-enrolment-blocking cases occur. The UNESCO General Conference + UNESCO Executive Board + UNESCO Director-General + UNESCO World Heritage Committee are the formal UNESCO bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
