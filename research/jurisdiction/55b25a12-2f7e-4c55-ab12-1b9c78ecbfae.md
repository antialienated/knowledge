# Autonomous Region of Bougainville (AROB)

The Autonomous Region of Bougainville (AROB), comprising Bougainville Island + Buka Island + outlying islands, is an Oceanian Melanesian mixed common-law/Adat-customary-law autonomous region of Papua New Guinea — structurally distinctive globally as the world's most decisive independence referendum result in modern history (97.7% YES for independence in Bougainville Independence Referendum 23 November - 7 December 2019, ~181,067 YES out of ~185,272 valid votes — highest YES-percentage of any modern independence referendum globally, ahead of 2020 Bougainville-equivalent jurisdictions), as the only Papua New Guinea autonomous region established via formal peace-agreement framework derived from internal armed conflict resolution (Bougainville Peace Agreement 30 August 2001 ending the Bougainville Civil War 1988-1998 between Bougainville Revolutionary Army BRA and Papua New Guinea Defence Force PNGDF with ~15,000-20,000 deaths — proportionally one of the deadliest modern Oceanian internal armed conflicts), as the central jurisdiction of the most economically distinctive modern Oceanian internal armed conflict catalysed by Rio Tinto-operated Panguna Copper Mine framework (Panguna Mine operated 1972-1989 — closed 1989 due to BRA insurgency, representing ~17% of Papua New Guinea export revenue and ~44% of Papua New Guinea export revenue at peak, never reopened post-Civil War), and as the only modern independence-bound jurisdiction subject to formal post-referendum constitutional implementation framework (post-2019 referendum + 2021 Bougainville-PNG Joint Consultation framework + 2027 target independence transition framework — Papua New Guinea Parliament 2027-target ratification framework). Bougainville comprises ~9,318 km² with a population of ~300,000. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Papua New Guinea common-law framework + Bougainville-specific Customary Court Adat-customary-law family-law framework for indigenous Melanesian communities. The Bougainville High Court is the apex regional court; the National Court of Papua New Guinea + Supreme Court of Papua New Guinea provide intermediate + final appellate jurisdiction. Bougainville is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Papua New Guinea is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Bougainville is governed by the PNG non-ratification status. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
