# NATO Treaty 1949 (North Atlantic Treaty / Washington Treaty)

The North Atlantic Treaty (NATO Treaty or Washington Treaty) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-NATO-treaty framework adopted by the 12 founding North Atlantic states in Washington — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern transatlantic regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal collective-defence-only framework via Article 5 (NATO Treaty signed 4 April 1949 in Washington DC by Belgium + Canada + Denmark + France + Iceland + Italy + Luxembourg + Netherlands + Norway + Portugal + UK + US + effective 24 August 1949 — formal foundational NATO treaty framework establishing comprehensive NATO organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate on economic-integration frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern transatlantic regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal collective-defence-only framework via Article 5 framework + oldest modern collective-defence supranational binding-treaty framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Article 5 collective-defence framework invoked once after September 11 attacks framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Sweden + Finland 2023-2024 post-Russia-invasion-of-Ukraine accession framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Article 5 Collective Defence framework (NATO Treaty Article 5 framework providing 'an armed attack against one or more of them in Europe or North America shall be considered an attack against them all' + 'each of them, in exercise of the right of individual or collective self-defence recognised by Article 51 of the Charter of the United Nations, will assist the Party or Parties so attacked' — formal foundational Article 5 collective-defence framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Article 5 collective-defence framework with operational invocation framework via 12 September 2001 invocation after September 11 attacks framework + only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal collective-defence-invocation framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal NATO Membership Expansion framework (NATO expansion via Greece + Turkey 1952 + West Germany 1955 + Spain 1982 + Czech Republic + Hungary + Poland 1999 + Bulgaria + Estonia + Latvia + Lithuania + Romania + Slovakia + Slovenia 2004 + Albania + Croatia 2009 + Montenegro 2017 + North Macedonia 2020 + Finland 4 April 2023 + Sweden 7 March 2024 expanding NATO from 12 founding to 32 current members — formal foundational NATO expansion framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Sweden + Finland 2023-2024 post-Russia-invasion-of-Ukraine accession framework ending Sweden + Finland historic neutrality framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal NATO 2% GDP Defence Spending Pledge framework (NATO 2014 Wales Summit Defence Investment Pledge committing NATO members to 2% GDP defence spending by 2024 + NATO 2025 Hague Summit 5% GDP defence spending pledge by 2035 — formal foundational NATO defence-spending-pledge framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal defence-spending-pledge framework with operational monitoring framework). The NATO Treaty is binding upon 32 NATO member states as of 2026 — comprehensive transatlantic regional coverage framework. The Treaty is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal NATO framework operates limited intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal transatlantic-military-family-cross-border framework.
