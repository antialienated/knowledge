# OSCE Helsinki Final Act 1975 + OSCE Charter (Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe)

The OSCE Helsinki Final Act is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-OSCE-instrument framework adopted by the Conference on Security and Co-operation in Europe in Helsinki — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Euro-Atlantic regional supranational framework operating formal Helsinki-Decalogue 10 principles framework + bridging-Cold-War framework via East-West cooperation framework (Helsinki Final Act signed 1 August 1975 in Helsinki Finland by 35 states + CSCE Paris Charter signed 21 November 1990 + Conference on Security and Co-operation in Europe CSCE transformed to Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe OSCE 1 January 1995 — formal foundational OSCE instrument framework operating non-treaty political-commitments framework establishing 10 Helsinki Decalogue principles framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate as formal binding-treaty frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Euro-Atlantic regional supranational framework operating non-treaty political-commitments framework + only modern Cold-War-bridging regional supranational framework + only modern regional supranational framework spanning Vancouver-to-Vladivostok geographic framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Helsinki Decalogue 10 Principles framework (Helsinki Final Act 10 Decalogue principles framework: sovereign equality + refraining from threat or use of force + inviolability of frontiers + territorial integrity + peaceful settlement of disputes + non-intervention in internal affairs + respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms including freedom of thought conscience religion or belief + equal rights and self-determination of peoples + cooperation among states + fulfilment in good faith of obligations under international law — formal foundational Helsinki Decalogue framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational framework operating formal Helsinki-Decalogue 10 principles framework with formal human-dimension + political-military-dimension + economic-environmental-dimension formal three-baskets framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OSCE Office for Democratic Institutions and Human Rights ODIHR framework (OSCE ODIHR established 1990 + based in Warsaw Poland providing formal Euro-Atlantic election-observation + human-rights monitoring framework — formal foundational Euro-Atlantic election-observation framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational framework operating formal election-observation framework with operational mandate framework via OSCE election-observation missions framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal OSCE Russia Suspension 2022-2024 framework (Russia withdrew from OSCE Parliamentary Assembly 2022 + Russia's chairmanship application blocked at 2022 Lodz Ministerial Council + OSCE Russia funding-share dispute 2022-2024 + Russia partial-suspension framework over invasion of Ukraine — formal foundational OSCE Russia-suspension framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational framework operating formal partial-Russia-suspension framework). The OSCE Helsinki Final Act is operating upon 57 OSCE participating states as of 2026 — comprehensive Vancouver-to-Vladivostok Euro-Atlantic coverage framework (largest regional security organisation globally framework). The Helsinki Final Act is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Helsinki Decalogue Principle VII human-rights framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border-Euro-Atlantic children's-rights framework + OSCE Human Dimension framework.
