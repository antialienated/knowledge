# African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child (ACRWC / Charter)

The African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child (ACRWC / Charter) is a Sub-Saharan African supranational meta-framework children's-rights instrument adopted by the Organisation of African Unity (OAU) — structurally distinctive globally as one of only two regional children's-rights binding treaty frameworks globally (alongside the European Convention on the Exercise of Children's Rights 1996 — distinct framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only African regional binding children's-rights treaty framework), as the only modern regional children's-rights framework operating both binding-treaty framework + dedicated monitoring-body framework (African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child ACERWC established under Charter Article 32 — distinct from UNCRC's Committee on the Rights of the Child UN framework + EU's no-dedicated-monitoring-body framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal African post-colonial children's-rights elaboration framework (ACRWC adopted 11 July 1990 by 26th Assembly of Heads of State and Government of the OAU in Addis Ababa + effective 29 November 1999 — second regional children's-rights instrument in the world after UNCRC 1989 + first regional African children's-rights instrument framework + formal African-specific children's-rights framework distinguishing from UNCRC 1989 in formal African-context-specific provisions including Article 31 Responsibilities of the Child Towards Family + Society framework, distinct from UNCRC operating universal-children's-rights framework without formal Responsibilities-of-the-Child framework), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern regional best-interests-of-the-child frameworks (Charter Article 4 Best Interests of the Child framework + Article 9 Freedom of Expression + Article 19 Parent Care and Protection + Article 20 Parental Responsibilities + Article 25 Separation from Parents framework — formal African-specific best-interests-of-the-child + parental-responsibilities framework, distinct from UNCRC Article 3 Best Interests of the Child framework). The ACRWC is binding upon all 49 of 55 African Union member states that have ratified the Charter as of 2026 (excluding Democratic Republic of the Congo + Equatorial Guinea + Tunisia + Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic + South Sudan + Somalia). The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though Article 4 Best Interests of the Child + Article 19 Parent Care and Protection + Article 20 Parental Responsibilities frameworks are applicable. The African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child (ACERWC) is the apex monitoring body; the African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights is the apex judicial body for state-party violations.
