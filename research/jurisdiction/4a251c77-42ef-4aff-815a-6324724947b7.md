# Qatar (دولة قطر)

Qatar is a Gulf mixed-legal-system absolute monarchy whose family-law framework operates under Family Law 22/2006 (Qanun al-Usra) drawn from Hanbali jurisprudence with provisions from other Sunni schools. Custody (hadana) and guardianship (wilaya) are governed by arts. 162-200 of the Family Law. The Court of Cassation (Mahkamat al-Tamyiz) is the apex court for civil and criminal matters; family-law matters are heard within the Family Courts. The Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) Civil and Commercial Court operates a common-law jurisdiction for commercial matters. Psychology profession is regulated under the Ministry of Public Health (MOPH) through the Department of Healthcare Professions Registration (DHP). Qatar is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label; courts operate substantively under the welfare-of-the-child standard. Qatar acceded to the Hague Convention 1980 on Child Abduction effective 1 March 2014. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
