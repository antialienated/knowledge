# Kenya (Republic of Kenya / Jamhuri ya Kenya)

Kenya is a mixed common-law jurisdiction with constitutional supremacy under the Constitution of Kenya 2010. Family courts operate under the Children Act 2022 (No. 29 of 2022, effective 26 July 2022, replacing the 2001 Children Act), the Marriage Act 2014, and Kadhi courts for Muslim personal-status matters under Constitution art. 170. The Constitution art. 53 establishes constitutionally-grounded child-rights framework including best-interests-of-the-child paramount standard. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court of Kenya and Court of Appeal have applied BIC standard in custody disputes. The Children Act 2022 modernises framework including parental responsibility, child-participation rights, and child-protection-measures. Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR) and Kenya Children's Department have informed institutional position. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
