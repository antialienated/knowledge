# OAS Charter 1948 (Charter of the Organization of American States)

The Charter of the Organization of American States (OAS Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-OAS-treaty framework adopted by the Ninth International Conference of American States in Bogotá — structurally distinctive globally as the oldest modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on Americas regional framework (OAS Charter signed 30 April 1948 in Bogotá Colombia + effective 13 December 1951 — formal foundational OAS treaty framework establishing comprehensive OAS organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-treaty frameworks which operate as subsequent regional treaties, structurally distinctive globally as the oldest modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework + first regional organisation established under UN Charter Chapter VIII framework as regional arrangement under UN Charter framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OAS Inter-American Human Rights System framework (OAS Charter Article 106 framework + 1948 American Declaration of the Rights and Duties of Man + 1969 American Convention on Human Rights Pact of San José + Inter-American Commission on Human Rights IACHR + Inter-American Court of Human Rights IACtHR framework — formal foundational Americas-regional-human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal two-tier Commission + Court Americas-regional-human-rights framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OAS Democratic Charter framework (Inter-American Democratic Charter adopted 11 September 2001 in Lima Peru providing 'The peoples of the Americas have a right to democracy and their governments have an obligation to promote and defend it' — formal foundational Americas-regional-democratic-governance framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional democratic-governance framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal Pact of Bogotá American Treaty on Pacific Settlement framework (American Treaty on Pacific Settlement Pact of Bogotá signed 30 April 1948 in Bogotá Colombia + effective 6 May 1949 providing formal pacific-settlement framework — formal foundational Americas-regional-pacific-settlement framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal regional pacific-settlement framework). The OAS Charter is binding upon 35 OAS member states + 73 OAS permanent observers as of 2026 — comprehensive Americas regional coverage framework (Cuba suspended since 2009 but reinstated as member). The Charter is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Inter-American Human Rights System framework operates substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border-children's-rights framework underlying Inter-American Convention on the International Return of Children 1989. The OAS General Assembly + OAS Permanent Council + OAS Secretary General + IACHR + IACtHR are the formal OAS bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
