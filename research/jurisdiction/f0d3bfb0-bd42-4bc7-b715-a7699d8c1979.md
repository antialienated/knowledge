# Northern Territory (NT / Northern Territory of Australia)

The Northern Territory (NT / Northern Territory of Australia) is a Northern Australian common-law constituent self-governing territory of the Commonwealth of Australia — structurally distinctive globally as the first modern jurisdiction worldwide to formally legalise voluntary euthanasia via primary legislation (Rights of the Terminally Ill Act 1995 (NT) effective 1 July 1996 — first formal voluntary-euthanasia legislation worldwide, applied to 4 cases prior to Commonwealth Euthanasia Laws Act 1997 override that nullified NT legislation 25 March 1997, establishing template framework for subsequent global voluntary-euthanasia legislation including Oregon Death with Dignity Act 1997 + Netherlands Termination of Life on Request Act 2002 + Belgium 2002 + Luxembourg 2009 + Colombia 2015 + Canada 2016 + Australia state-by-state 2017-2023 + New Zealand 2020 + Spain 2021 + Austria 2022 + Portugal 2023 + Ecuador 2024), as the only Australian sub-national jurisdiction with Aboriginal-majority demographic framework (~33% Aboriginal + ~62% non-Aboriginal — distinct from rest-of-Australian-states + territories which operate non-Aboriginal-majority frameworks), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern Australian Commonwealth-state-level interventions (Northern Territory Intervention 'Closing the Gap' framework 2007-2022 — Howard Government Commonwealth response to Little Children are Sacred Report 15 June 2007 + formal suspension of Racial Discrimination Act 1975 (Cth) provisions over 73 Aboriginal communities + ~$680 million Commonwealth direct-administration framework, gradually replaced by 2022 Aboriginal-Self-Determination framework), and as one of the only modern Australian sub-national jurisdictions formally operating under limited self-government framework (Northern Territory (Self-Government) Act 1978 (Cth) effective 1 July 1978 — limited self-government framework with Commonwealth retained powers over Aboriginal Affairs + Uranium Mining + National Parks, distinct from Australian state framework operating full state-government powers, also distinct from Australian Capital Territory ACT framework operating different limited self-government framework under Australian Capital Territory (Self-Government) Act 1988). Northern Territory historically operated under Aboriginal frameworks ~60,000 years + British exploration 1606 + South Australian Crown Land 1863-1911 + Northern Territory Commonwealth direct administration 1911-1978 + Northern Territory limited self-government 1978+. Northern Territory comprises ~1,419,630 km² with a population of ~250,000. Family-law framework operates under the federal Australian common-law framework (Family Law Act 1975 (Cth)) applied uniformly across Australian sub-national territories. The Supreme Court of the Northern Territory is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the High Court of Australia. Northern Territory is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Australia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 29 October 1986 effective 1 January 1987) — Northern Territory is governed by Australian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
