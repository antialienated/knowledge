# Italy (Repubblica Italiana)

Italy is a civil-law jurisdiction whose family courts (Tribunale per i minorenni and Tribunale ordinario sezione famiglia) operate under the Codice civile arts. 315-bis to 337-octies (responsabilità genitoriale) and the Codice di procedura civile. The Corte di cassazione Sezione I civile in Cass. civ. ord. n. 13217/2021 and ord. n. 9691/2022 expressly characterised PAS as pseudoscientific ('priva di base scientifica') and excluded its use as a diagnostic category in custody proceedings. This places Italy firmly in the institutional-critique camp, alongside Germany (BVerfG 2023) and Spain (CGPJ guides). The Consiglio Superiore di Sanità has not validated PAS as a clinical diagnosis.
