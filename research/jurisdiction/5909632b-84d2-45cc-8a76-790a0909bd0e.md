# World Bank IBRD Articles of Agreement 1944 (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development)

The Articles of Agreement of the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (World Bank IBRD Articles of Agreement) and the World Bank Group is a global supranational meta-framework foundational-World-Bank-Group-treaty framework adopted by the 44 founding states at Bretton Woods — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal international-development-financing framework + foundational Bretton Woods framework alongside IMF framework + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal World-Bank-Group-five-institutions framework (World Bank IBRD Articles of Agreement adopted 22 July 1944 at Bretton Woods Conference in Bretton Woods New Hampshire USA by 44 founding states + effective 27 December 1945 + World Bank operations began 25 June 1946 — formal foundational World Bank IBRD treaty framework establishing comprehensive World Bank organisation framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-treaty frameworks which operate on different substantive areas, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal international-development-financing framework + foundational Bretton Woods framework alongside IMF + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal World-Bank-Group-five-institutions framework via IBRD + IDA + IFC + MIGA + ICSID + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal weighted-voting framework via shareholding-based-voting framework + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal traditionally-American-President framework + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Aiib + NDB China-led-alternative-banks framework parallel framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal World Bank Group Five Institutions framework (World Bank Group framework providing formal five-institutions framework: International Bank for Reconstruction and Development IBRD 1944 + International Development Association IDA 1960 + International Finance Corporation IFC 1956 + Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency MIGA 1988 + International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes ICSID 1966 — formal foundational World-Bank-Group-five-institutions framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal World-Bank-Group-five-institutions framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal ICSID Convention framework (Convention on the Settlement of Investment Disputes between States and Nationals of Other States ICSID Convention signed 18 March 1965 in Washington DC + effective 14 October 1966 providing formal investor-state dispute settlement framework — formal foundational ICSID Convention framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal investor-state-dispute-settlement framework + only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Bolivia + Ecuador + Venezuela withdrawal framework via Bolivia withdrew 2 May 2007 + Ecuador withdrew 7 July 2009 + Venezuela withdrew 24 January 2012 framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal World Bank Climate + SDG financing framework (World Bank Climate + SDG financing framework providing formal Climate Investment Funds CIF + Green Climate Fund GCF + 2025 IDA21 Replenishment USD 100 billion framework — formal foundational World Bank Climate + SDG financing framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern global supranational binding-treaty framework operating formal Climate + SDG financing framework). The World Bank IBRD Articles of Agreement is binding upon 189 World Bank IBRD member states + 174 IDA member states + 186 IFC member states + 182 MIGA member states + 158 ICSID Convention member states as of 2026
