# Hala'ib Triangle (مثلث حلايب)

The Hala'ib Triangle (Arabic: مثلث حلايب Muthallath Ḥalāyib) is a North African mixed disputed-claim territory between Egypt and Sudan on the Red Sea coast — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern doubly-claimed territory between two sovereign states paired with formal terra nullius adjacent territory framework (Hala'ib Triangle ~20,580 km² disputed territory claimed by both Egypt + Sudan, paired with Bir Tawil ~2,060 km² not claimed by either Egypt or Sudan — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern paired claim-vs-non-claim framework between two sovereign states deriving from formal Egypt-Sudan border-line discrepancy 1899 22°N parallel framework vs. 1902 Anglo-Egyptian administrative-line framework), as the central jurisdiction of one of the longest-running modern post-colonial African border-disputes (Hala'ib Triangle dispute 1958-2026 68+ years — continuous Egypt-Sudan diplomatic dispute, with Egypt establishing formal Egyptian administrative-control framework over Hala'ib Triangle 1995+ following Sudan's recognition of Sudanese-Iraqi assassination-attempt on Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak 26 June 1995 in Addis Ababa, Egypt extending de facto administrative-control framework 1995-present), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern formal sovereign-claim+counter-claim-frameworks (Egypt formal claim under 1899 Anglo-Egyptian Sudan Condominium 22°N parallel framework + Sudan formal claim under 1902 Anglo-Egyptian administrative-line framework — both states maintain formal sovereign claim despite Egyptian de facto administrative-control framework 1995-present), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most economically distinctive disputed-territory frameworks (~20,580 km² Red Sea coastal territory with substantial fishing-rights + offshore oil-exploration + tourism + Beja indigenous communities ~60,000 population — distinct economically-substantial-disputed-territory framework). The Hala'ib Triangle territory historically operated under Egyptian Pharaonic Period ~3000 BCE + Roman + Arab Caliphate framework 640 CE + Egyptian + Anglo-Egyptian Sudan Condominium 1899-1956 + Egyptian-Sudanese-shared-administration 1956-1995 + Egyptian de facto administrative-control 1995-present. Hala'ib Triangle comprises ~20,580 km² with a population of ~60,000 (predominantly Beja indigenous communities). Family-law framework operates under Egyptian civil-law framework via Egyptian de facto administrative-control 1995-present (Egyptian Personal Status Law 25/1929 + Egyptian Family Court Law 10/2004) + contested Sudanese Personal Status Law claim. The Court of Appeal of Hala'ib + Aswan Court of First Instance operate under Egyptian framework; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of Egypt + contested Sudanese Supreme Court claim. Hala'ib Triangle is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Egypt is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party + Sudan is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Hala'ib Triangle is governed by Egyptian + Sudanese non-Hague-Convention-1980-ratification framework.
