# Nigeria (Federal Republic of Nigeria)

Nigeria is a federal mixed-jurisdiction with English common-law foundation, customary law operative across ethnic communities, and Islamic Sharia law in the 12 northern states (post-1999 Sharia implementation). Family courts operate under the Matrimonial Causes Act 1970 (federal) for statutory marriages, customary courts for customary marriages, and Sharia courts for Muslim personal-status matters. The Child Rights Act 2003 (federal) implements UN CRC at federal level but requires state-level domestication — adopted by 36 states as of 2026. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court of Nigeria has addressed custody disputes applying welfare-of-the-child standard. The National Human Rights Commission and Federal Ministry of Women Affairs have informed institutional position. Tripartite legal pluralism (common-law + customary law + Sharia law) operationally creates structurally distinctive family-law architecture across Nigeria's federal entities.
