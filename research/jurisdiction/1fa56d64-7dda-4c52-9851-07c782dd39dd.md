# Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam 1990 + OIC Charter Framework

The Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam 1990 (formally Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam adopted by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation OIC) is a Pan-Islamic supranational meta-framework Islamic-context human-rights soft-law instrument adopted by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC — formerly Organisation of the Islamic Conference 1969-2011) — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern supranational meta-framework operating formal Islamic-context human-rights framework (Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam adopted 5 August 1990 by 19th Islamic Conference of Foreign Ministers in Cairo + soft-law framework — formal Islamic-context human-rights framework explicitly based on Islamic Sharia framework via Article 24 + Article 25 framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-human-rights frameworks which operate on secular human-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern supranational human-rights framework with formal foundational Islamic Sharia framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal OIC Pan-Islamic human-rights elaboration framework (Cairo Declaration adopted as Islamic supplement to Universal Declaration of Human Rights 1948 framework — applicable to all 57 OIC member states as soft-law framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Pan-Islamic human-rights soft-law framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Cairo Declaration Article 5 family-rights framework (Cairo Declaration Article 5 framework on family-rights — formal Islamic-context family-rights framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-human-rights frameworks operating without formal Islamic-context family-rights framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Islamic-context family-rights formal framework), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern soft-law-vs-binding-treaty framework debates globally (Cairo Declaration soft-law framework + 2008 OIC Independent Permanent Human Rights Commission IPHRC framework + 2020 Cairo Declaration Update Process framework — formal ongoing OIC framework debate on transitioning from soft-law to binding-treaty framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-human-rights frameworks operating on either binding-treaty framework or soft-law framework without transition framework). The Cairo Declaration on Human Rights in Islam 1990 is binding upon 57 OIC member states as soft-law framework — universal Pan-Islamic coverage framework as soft-law framework. The Declaration is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Article 5 family-rights framework intersects with parental-rights framework. The OIC Independent Permanent Human Rights Commission IPHRC is the formal monitoring framework body; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
