# GCC Charter 1981 (Gulf Cooperation Council)

The Charter of the Cooperation Council for the Arab States of the Gulf (GCC Charter) is a regional supranational meta-framework foundational-GCC-treaty framework adopted by the 6 Gulf Arab monarchies in Abu Dhabi — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Gulf-monarchies regional supranational binding-treaty framework operating on formal Arab-Gulf-monarchies-only framework (GCC Charter
