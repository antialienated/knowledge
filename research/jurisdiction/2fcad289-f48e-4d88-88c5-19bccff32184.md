# Aceh / Nanggroe Aceh Darussalam (نڠكرو اچيه دارالسلام)

Aceh (formally Nanggroe Aceh Darussalam نڠكرو اچيه دارالسلام / Daerah Istimewa Aceh in Indonesian) is a Northwestern Sumatran Indonesian mixed civil-law/Islamic-law special-autonomy province — structurally distinctive globally as the only Indonesian province operating a formal Sharia framework (Qanun Jinayat 2014 Islamic Criminal Code + Mahkamah Syariah Islamic Court framework — Aceh-specific Islamic criminal-law framework including public caning for moral offences distinct from rest-of-Indonesia's secular Pancasila-based legal framework), as the only Indonesian province with formal special-autonomy framework derived from peace-agreement framework (Law on the Governing of Aceh 11/2006 UU 11/2006 implementing the Helsinki Memorandum of Understanding 15 August 2005 ending the Aceh War 1976-2005 between Indonesian Government and Free Aceh Movement Gerakan Aceh Merdeka GAM), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most devastating natural disasters of the 21st century (2004 Indian Ocean earthquake-tsunami 26 December 2004 — ~170,000 deaths in Aceh out of ~230,000 total — most deaths in any single jurisdiction from the tsunami framework), and as a distinctive jurisdiction within Indonesia for the Acehnese-cultural-political-identity-vs-Indonesian-cultural-political-assimilation tension (historic Aceh Sultanate 1496-1903 + Dutch-Aceh War 1873-1914 + 1949 Indonesian incorporation + 1976-2005 GAM insurgency framework). Aceh historically operated the Aceh Sultanate 1496-1903 as a major Indian Ocean Islamic maritime power, was subjected to Dutch-Aceh War 1873-1914 with substantial Acehnese resistance, was incorporated into Indonesia 1949 following Dutch decolonisation, was granted Daerah Istimewa (Special Region) status 1959, suffered the GAM insurgency 1976-2005, achieved the Helsinki MoU peace agreement 15 August 2005 (mediated by Crisis Management Initiative Martti Ahtisaari), and was granted Law on the Governing of Aceh 11/2006 framework. Aceh comprises ~58,377 km² with a population of ~5.4 million. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Indonesian civil-law framework + Aceh-specific Mahkamah Syariah Islamic-court family-law framework — distinct from rest-of-Indonesia. The Mahkamah Syariah (Sharia Courts) operates as primary court for Islamic family-law matters; the Banda Aceh High Court is the apex appellate court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of Indonesia. Aceh is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Indonesia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Aceh is governed by the Indonesian non-ratification status.
