# ASEAN Commission on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children (ACWC)

The ASEAN Commission on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children (ACWC) is a Southeast Asian supranational meta-framework children's-rights + women's-rights instrument adopted by the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional combined children's-rights + women's-rights monitoring-body framework (ASEAN ACWC established 7 April 2010 by 16th ASEAN Summit in Hanoi — formal regional combined children's-rights + women's-rights monitoring-body framework, distinct from ACERWC African children's-rights-only framework + Inter-American Commission on Human Rights universal-framework + UNCRC Committee on the Rights of the Child universal-framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only regional monitoring-body framework with formal combined children's-rights + women's-rights mandate), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Southeast Asian post-2007 children's-rights + women's-rights elaboration framework (ACWC established under ASEAN Charter 2007 + ASEAN Declaration on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children 2004 + Declaration of the Establishment of the ASEAN Commission on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children 2010 — formal ASEAN-specific framework distinguishing from UNCRC + CEDAW universal frameworks in formal regional-soft-law-monitoring-body framework operating without formal binding-treaty framework, distinct from ACRWC binding-treaty framework + Inter-American Convention 1989 binding-treaty framework), as the only modern regional children's-rights + women's-rights framework operating without formal binding-treaty framework (ACWC operates under formal regional-soft-law-monitoring-body framework — distinct from ACRWC + Inter-American Convention 1989 binding-treaty frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern regional children's-rights + women's-rights framework relying primarily on consensus-driven cooperative framework), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern ASEAN-non-interference + consensus-driven cooperative frameworks (ACWC operates under formal ASEAN principles of non-interference + consensus-driven decision-making + soft-law framework — distinct from rest-of-modern-regional-children's-rights frameworks operating under formal binding-treaty + monitoring-body frameworks). The ACWC is binding upon all 10 ASEAN member states: Brunei Darussalam + Cambodia + Indonesia + Laos + Malaysia + Myanmar + Philippines + Singapore + Thailand + Vietnam + Timor-Leste (accession ongoing). The ACWC is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal mandate areas include implementation of UN Convention on the Rights of the Child UNCRC 1989 + UN Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women CEDAW 1979 frameworks. The ACWC is the formal monitoring body framework; the ASEAN Intergovernmental Commission on Human Rights (AICHR) is the broader regional human-rights body.
