# Tibet Autonomous Region (西藏自治区 / བོད་རང་སྐྱོང་ལྗོངས་)

The Tibet Autonomous Region (西藏自治区 Xīzàng Zìzhìqū in Chinese / བོད་རང་སྐྱོང་ལྗོངས་ Bod Rang skyong ljongs in Tibetan) is a Southwestern Chinese civil-law Chinese autonomous-region constituent unit of the People's Republic of China — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern jurisdiction with continuous theocratic Tibetan-Buddhist Dalai Lama framework predating Chinese incorporation (Tibetan-Buddhist Ganden Phodrang government 1642-1959 under Dalai Lama institutional framework — distinct from rest-of-China's secular Han-cultural-political framework), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most contested modern Sino-foreign sovereignty disputes (Tibetan Government-in-Exile བོད་གཞུང་དགའ་ལྡན་ཕོ་བྲང་ Central Tibetan Administration CTA based in Dharamshala India since 1960 — 14th Dalai Lama Tenzin Gyatso framework continuously contesting Chinese sovereignty claims), as the only Chinese autonomous-region subject to formal 1951 17-Point Agreement framework (17-Point Agreement for the Peaceful Liberation of Tibet 23 May 1951 between People's Republic of China + Tibetan Local Government — formal annexation framework, contested by Tibetan Government-in-Exile as signed under duress), and as the largest Chinese autonomous-region by area (~1,228,400 km² — ~12.8% of Chinese land area, fourth-largest Chinese province-level division). Tibet historically operated the Ganden Phodrang government 1642-1959 under the Sho Dynasty Dalai Lama institutional framework, was subjected to Qing protectorate framework 1720-1912, achieved de facto independence 1912-1950 following Qing collapse, was annexed by People's Republic of China via 17-Point Agreement 23 May 1951, suffered the 1959 Tibetan Uprising 10 March 1959 leading to 14th Dalai Lama's exile to India 17 March 1959, and was formally established as Tibet Autonomous Region 9 September 1965. Tibet Autonomous Region comprises ~1,228,400 km² with a population of ~3.6 million. Family-law framework operates under the federal Chinese civil-law framework (Chinese Marriage Law 1980 + Chinese Civil Code 2020 Family Law portion Articles 1040-1118) applied uniformly across Chinese autonomous regions. The Tibet High People's Court is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme People's Court of China. Tibet is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. China is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Tibet is governed by the Chinese non-ratification status. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
