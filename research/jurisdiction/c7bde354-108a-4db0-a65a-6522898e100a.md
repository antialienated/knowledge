# Republic of Kalmykia (Республика Калмыкия / Хальмг Таңһч)

The Republic of Kalmykia (Russian: Республика Калмыкия Respublika Kalmykiya / Kalmyk: Хальмг Таңһч Halmg Tanghch) is a Southwestern Russian civil-law constituent republic of the Russian Federation — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern jurisdiction in Europe with Buddhism as predominant religion (Tibetan Gelug-school Buddhism predominant among Kalmyk Mongol population — ~53% of Kalmykia population is Kalmyk, distinct from rest-of-Europe which operates Christian + secular + Islamic-majority frameworks, structurally distinctive globally as westernmost formal Buddhist jurisdiction worldwide), as the only modern jurisdiction in Europe operating formal Mongolic-language (Oirat-Kalmyk language family) official-administrative framework (Kalmyk language formal official language of Kalmykia under Constitution of Kalmykia 1994 Article 17 alongside Russian — only formal European Mongolic-language sub-national jurisdiction, distinct from rest-of-Europe operating Indo-European + Uralic + Caucasian language families), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Kalmyk Deportation 1943-1957 historical framework (Soviet Operation Ulusy 28 December 1943 — Stalin Soviet government deportation of ~92,000 Kalmyk people to Siberia + Central Asia + Soviet Far East with ~40-50% mortality framework, formally recognised as Soviet genocide by Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic Supreme Soviet on 26 April 1991), and as the central jurisdiction of historical Kalmyk Khanate 1635-1771 framework (Kalmyk Khanate established 1635 by Oirat Mongol migration from Dzungaria + Kalmyk Khanate dissolution 1771 via Mass Exodus from Volga Steppe back to Dzungaria + remnant Volga Kalmyk framework — only modern jurisdiction globally with continuous Oirat Mongol historical-cultural-political-linguistic framework since 1635). Kalmykia historically operated under Kalmyk Khanate 1635-1771 + Russian Empire 1771-1917 + Soviet Kalmyk Autonomous Oblast 1920-1935 + Soviet Kalmyk ASSR 1935-1943 + Soviet Deportation framework 1943-1957 (with formal abolition of Kalmyk ASSR 27 December 1943 by Stalin) + restored Kalmyk ASSR 1957-1992 + Republic of Kalmykia 1992+. Kalmykia comprises ~74,731 km² with a population of ~270,000. Family-law framework operates under the federal Russian civil-law framework (Russian Family Code 1995 + Russian Civil Code 1994) applied uniformly across Russian sub-national territories. The Supreme Court of Kalmykia is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation. Kalmykia is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Russia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 28 July 2011 effective 1 October 2011) — Kalmykia is governed by Russian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
