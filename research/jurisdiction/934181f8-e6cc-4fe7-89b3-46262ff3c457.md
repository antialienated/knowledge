# Guadeloupe (Région et Département de la Guadeloupe / French DOM-ROM)

Guadeloupe is a French Département d'Outre-Mer / Région d'Outre-Mer (DOM-ROM) operating French civil-law family-law framework under Code civil arts. 371-1 to 387ter (autorité parentale). Family courts operate under the Loi du 27 juin 2018 instituant le juge aux affaires familiales (JAF) framework with French civil-law procedural framework via Tribunal Judiciaire de Pointe-à-Pitre and Tribunal Judiciaire de Basse-Terre. The statutory framework is silent on the 'aliénation parentale' label. The Cour d'appel de Basse-Terre and Cour de cassation develop intérêt supérieur de l'enfant jurisprudence within French DOM framework. EU Brussels IIb framework applies — operationally significant for cross-border family-law adjudication including expatriate-population and metropolitan-France diaspora cases. Substantively significant Caribbean French overseas department framework. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
