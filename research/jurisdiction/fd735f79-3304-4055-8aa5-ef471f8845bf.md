# Peru (Republic of Peru / República del Perú)

Peru is an Andean South American civil-law republic whose family-law framework operates under the Civil Code 1984 (Código Civil, Decreto Legislativo 295) Book III (Family Law), drawing on Spanish civil-law substantive heritage with substantial modernisation. Parental authority (patria potestad) and child custody are governed by Civil Code arts. 418-471, supplemented by the Children and Adolescents Code 2000 (Law 27337). The Supreme Court of Justice (Corte Suprema de Justicia) is the apex court for civil and criminal matters; the Constitutional Court (Tribunal Constitucional) operates separate constitutional review. Family-law matters are heard at first instance in the Family Courts (Juzgados de Familia). Psychology profession is regulated through the Colegio de Psicólogos del Perú (Law 23019/1980). Peru is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label; courts operate substantively under the superior-interest-of-the-child standard codified in Children and Adolescents Code art. IX.
