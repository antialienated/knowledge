# Ghana (Republic of Ghana)

Ghana is a common-law jurisdiction operating tripartite-pluralism family-law framework (English common-law, customary law, Islamic personal-status) under the Constitution 1992. Family courts operate under the Matrimonial Causes Act 1971 (Act 367) for statutory marriages, customary courts for customary marriages, and Marriage of Mohammedans Ordinance for Muslim marriages. The Children's Act 1998 (Act 560) provides comprehensive child-rights framework implementing UN CRC, African Charter on Rights and Welfare of the Child. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court of Ghana has applied welfare paramount standard under Children's Act s. 2. The Commission on Human Rights and Administrative Justice (CHRAJ) and Department of Social Welfare have informed institutional position. Ghana is the first African country to ratify UN CRC (29 January 1990). Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
