# UN Convention against Corruption (UNCAC 2003)

The UN Convention against Corruption (UNCAC) is a global supranational meta-framework anti-corruption binding-treaty instrument adopted by the UN General Assembly — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal multilateral binding-treaty framework operating specifically on anti-corruption framework (UNCAC adopted 31 October 2003 by UN General Assembly Resolution 58/4 + opened for signature 9 December 2003 in Merida Mexico + effective 14 December 2005 — formal universal multilateral binding-treaty framework establishing comprehensive anti-corruption framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-international-anti-corruption frameworks which operate as regional + sub-national treaties, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating on formal-anti-corruption-specific framework + first major UN anti-corruption treaty framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Chapter V Asset Recovery Framework (UNCAC Chapter V Articles 51-59 framework providing 'The return of assets pursuant to this chapter is a fundamental principle of this Convention, and States Parties shall afford one another the widest measure of cooperation and assistance in this regard' — formal foundational asset-recovery framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal asset-recovery framework), as the central jurisdiction of the formal Chapter IV International Cooperation Framework (UNCAC Chapter IV Articles 43-50 framework providing formal extradition + mutual-legal-assistance + transfer-of-sentenced-persons + joint-investigations + special-investigative-techniques framework — formal foundational anti-corruption international-cooperation framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal anti-corruption international-cooperation framework), and as the central jurisdiction of the formal UNCAC Implementation Review Mechanism IRM (UNCAC IRM operational from 2010 via UNCAC Conference of States Parties COSP Resolution 3/1 — formal foundational anti-corruption peer-review-mechanism framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern universal binding-treaty framework operating formal anti-corruption peer-review-mechanism framework). The UNCAC is binding upon 190 state parties as of 2026 — substantial universal coverage framework. The Convention is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label, though formal Chapter V asset-recovery framework + Chapter IV international-cooperation frameworks operate substantial intersection with parental-alienation framework via formal cross-border child-support-enforcement-corruption + judicial-corruption-in-family-court framework. The UN Office on Drugs and Crime UNODC + UNCAC Conference of States Parties COSP + UNCAC Implementation Review Group IRG are the formal anti-corruption framework bodies; national-level apex courts operate as judicial bodies.
