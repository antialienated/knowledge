# India (Republic of India / भारत गणराज्य)

India is a federal common-law jurisdiction with personal-status legal pluralism — Hindu Marriage Act 1955, Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act 1956, Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act 1956 (HMGA) governing Hindu families; Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act 1937 governing Muslims; Indian Christian Marriage Act 1872, Divorce Act 1869 governing Christians; Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act 1936 governing Parsis; and the secular Special Marriage Act 1954, Guardians and Wards Act 1890 (GWA) available across communities. The Constitution of India 1950 establishes constitutional framework. The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label. The Supreme Court of India in Yashita Sahu v State of Rajasthan (2020) and Tejaswini Gaud v Shekhar Tewari (2019) has applied welfare paramount standard. India ratified UN CRC 1992. India is NOT a Hague Convention 1980 signatory. Parental-alienation recognition status: [object Object].
