# Brčko District (Brčko Distrikt / Брчко Дистрикт)

The Brčko District of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Bosnian: Brčko Distrikt / Cyrillic: Брчко Дистрикт) is a Balkan civil-law constitutionally-recognised condominium third entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina (operating alongside Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina + Republika Srpska entities) — structurally distinctive globally as the only sub-state-level entity in the world constituted via formal international-tribunal arbitration award (Brčko Arbitration Tribunal Final Award 5 March 1999 + Annex of the Final Award 18 August 1999 + International Supervisor framework — distinct from rest-of-sub-state-level entities globally where constitutional establishment derives from peace agreements, constitutional reforms, or referendums), as the only sub-state-level entity operating as a formal condominium of two larger entities (Brčko District operates simultaneously as territory of both Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina AND Republika Srpska — neither entity has exclusive jurisdiction, formally constituted as Inter-Entity Boundary Line condominium framework, distinct from rest-of-sub-state-level entities globally), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern post-armed-conflict reconciliation frameworks (Brčko Final Award + International Supervisor + post-Bosnian-War demilitarisation framework — Brčko was the most strategically contested town during Bosnian War 1992-1995 + ICTY-recognised site of war crimes, formal reconciliation framework via Brčko Statute 8 March 2000), and as the smallest formally-administered Bosnia and Herzegovina entity by area (~493 km² — smaller than rest-of-BiH-entities Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina ~26,110 km² + Republika Srpska ~24,857 km²). The Brčko District territory historically operated under Ottoman 1463-1878 + Austro-Hungarian 1878-1918 + Kingdom of Yugoslavia 1918-1941 + WWII Independent State of Croatia 1941-1945 + Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia 1945-1992 + Bosnian War period 1992-1995 + Dayton Accord 1995 contested-status framework + Brčko Arbitration Tribunal Final Award 1999 + Brčko Statute 2000+ frameworks. Brčko District comprises ~493 km² with a population of ~92,000 (~42% Bosniak + ~35% Serb + ~21% Croat + others). Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Bosnia and Herzegovina civil-law framework + Brčko District-specific Family Law of Brčko District 2007 framework. The Court of Brčko District is the primary trial + apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Appellate Court of Brčko District + Constitutional Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Brčko District is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Bosnia and Herzegovina is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 23 August 1991 effective 1 December 1991 via succession from SFRY) — Brčko District is governed by Bosnia and Herzegovina Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
