# Kaliningrad Oblast (Калининградская область / Königsberg-Kaliningrad)

Kaliningrad Oblast (Russian: Калининградская область Kaliningradskaya oblast — historically Königsberg until 1946) is a Baltic Russian civil-law constituent oblast of the Russian Federation — structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Russian sub-national territory not contiguous with main Russia (~15,100 km² Russian exclave on the Baltic Sea between Poland to the south + Lithuania to the north + east — formal Russian exclave framework, distinct from rest-of-Russian-sub-national-territorial framework, separated from main Russia by Lithuania ~360 km + Belarus framework via Suwałki Gap framework or via Belarus-Russia surface-connection framework), as the only modern Russian sub-national territory transferred to USSR via Allied WWII post-war territorial-settlement framework (Potsdam Agreement 17 July - 2 August 1945 + Potsdam Final Communiqué 2 August 1945 — formal Allied WWII territorial transfer framework, structurally distinctive globally as the only modern Russian sub-national territory established via formal Allied WWII post-war territorial-settlement framework), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern ethnic-cleansing-and-resettlement frameworks (Soviet expulsion of ~1.1 million Königsberg + East Prussian German civilian population 1945-1948 + Soviet resettlement of ~430,000 Russian + Belarusian + Ukrainian civilian framework 1947-1948 — formal post-WWII population-transfer framework, distinct from rest-of-modern-territorial-frameworks), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern Western-NATO-encircled Russian sub-national frameworks (Kaliningrad Oblast surrounded by Polish + Lithuanian NATO member states since Polish 1999 + Lithuanian 2004 NATO accession — only modern Russian sub-national territory fully surrounded by NATO member states + Baltic Sea, distinct from rest-of-Russian-sub-national territorial framework), and as the only modern Russian sub-national territory operating formal Königsberg-Kaliningrad-Königsberg historical-naming-framework (city renamed Kaliningrad 4 July 1946 from Königsberg — only modern Russian sub-national territory with formal foundational name-change framework establishing distinct Soviet-Russian identity framework). Kaliningrad Oblast historically operated under Old Prussian + Teutonic Order 13th-15th centuries + Duchy of Prussia 1525-1701 + Kingdom of Prussia 1701-1871 + German Empire 1871-1918 + Weimar Republic 1918-1933 + Nazi Germany 1933-1945 + Soviet Königsberg Special District 1945-1946 + Kaliningrad Oblast 1946-1990 + Russian Federation Kaliningrad Oblast 1991+. Kaliningrad Oblast comprises ~15,100 km² with a population of ~1.0 million (~86% Russian + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Russian civil-law framework (Russian Family Code 1995 + Russian Civil Code 1994) applied uniformly across Russian sub-national territories. The Kaliningrad Regional Court is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation. Kaliningrad is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Russia is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 28 July 2011 effective 1 October 2011) — Kaliningrad is governed by Russian Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
