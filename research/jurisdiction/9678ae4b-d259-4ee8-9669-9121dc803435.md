# Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Federacija Bosne i Hercegovine / FBiH)

The Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Bosnian/Croatian: Federacija Bosne i Hercegovine / FBiH) is a Balkan civil-law constitutionally-recognised entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina (one of two constitutionally-recognised entities under the Dayton Accord 1995 framework: Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina + Republika Srpska, with Brčko District operating as condominium third entity) — structurally distinctive globally as the only sub-state-level entity constituted via formal pre-Dayton-Accord-1995 Washington Agreement framework (Washington Agreement 18 March 1994 — formal peace agreement between Bosnia and Herzegovina + Republic of Croatia + Bosnian Croats ending Croat-Bosniak War 1992-1994 within Bosnian War 1992-1995 + establishing Bosniak-Croat federation framework, distinct from rest-of-Bosnia-and-Herzegovina entities where Dayton Accord 1995 is sole establishing framework), as the only sub-state-level entity operating formal dual-ethnic-constituent-peoples governance framework (formal constitutional framework requiring Bosniak + Croat + Other constituent-peoples representation at executive + legislative + judicial branches — distinct from rest-of-sub-state-level entities operating mono-ethnic governance frameworks), as the only sub-state-level entity operating formal 10-cantons sub-sub-territorial framework (Federation comprises 10 cantons each with own constitution + government + parliament + judiciary — Una-Sana + Posavina + Tuzla + Zenica-Doboj + Bosnian Podrinje + Central Bosnia + Herzegovina-Neretva + West Herzegovina + Sarajevo + Canton 10, distinct from rest-of-sub-state-level entities operating unitary or simple sub-territorial frameworks), and as the central jurisdiction of one of the most distinctive modern post-Cold-War European federalisation frameworks (1994-1995 Bosniak-Croat federation framework + 1995 Dayton Accord federalisation framework + 2002 Constituent Peoples Decision Constitutional Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina Article U-5/98 — formalising Bosniak + Croat + Serb constituent-peoples framework across Federation + Republika Srpska entities). The Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina territory historically operated under Ottoman 1463-1878 + Austro-Hungarian 1878-1918 + Kingdom of Yugoslavia 1918-1941 + WWII Independent State of Croatia 1941-1945 + Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia 1945-1992 + Bosnian War period 1992-1995 + Washington Agreement 1994+ frameworks. Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina comprises ~26,110 km² with a population of ~2.2 million. Family-law framework operates under a dual federal Bosnia and Herzegovina civil-law framework + Federation-specific Family Law of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina 2005 framework. The Supreme Court of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Constitutional Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. Bosnia and Herzegovina is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory and party (acceded 23 August 1991 effective 1 December 1991 via succession from SFRY) — Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina is governed by Bosnia and Herzegovina Hague-Convention-1980 framework.
