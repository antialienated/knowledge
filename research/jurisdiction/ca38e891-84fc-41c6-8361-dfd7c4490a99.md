# Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region (新疆维吾尔自治区 / شىنجاڭ ئۇيغۇر ئاپتونوم رايونى)

The Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region (新疆维吾尔自治区 Xīnjiāng Wéiwú'ěr Zìzhìqū in Chinese / شىنجاڭ ئۇيغۇر ئاپتونوم رايونى Shinjang Uyghur Aptonom Rayoni in Uyghur) is a Northwestern Chinese civil-law Chinese autonomous-region constituent unit of the People's Republic of China — structurally distinctive globally as the largest Chinese province-level division by area (~1,664,897 km² — ~17.3% of Chinese land area, larger than any Western European country individually and equivalent to Iran in area), as the central jurisdiction of one of the most internationally-scrutinised modern Chinese sub-national human-rights contexts (2017+ Xinjiang Vocational Education and Training Centers framework — characterised by Chinese Government as counter-terrorism + counter-extremism framework, characterised by Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights 2022 Assessment Report 31 August 2022 as possible crimes against humanity framework, multiple UN Special Procedures + UN Treaty Body findings of systematic ethnic-based human rights violations), as the only Chinese autonomous-region established on the basis of Turkic-Muslim Uyghur ethnic-identity framework (~11 million Uyghur Turkic-Muslim population — distinct from rest-of-China's Han-cultural-political framework), and as the central jurisdiction of contested East Turkestan independence framework (East Turkestan Government-in-Exile based in Washington DC since 2004 contesting Chinese sovereignty claims via cultural-political historical framework — 1933-1934 First East Turkestan Republic + 1944-1949 Second East Turkestan Republic precedent frameworks). Xinjiang historically operated under multiple Turkic Khaganate frameworks 6th-13th centuries + Chagatai Khanate framework 1227-1680 + Dzungar Khanate 1635-1755 + Qing protectorate 1759-1912, was contested 1912-1949 between Republic of China + Soviet Union + Turkic-Muslim self-government frameworks (1933-1934 First East Turkestan Republic + 1944-1949 Second East Turkestan Republic), was incorporated into People's Republic of China 1949, and was formally established as Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region 1 October 1955. Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region comprises ~1,664,897 km² with a population of ~25.8 million (~11.6 million Uyghur + ~10.9 million Han + ~1.6 million Kazakh + others). Family-law framework operates under the federal Chinese civil-law framework (Chinese Marriage Law 1980 + Chinese Civil Code 2020 Family Law portion Articles 1040-1118) applied uniformly across Chinese autonomous regions. The Xinjiang High People's Court is the apex regional court; final appellate jurisdiction lies with the Supreme People's Court of China. Xinjiang is silent on 'parental alienation' as a statutory label. China is a Hague Convention 1980 signatory but not a party — Xinjiang is governed by the Chinese non-ratification status.
