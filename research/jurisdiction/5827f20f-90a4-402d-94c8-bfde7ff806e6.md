# South Africa (Republic of South Africa)

South Africa is a mixed-jurisdiction (Roman-Dutch civil-law, English common-law overlay) with constitutional supremacy under the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa 1996. Family courts operate under the Children's Act 38 of 2005 (effective 1 April 2010) and the Divorce Act 70 of 1979. The Constitutional Court has applied the best standard under Constitution s 28(2). The statutory framework is silent on the 'parental alienation' label as a diagnostic category. The Family Advocate's Office (under Mediation in Certain Divorce Matters Act 24 of 1987) provides expert-mediation function with institutional caution toward PAS-syndromic framing. The South African Law Reform Commission and Health Professions Council of South Africa have informed institutional-critique register against PAS-syndromic framing — particularly in DV-intersection cases under the Domestic Violence Act 116 of 1998.
