# Williamson v. Williamson, 2016 BCCA 87

Williamson v Williamson 2016 BCCA 87 is the canonical Canadian appellate citation for residential-reunification-programme intervention in PA-adjacent custody-modification fact-patterns. The decision engages Family Reflections Reunification Program (Kathleen Reay, BC) — the only Canadian-based residential reunification programme regularly named in Canadian custody jurisprudence — alongside US extra-regulator programmes (Family Bridges Warshak + Overcoming Barriers AFCC). The decision operates at provincial-Court-of-Appeal level under BC Family Law Act 2011 + federal Divorce Act framework. Together with L.A.G. v. D.K.B. 2009 CanLII 6452 (ON SC) (canonical Ontario PA decision) Williamson v Williamson establishes the Canadian appellate jurisprudence on PA-adjacent custody-modification + reunification-programme intervention.
