# BGB § 1697a — Kindeswohlprinzip (verbatim)

# BGB § 1697a — Kindeswohlprinzip (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Germany · **Citation:** *Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch* § 1697a · **Authoritative source:** [gesetze-im-internet.de — § 1697a BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__1697a.html)

The **welfare-principle meta-provision** in German family law. § 1697a BGB establishes the residual welfare standard the *Familiengericht* applies to decisions in parental-responsibility matters where no specific provision otherwise governs the standard. The provision codifies the *Kindeswohlprinzip* — the welfare-of-the-child principle — that operates as the substantive constant throughout the German family-law framework.

§ 1697a is doctrinally significant in PA litigation because it establishes that even where no specific welfare-protective remedy is being engaged (e.g. not § 1666 Kindeswohlgefährdung; not § 1671 Alleinsorge transfer), the court's decision must still be the one that *best corresponds* to the welfare of the child. It is the structural German parallel to UK [Children Act 1989 s. 1](../united-kingdom/children-act-1989-section-1.md) welfare paramountcy.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### § 1697a BGB — Kindeswohlprinzip

> **(1)** Soweit nichts anderes bestimmt ist, trifft das Gericht in Verfahren über die in diesem Titel geregelten Angelegenheiten diejenige Entscheidung, die unter Berücksichtigung der tatsächlichen Gegebenheiten und Möglichkeiten sowie der berechtigten Interessen der Beteiligten dem Wohl des Kindes am besten entspricht.
>
> **(2)** Lebt das Kind in Familienpflege, so hat das Gericht, soweit nichts anderes bestimmt ist, in Verfahren über die in diesem Titel geregelten Angelegenheiten auch zu berücksichtigen, ob und inwieweit sich innerhalb eines im Hinblick auf die Entwicklung des Kindes vertretbaren Zeitraums die Erziehungsverhältnisse bei den Eltern derart verbessert haben, dass diese das Kind selbst erziehen können. Liegen die Voraussetzungen des § 1632 Absatz 4 Satz 2 Nummer 1 vor, so hat das Gericht bei seiner Entscheidung auch das Bedürfnis des Kindes nach kontinuierlichen und stabilen Lebensverhältnissen zu berücksichtigen. Die Sätze 1 und 2 gelten entsprechend, wenn das Kind im Rahmen einer Hilfe nach § 34 oder 35a Absatz 2 Nummer 4 des Achten Buches Sozialgesetzbuch erzogen und betreut wird.

## English working translation

### § 1697a BGB — Welfare-of-the-child principle

> **(1)** In so far as nothing else is provided, the court, in proceedings on matters regulated in this Title, makes the decision which — taking into account the actual circumstances and possibilities and the legitimate interests of the parties — best corresponds to the welfare of the child.
>
> **(2)** Where the child lives in family foster care, the court — in so far as nothing else is provided — must also consider, in proceedings on matters regulated in this Title, whether and to what extent the parental-care conditions have improved within a period reasonable having regard to the development of the child, such that the parents can themselves bring up the child. Where the conditions of § 1632 paragraph 4 sentence 2 number 1 are present, the court must also consider in its decision the child's need for continuous and stable living conditions. Sentences 1 and 2 apply accordingly where the child is being brought up and cared for within the framework of assistance under § 34 or § 35a paragraph 2 number 4 of the Eighth Book of the Social Code.

## Why this matters for PA

- **Soweit nichts anderes bestimmt ist — the residual clause.** *In so far as nothing else is provided.* § 1697a operates as the *residual* welfare provision — it applies where no specific provision otherwise governs the welfare standard. In PA-pattern cases, specific provisions (§§ 1666, 1671, 1684, etc.) typically govern; but where the court is making decisions outside those frameworks, § 1697a provides the structural welfare anchor.
- **Three operative considerations under (1).** The provision frames the court's analysis around three factors:
  - *Die tatsächlichen Gegebenheiten und Möglichkeiten* — the actual circumstances and possibilities
  - *Die berechtigten Interessen der Beteiligten* — the legitimate interests of the parties
  - *Dem Wohl des Kindes am besten entspricht* — best corresponding to the welfare of the child
- The third factor is decisive (*am besten entspricht* — best corresponds); the first two are constraints on how that determination is reached.
- **Subsection (2) — Familienpflege framework.** Where the child is in foster care, the court must consider:
  - Whether parental-care conditions have improved *innerhalb eines vertretbaren Zeitraums* (within a reasonable period) such that the parents can bring up the child themselves
  - Where [§ 1632 (4) Satz 2 Nr. 1](bgb-1632-verbleibensanordnung-verbatim.md) conditions are met, the child's need for *kontinuierliche und stabile Lebensverhältnisse* (continuous and stable living conditions) — the *Kontinuitätsprinzip*
- The Kontinuitätsprinzip is doctrinally distinctive: it codifies the recognition that change disrupts welfare even where the underlying parental-care conditions improve. This is doctrinally significant in long-running PA cases where the child has been in foster care for an extended period.
- **The interaction with § 1632 (4) Dauerverbleib.** Where § 1632 (4) Satz 2 Nr. 1 conditions (parental-care conditions have not sustainably improved despite offered support) are met, the court must additionally consider the Kontinuitätsprinzip under § 1697a (2). This is the structural protection of long-term foster placements against late-stage parental rehabilitation applications.
- **The structural parallel with UK CA 1989 s. 1.** § 1697a (1) operates as the German functional parallel to UK [CA 1989 s. 1 welfare paramountcy](../united-kingdom/children-act-1989-section-1.md).
