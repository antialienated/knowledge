# Forældreansvarsloven §§ 4 + 11 + 21 — Barnets ret + fælles forældremyndighed + samvær (verbatim)

# Forældreansvarsloven §§ 4 + 11 + 21 — Barnets ret + fælles forældremyndighed + samvær (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Denmark · **Citation:** *Forældreansvarsloven* (LBK nr 1768 af 30/11/2020) §§ 4, 11, 21 · **Authoritative source:** [retsinformation.dk — Forældreansvarsloven](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2020/1768)

The **three structural pillars** of Danish family law. Forældreansvarsloven (Parental Responsibility Act 2007, consolidated 2020) establishes (i) the *barnets ret* (child's-rights) framework at § 4, (ii) the *fælles forældremyndighed* (joint parental responsibility) default at § 11, and (iii) the *samvær* (contact) framework at § 21. The provisions operate together with the 2019 *Familieretshuset* reform — the unified front-door for all separation/custody matters.

Denmark is doctrinally distinctive among the Nordic jurisdictions because Forældreansvarsloven framings contact as the *child's* right (not the parent's right) and codifies the resident parent's duty to facilitate contact. The provisions parallel:

- [Iceland barnalög 2003 (forsjá)](../iceland/barnalog-2003-parental-responsibility.md)
- [Finland Lapsenhuoltolaki (custody + access)](../finland/lapsenhuoltolaki-1983-2019-reform.md)

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### § 4 — Barnets bedste

> Afgørelser efter loven skal træffes ud fra, hvad der er bedst for barnet.

### § 11 — Fælles forældremyndighed

> Var der fælles forældremyndighed ved separation eller skilsmisse, fortsætter denne. Familieretten kan dog efter anmodning ophæve den fælles forældremyndighed, hvis der er holdepunkter for at antage, at forældrene ikke kan samarbejde om barnets forhold til barnets bedste.

### § 21 — Samvær

> **Stk. 1.** Barnets forbindelse med begge forældre søges bevaret ved, at barnet har ret til samvær med den forælder, som det ikke har bopæl hos.
>
> **Stk. 2.** Forældrene har et fælles ansvar for, at barnet har samvær med den forælder, det ikke har bopæl hos.
>
> **Stk. 3.** Familieretshuset eller familieretten kan efter anmodning fastsætte omfanget og udøvelsen af samværet og kan fastsætte de nødvendige bestemmelser i forbindelse hermed. Afgørelsen træffes efter barnets bedste, og der skal blandt andet tages hensyn til
>
> 1) barnets forhold til begge forældre,
>
> 2) barnets egne synspunkter alt efter alder og modenhed,
>
> 3) barnets tryghed og trivsel, og
>
> 4) forældrenes evne til at samarbejde om barnets forhold.

## English working translation

### § 4 — The best interests of the child

> Decisions under the Act shall be made on the basis of what is *best for the child* (*bedst for barnet*).

### § 11 — Joint parental responsibility

> Where there was joint parental responsibility (*fælles forældremyndighed*) at separation or divorce, this *continues*. The Family Court may, however, upon application, *terminate* the joint parental responsibility where there are *holdepunkter* (substantial grounds) for assuming that the parents cannot cooperate on the child's affairs to the child's best interests.

### § 21 — Contact

> **(1)** The child's *connection with both parents* (*forbindelse med begge forældre*) is sought to be preserved by the child having a *right to contact* (*ret til samvær*) with the parent with whom the child does not have its *residence* (*bopæl*).
>
> **(2)** The parents have a *joint responsibility* (*fælles ansvar*) that the child has contact with the parent with whom the child does not have its residence.
>
> **(3)** The *Familieretshuset* (Family Law House) or the Family Court may, upon application, fix the *scope and exercise* of contact and may make the necessary provisions in connection therewith. The decision is made according to the child's best interests, and *regard shall be had* among other things to:
>
> 1) the *child's relationship* with both parents,
>
> 2) the child's *own views* according to age and maturity,
>
> 3) the child's *security and well-being* (*tryghed og trivsel*),
>
> 4) the parents' *capacity to cooperate* (*evne til at samarbejde*) on the child's affairs.

## Why this matters for PA

- **The barnets bedste paramountcy (§ 4).** *Afgørelser efter loven skal træffes ud fra, hvad der er bedst for barnet.* Decisions are made on what is *best for the child*. This is the structural Danish parallel to other Nordic and DACH paramountcy provisions, but doctrinally significant for its simplicity — § 4 establishes the unqualified primacy of the welfare standard without elaboration, leaving operational content to §§ 11, 19, 21 and the jurisprudence.
- **The fælles forældremyndighed default (§ 11).** *Var der fælles forældremyndighed ved separation eller skilsmisse, fortsætter denne.* Joint parental responsibility continues after separation by default. The Familieretten may only *ophæve* (terminate) joint responsibility where there are *holdepunkter* (substantial grounds) that the parents cannot cooperate *to the child's best interests*. The *til barnets bedste* qualifier is critical — sustained alienating conduct can satisfy the substantial-grounds test, because cooperation failure that *serves alienation* operates against the child's interests.
- **The child's right framing (§ 21 (1)).** *Barnet har ret til samvær.* The child *has a right to contact* — not the parent. This is doctrinally distinctive in Nordic family law and operationally powerful in PA-pattern cases:
  - Contact-denial cannot be justified solely on the alienating parent's preference
  - The targeted parent's application is structurally a vindication of the child's right
  - The alienating parent's framing of contact as the child's choice is rebuttable — the right exists irrespective of the child's expressed preference
