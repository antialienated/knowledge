# Lithuania Civilinis Kodeksas Book Three §§ 3.156 · 3.159 · 3.165 · 3.168 · 3.170 · 3.175 · 3.176 — Parental Rights and Contact (Verbatim)

# Lithuania Civilinis Kodeksas Book Three (Family Law) — Parental Rights and Contact (Verbatim)

## TL;DR

Lithuania's **Civilinis kodeksas** (Civil Code, Law No. VIII-1864 of 18 July 2000, in force 1 July 2001) integrates all family law into a unified civil code as **Book Three — Trecioji knyga: Seimos teise**. Sections XI–XII (arts. 3.155 – 3.183) govern the substance of parental authority (**tevu valdzia**) and the child's contact rights. Four provisions carry the operational anti-alienation weight: **§ 3.159** (parents exercise rights jointly and by mutual agreement); **§ 3.165(3)** (a parent who lives apart retains all rights and duties in respect of the child); **§ 3.170** (right of the separated parent to associate with the child AND the residential parent's duty not to obstruct that association); and **§ 3.175 – 3.176** (the court fixes the contact order in the child's interests where parents cannot agree, and may vary it on changed circumstances). The statutory scheme is complemented by CPK (Code of Civil Procedure) art. 771 which authorises coercive enforcement fines (bauda) for non-compliance with contact orders. Lithuanian text quoted below is the consolidated version (aktuali redakcija) at e-seimas.lrs.lt; English rendering is a working translation for research use only — the Lithuanian original controls.

## Statute — Verbatim Text

### § 3.156 CK — Lygios tevu teises ir pareigos vaikams (Equality of parental rights and duties)

**Lithuanian (consolidated text):**

> 1. Tevo ir motinos teises ir pareigos savo vaikams yra lygios.
>
> 2. Tevai turi lygias teises ir lygias pareigas savo vaikams, nesvarbu, gime vaikas susituokusiems ar nesusituokusiems tevams, jiems santuoka nutraukus, teismui pripazinus ja negaliojancia ar tevams gyvenant skyrium.

**English (working translation):**

> 1. The rights and duties of the father and the mother in respect of their children are equal.
>
> 2. Parents have equal rights and equal duties in respect of their children irrespective of whether the child was born to married or unmarried parents, after the dissolution or annulment of the marriage, or where the parents live separately.

**Operational note:** Marital status and cohabitation status are irrelevant to the *substance* of parental rights. The statute forecloses the "primary parent" default at the level of doctrine; equality is the starting point in every case, including post-separation.

### § 3.159 CK — Tevu valdzios lygiateisiskumas ir bendras jos igyvendinimas (Equality and joint exercise of parental authority)

**Lithuanian:**

> 1. Tevai turi lygias teises ir lygias pareigas vaikams, nors santuoka nutraukta, pripazinta negaliojancia ar jie gyvena skyrium.
>
> 2. Tevai savo valdzia igyvendina bendru sutarimu. Kai tarp tevu nera sutarimo, gincytina klausima sprendzia teismas.

**English:**

> 1. Parents have equal rights and equal duties in respect of the children even where the marriage has been dissolved, declared void or where the parents live separately.
>
> 2. Parents shall exercise their authority by mutual agreement. Where the parents cannot agree, the disputed question shall be resolved by the court.

**Operational note:** The default mode of exercise is *joint*. There is no requirement that the parents share a household — separation does not switch the regime from joint to sole; only a court order can. This is the Lithuanian analogue of Estonia's Perekonnaseadus § 116(2) and Malta's Civil Code art. 143A(1), and it sits at the top of any argument that unilateral relocation, unilateral schooling changes, or unilateral gate-keeping of contact is *ultra vires* the acting parent's authority.

### § 3.165 CK — Asmeninių tevu teisiu ir pareigu turinys (Content of the personal rights and duties of parents)

**Lithuanian (extracts):**

> 1. Tevai turi teise ir pareiga dorai aukleti ir prizureti savo vaikus, rupintis ju sveikata, isilaikymu ir islaikymu, atsizvelgdami i ju fizine ir protinę bukle sudaryti palankias sąlygas visapusiskai ir harmoningai vaikų raidai, kad vaikas butų parengtas savarankiskam gyvenimui visuomeneje.
>
> 2. Tevai, atlikdami sias pareigas, turi pirmumo teise pries kitus asmenis.
>
> 3. Kai vaikas gyvena skyrium nuo vieno is tevu, sis tevas islaiko visas teises ir pareigas savo vaikui, isskyrus atvejus, kai teismas nusprendzia kitaip.

**English:**

> 1. Parents have the right and the duty to raise their children with honesty, to look after their health, welfare and maintenance, and, having regard to the children's physical and mental capacity, to create favourable conditions for the child's full and harmonious development so that the child is prepared for independent life in society.
>
> 2. In the discharge of these duties, parents shall have precedence over all other persons.
>
> 3. Where the child lives separately from one of the parents, that parent retains all the rights and duties in respect of the child, save where the court decides otherwise.

**Operational note:** § 3.165(3) is the statutory anchor: **living apart does not diminish rights and duties.** A parent who has been excluded from the residence has, by force of statute, retained the full complement of parental authority; the residential parent has no legal power to *substitute* physical separation for a modification of authority. Any *de facto* stripping of rights that has not been ordered by a court is contrary to § 3.165(3).

### § 3.168 CK — Vaiko atskirimo nuo tevu vengimas (Avoidance of separation of the child from the parents)

**Lithuanian:**

> Vaiko atskirimas nuo tevu galimas tik istatymu numatytais pagrindais ir tvarka. Toks atskirimas negali buti savavaliskas.

**English:**

> Separation of the child from the parents shall be permitted only on the grounds and under the procedure established by law. Such separation may not be arbitrary.
