# Codice Civile, art. 337-quater — Affidamento a un solo genitore (verbatim)

# Codice Civile, art. 337-quater — Affidamento a un solo genitore (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Italy · **Citation:** *Codice Civile*, art. 337-quater · **Authoritative source:** [brocardi.it — art. 337-quater](https://www.brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-primo/titolo-ix/capo-ii/art337quater.html)

The **sole-custody provision** in Italian family law — the principal exception to the shared-custody (*affidamento condiviso*) default established by [art. 337-ter CC](codice-civile-art-337-ter-provvedimenti-riguardo-ai-figli.md). Art. 337-quater operates as the operational route by which a court allocates exclusive *affidamento* to one parent where shared custody is contrary to the child's interest — including, in alienating-conduct cases, where the alienating parent's conduct establishes the welfare-incompatibility threshold.

The provision has a distinctive structural feature: the second paragraph includes an express **anti-weaponisation sanction** — where an application for sole custody is *manifestamente infondata* (manifestly unfounded), the court may take that into account in adverse measures against the applying parent.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### Art. 337-quater — Affidamento a un solo genitore e opposizione all'affidamento condiviso

> Il giudice può disporre l'affidamento dei figli ad uno solo dei genitori qualora ritenga con provvedimento motivato che l'affidamento all'altro sia contrario all'interesse del minore.
>
> Ciascuno dei genitori può, in qualsiasi momento, chiedere l'affidamento esclusivo quando sussistono le condizioni indicate al primo comma. Il giudice, se accoglie la domanda, dispone l'affidamento esclusivo al genitore istante, facendo salvi, per quanto possibile, i diritti del minore previsti dal primo comma dell'articolo 337-ter. Se la domanda risulta manifestamente infondata, il giudice può considerare il comportamento del genitore istante ai fini della determinazione dei provvedimenti da adottare nell'interesse dei figli, rimanendo ferma l'applicazione dell'articolo 96 del codice di procedura civile.
>
> Il genitore cui sono affidati i figli in via esclusiva, salva diversa disposizione del giudice, ha l'esercizio esclusivo della responsabilità genitoriale su di essi; egli deve attenersi alle condizioni determinate dal giudice. Salvo che non sia diversamente stabilito, le decisioni di maggiore interesse per i figli sono adottate da entrambi i genitori. Il genitore cui i figli non sono affidati ha il diritto ed il dovere di vigilare sulla loro istruzione ed educazione e può ricorrere al giudice quando ritenga che siano state assunte decisioni pregiudizievoli al loro interesse.

## English working translation

### Art. 337-quater — Sole custody to one parent and opposition to shared custody

> The judge may order sole custody (*affidamento*) to only one of the parents where he or she considers, by reasoned order, that custody to the other parent would be contrary to the child's interest.
>
> Each parent may, at any time, request sole custody where the conditions indicated in the first paragraph subsist. The judge, if granting the application, orders sole custody to the applying parent, preserving so far as possible the rights of the child set out in the first paragraph of article 337-ter. **If the application proves manifestly unfounded, the judge may consider the conduct of the applying parent for the purposes of determining the measures to be adopted in the child's interest, without prejudice to the application of article 96 of the Code of Civil Procedure.**
>
> The parent to whom sole custody is granted, unless the judge orders otherwise, has the exclusive exercise of *responsabilità genitoriale* over the children; he or she must comply with the conditions determined by the judge. Unless otherwise established, decisions on matters of greater interest for the children are taken by both parents. The parent to whom the children are not entrusted has the right and duty to oversee their instruction and education, and may apply to the judge where he or she considers that decisions prejudicial to the children's interest have been taken.

## Why this matters for PA

- **The default is shared custody; art. 337-quater is the exception.** Italian law since the 2006 reform operates on a shared-custody (*affidamento condiviso*) default established by [art. 337-ter CC](codice-civile-art-337-ter-provvedimenti-riguardo-ai-figli.md). Art. 337-quater is the operational route by which the default is displaced — but only on the basis of a *reasoned order* finding that shared custody is contrary to the child's interest.
- **The second-paragraph anti-weaponisation sanction.** The second paragraph contains an express provision against *manifestamente infondata* applications. Where a parent applies for sole custody on grounds the court determines to be manifestly unfounded — for example, false or exaggerated allegations against the other parent — the court may take the *conduct of the applying parent* into account in determining custody and other measures. This is doctrinally significant for PA litigation: it provides express statutory authority for treating false-allegation tactics as evidence against the applying parent.
- **Art. 96 CPC cross-reference.** *Rimanendo ferma l'applicazione dell'articolo 96 del codice di procedura civile* — the express reference to art. 96 CPC (responsibility for procedural abuse, including punitive damages for vexatious litigation) extends the sanction to costs and damages. A parent advancing manifestly unfounded sole-custody applications can be ordered to compensate the other parent under the procedural-abuse framework.
- **Operational use in sustained-PA cases.** In the most severe sustained-alienation cases, art. 337-quater is the route to allocating exclusive custody to the *targeted* (non-alienating) parent.
