# South Korea Civil Act arts. 837 · 837-2 · 909 · 909-2 · 912 · 913 · 924 · 924-2 — Chinkwon and Right of Visitation (Verbatim)

# South Korea Civil Act — Chinkwon (Parental Authority) and Right of Visitation (Verbatim)

## TL;DR

South Korea governs family law primarily under the **Civil Act** (**민법 / Minbeop**, Law No. 471 of 22 February 1958, in force 1 January 1960, extensively amended). Chapter IV (Parent and Child) of Book IV (Family Relations) contains the substantive parental-authority regime. The relevant provisions have been repeatedly reformed since 1990, most importantly by the 2007 amendment (Law No. 8720) which introduced the **right of visitation** at art. 837-2 and reframed the post-divorce parental-authority regime at art. 909(4); the 2011 amendment (Law No. 10645) which introduced the **welfare-of-the-child paramountcy standard** at art. 912 and the **appointment-of-parental-authority-holder** mechanism at art. 909-2; the 2014 – 2015 amendments on temporary restriction of parental authority; and the 2021 amendment (Law No. 17905 of 8 January 2021) which **removed art. 915** (the former "right of correction" clause) from the Civil Act as part of the reform against physical discipline. Four provisions carry the operational anti-alienation weight: **art. 837** (post-divorce agreement or family-court decision on the person raising the child, on maintenance, and on visitation, with welfare-of-the-child paramountcy); **art. 837-2** (mutual right of visitation of the non-caregiving parent *and the child*, with court modification and restriction jurisdiction); **art. 909(4)** (post-divorce parental-authority holder by agreement or, failing agreement, by family court decision); **art. 912** (welfare-of-the-child paramountcy as substantive standard for exercise of chinkwon and appointment of parental-authority holder). The Korean text below is drawn from the consolidated National Law Information Center (law.go.kr) publication; the English is a working translation for research use. The Korean original controls.

## Statute — Verbatim Text

### Art. 837 CA — Care and Custody of the Child After Divorce

**Korean (consolidated text):**

> ① 당사자는 그 자의 양육에 관한 사항을 협의에 의하여 정한다.
>
> ② 제1항의 협의는 다음 각 호의 사항을 포함하여야 한다.
>
> 1. 양육자의 결정
>
> 2. 양육비용의 부담
>
> 3. 면접교섭권의 행사 여부 및 그 방법
>
> ③ 제1항에 따른 협의가 이루어지지 아니하거나 협의할 수 없는 때에는 가정법원은 당사자의 청구 또는 직권으로 그 자의 의사·연령과 부모의 재산상황, 그 밖의 사정을 참작하여 양육에 필요한 사항을 정한다.
>
> ④ 양육에 관한 사항의 협의 또는 심판이 자의 복리에 반하는 것으로 인정되는 경우 가정법원은 보정을 명하거나 직권으로 그 자의 의사·연령과 부모의 재산상황, 그 밖의 사정을 참작하여 이를 변경할 수 있다.
>
> ⑤ 가정법원은 자의 복리를 위하여 필요하다고 인정하는 경우에는 부·모·자 및 검사의 청구 또는 직권으로 자의 양육에 관한 사항을 정한다.
>
> ⑥ 제3항부터 제5항까지의 규정은 양육에 관한 사항 외에는 부모의 권리의무에 변경을 가져오지 아니한다.

**English (working translation):**

> (1) The parties shall determine by agreement the matters concerning the raising of the child.
>
> (2) The agreement under paragraph (1) shall include the following matters:
>
>   1. Determination of the person raising the child;
>
>   2. Sharing of the costs of raising the child;
>
>   3. Whether the right of visitation is to be exercised, and the manner of its exercise.
>
> (3) Where the agreement under paragraph (1) has not been reached or cannot be reached, the family court shall, upon application of a party or on its own motion, determine the matters necessary for the raising of the child, taking into account the child's opinion and age, the property situation of the parents, and other circumstances.
>
> (4) Where the agreement or the adjudication on the matters concerning the raising of the child is found to be contrary to the welfare of the child, the family court may order rectification, or, on its own motion, may modify it, taking into account the child's opinion and age, the property situation of the parents, and other circumstances.
>
> (5) Where the family court finds it necessary for the welfare of the child, it shall, upon application of the father, the mother, the child, or the public prosecutor, or on its own motion, determine the matters concerning the raising of the child.
>
> (6) The provisions of paragraphs (3) through (5) shall not bring about a change in the rights and duties of the parents beyond the matters concerning the raising of the child.

**Operational note:** Art. 837 is the core post-divorce raising jurisdiction. Four features:

  - **Paragraph 2 mandatory-inclusion list.** Any parental agreement must include all three items — the raising person, the cost sharing, and the visitation. Silence on visitation is a defect that triggers art. 837(3) court determination.
  - **Paragraph 4 rectification/modification jurisdiction.** Where an agreement or adjudication is contrary to the welfare of the child, the family court may order rectification *or* modify it on its own motion. The trigger is welfare-inconsistency, not agreement failure. This is a strong retained-jurisdiction provision.
  - **Paragraph 5 sua sponte jurisdiction.** The family court may act on its own motion where welfare requires — a rare but not unknown feature in comparative family law.
  - **Paragraph 6 substance-preservation clause.** The paragraph-3-to-5 raising-of-child determinations do not affect the substantive rights and duties of the parents beyond raising. This is the doctrinal footing for the separation of *chinkwon* (parental authority, art. 909) from *yangyuk* (raising, art. 837): the raising designation is about physical care and day-to-day; parental authority is the substantive right structure that survives it.

### Art. 837-2 CA — Right of Visitation (added 2007, amended 2016)

**Korean (consolidated text):**

> ① 자를 직접 양육하지 아니하는 부모 중 일방과 자는 상호 면접교섭을 할 수 있는 권리를 가진다.
>
> ② 자를 직접 양육하지 아니하는 부모의 일방의 직계존속은 그 부모 일방이 사망하였거나 질병, 외국 거주, 그 밖에 불가피한 사정으로 자를 면접교섭할 수 없는 경우 가정법원에 자와의 면접교섭을 청구할 수 있다. 이 경우 가정법원은 자의 의사, 면접교섭을 청구한 사람과 자의 관계, 청구의 동기, 그 밖의 사정을 참작하여야 한다.
>
> ③ 가정법원은 자의 복리를 위하여 필요한 때에는 당사자의 청구 또는 직권에 의하여 면접교섭을 제한·배제·변경할 수 있다.
