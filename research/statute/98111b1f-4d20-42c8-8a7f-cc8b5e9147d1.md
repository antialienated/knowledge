# Codice Civile art. 337-quater — Affidamento ad un solo genitore

# Codice Civile art. 337-quater — Affidamento ad un solo genitore

> **TL;DR.** Italian Codice Civile article 337-quater is the **statute governing exclusive custody (affido esclusivo)** — the residual exception to the joint-custody-default established by art. 337-ter. **First paragraph**: the court may order exclusive custody where, with motivated reasoning, it finds that joint custody to the other parent would be contrary to the child's interest. **Second paragraph**: either parent may apply at any time for exclusive custody; if the court grants it, the rights of the child under art. 337-ter first paragraph (balanced and continuous relationship with each parent) are preserved as far as possible. **Critically**, if the application is "manifestly unfounded," the court may **consider the applicant parent's conduct in determining provisions in the children's interest** — a direct anti-strategic-litigation provision. **Third paragraph**: the sole-custody parent exercises parental responsibility exclusively subject to the court's conditions, but **major decisions remain joint**, and the non-custodial parent retains the vigilance role over education and may apply to the court if prejudicial decisions are made. The Cassazione (Cass. civ. 31571/2024) has affirmed that **"super-exclusive custody" (affidamento super-esclusivo)** is a **further residual measure applicable only in situations of extreme gravity** — where one parent may make all decisions without consulting the other, a tier above ordinary affido esclusivo.

## Full statutory text — verbatim Italian

### Art. 337-quater — Affidamento ad un solo genitore e opposizione all'affidamento condiviso

**Primo comma.** Il giudice può disporre l'affidamento dei figli ad uno solo dei genitori qualora ritenga con provvedimento motivato che l'affidamento all'altro sia contrario all'interesse del minore.

**Secondo comma.** Ciascuno dei genitori può, in qualsiasi momento, chiedere l'affidamento esclusivo quando sussistono le condizioni indicate al primo comma. Il giudice, se accoglie la domanda, dispone l'affidamento esclusivo al genitore istante, facendo salvi, per quanto possibile, i diritti del minore previsti dal primo comma dell'articolo 337 ter. Se la domanda risulta manifestamente infondata, il giudice può considerare il comportamento del genitore istante ai fini della determinazione dei provvedimenti da adottare nell'interesse dei figli, rimanendo ferma l'applicazione dell'articolo 96 del codice di procedura civile.

**Terzo comma.** Il genitore cui sono affidati i figli in via esclusiva, salva diversa disposizione del giudice, ha l'esercizio esclusivo della responsabilità genitoriale su di essi; egli deve attenersi alle condizioni determinate dal giudice. Salvo che non sia diversamente stabilito, le decisioni di maggiore interesse per i figli sono adottate da entrambi i genitori. Il genitore cui i figli non sono affidati ha il diritto ed il dovere di vigilare sulla loro istruzione ed educazione e può ricorrere al giudice quando ritenga che siano state assunte decisioni pregiudizievoli al loro interesse.

## English translation (working — not an official translation)

### Article 337-quater — Custody to one parent and opposition to joint custody

**First paragraph.** The court may order the custody of the children to one parent only where it finds, by reasoned ruling, that custody to the other parent would be contrary to the interest of the minor.

**Second paragraph.** Each parent may, at any time, request exclusive custody where the conditions indicated in the first paragraph exist. The court, if it grants the request, orders exclusive custody to the requesting parent, preserving as far as possible the rights of the minor provided for in the first paragraph of article 337-ter. **If the request is manifestly unfounded, the court may consider the conduct of the requesting parent for the purposes of determining the provisions to be adopted in the interest of the children**, leaving the application of article 96 of the Code of Civil Procedure unaffected.

**Third paragraph.** The parent to whom the children are entrusted exclusively, unless otherwise ordered by the court, holds the exclusive exercise of parental responsibility over them; that parent must comply with the conditions determined by the court. Unless otherwise established, decisions of greater interest for the children shall be taken by both parents. The parent to whom the children are not entrusted has the right and the duty to oversee their instruction and education and may apply to the court where prejudicial decisions to their interest are made.

## Why article 337-quater matters for parental-alienation analysis

Article 337-quater is the **canonical statutory mechanism for displacing the joint-custody default of art. 337-ter where PA-pattern conduct has rendered joint custody contrary to the child's interest**. The provision operates symmetrically — it can be used to grant exclusive custody to either parent, but in PA-context litigation it is most often invoked **by the target parent seeking exclusive custody where the other parent has engaged in sustained alienating conduct**.
