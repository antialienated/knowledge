# Codice Civile art. 337-octies — Poteri del giudice e ascolto del minore

> **TL;DR.** Italian Codice Civile article 337-octies was the **canonical listening-of-the-minor (ascolto del minore) and mediation-referral statute** from 2013 to 2023. **First paragraph**: before adopting provisions under art. 337-ter (joint-custody-default + bigenitoriality + non-compliance modification), the court must order the **hearing of the minor child aged 12+** — and also of a minor under 12 **if capable of discernment** (se capace di discernimento). In proceedings where the court is approving a parental agreement on custody, hearing is dispensed only where it would be contrary to the child's interest or manifestly superfluous. **Second paragraph**: where appropriate, the court may, with party consent, defer adopting art. 337-ter provisions to allow the spouses, with expert assistance, to attempt mediation focused on the child's moral and material interest.
>
> ⚠ **The article was abrogated effective 28 February 2023** by D.Lgs. 149/2022 (Riforma Cartabia). The current regime is found in the **new Codice di Procedura Civile articles 473-bis 4 (right of the minor to be heard) and 473-bis 5 (modalities)** which apply to proceedings instituted on or after 28 February 2023. Per the transitional rule (art. 35 D.Lgs. 149/2022), proceedings instituted before that date remain governed by art. 337-octies.

## Full statutory text — verbatim Italian (article as in force 2013-2023)

### Art. 337-octies — Poteri del giudice e ascolto del minore

**Primo comma.** Prima dell'emanazione, anche in via provvisoria, dei provvedimenti di cui all'articolo 337 ter, il giudice può assumere, ad istanza di parte o d'ufficio, mezzi di prova. Il giudice dispone, inoltre, l'ascolto del figlio minore che abbia compiuto gli anni dodici e anche di età inferiore ove capace di discernimento. Nei procedimenti in cui si omologa o si prende atto di un accordo dei genitori, relativo alle condizioni di affidamento dei figli, il giudice non procede all'ascolto se in contrasto con l'interesse del minore o manifestamente superfluo.

**Secondo comma.** Qualora ne ravvisi l'opportunità, il giudice, sentite le parti e ottenuto il loro consenso, può rinviare l'adozione dei provvedimenti di cui all'articolo 337 ter per consentire che i coniugi, avvalendosi di esperti, tentino una mediazione per raggiungere un accordo, con particolare riferimento alla tutela dell'interesse morale e materiale dei figli.

## English translation (working — not an official translation)

### Article 337-octies — Court powers and the hearing of the minor

**First paragraph.** Before adopting, even provisionally, the provisions referred to in article 337-ter, the court may take evidence, on the application of a party or on its own motion. The court further orders the hearing of the minor child who has reached the age of twelve, and also of a minor of a younger age where capable of discernment. In proceedings in which the court approves or takes note of an agreement of the parents regarding the conditions of custody of the children, the court does not proceed to the hearing if this is contrary to the interest of the minor or manifestly superfluous.

**Second paragraph.** Where appropriate, the court, having heard the parties and obtained their consent, may defer the adoption of the provisions referred to in article 337-ter to allow the spouses, with the assistance of experts, to attempt mediation to reach an agreement, with particular reference to the protection of the moral and material interest of the children.

## Current law — Riforma Cartabia (D.Lgs. 149/2022, effective 28 February 2023)

The Riforma Cartabia abolished art. 337-octies and substituted a **generalised right of the minor to be heard** in family proceedings, codified in new articles of the Codice di Procedura Civile (c.p.c.):

- **Art. 473-bis 4 c.p.c. (Diritto del minore all'ascolto)** — establishes the right of the minor to be heard "in proceedings in which provisions to be adopted concern the minor." The hearing is now framed as a **fundamental right of the minor** (diritto fondamentale del minore), elevating it from a procedural duty of the court (the art. 337-octies framing) to a substantive right held by the child. The age threshold remains at 12+ with discernment-based hearings below 12. **New** provisions allow the court not to proceed with the hearing where the minor has physical or psychological inability, or where the minor expresses the wish not to be heard.

- **Art. 473-bis 5 c.p.c. (Modalità dell'ascolto)** — sets out the procedural modalities. The court must: (i) explain to the minor "the nature of the proceedings and the effects of the hearing" and ask whether the minor wishes to be heard; (ii) conduct the hearing in conditions guaranteeing the minor's serenity and confidentiality; (iii) **audio-visually record the hearing** (no facultative non-recording — only "technical reasons" permit omission); (iv) prepare a detailed verbale recording the minor's behaviour. The court must guard against secondary victimisation (vittimizzazione secondaria) from the audio-visual procedure.

- **Art. 152-quinquies disp. att. c.p.c.** — provides for technical regulations on audio-visual recording, conservation, and inclusion in the digital case file (fascicolo telematico).

The substantive content at **art. 315-bis Codice Civile, 3° comma** (right of the minor to be heard in matters affecting them) is unaffected by the Cartabia reform and remains the codice civile-level right.
