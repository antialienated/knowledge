# Barnelova § 48 — Det viktigaste omsynet til barnets beste (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Norway · **Citation:** *Lov om barn og foreldre* (barnelova) 8 april 1981 nr 7, § 48 · **Authoritative source:** [lovdata.no — barnelova § 48](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1981-04-08-7/§48)

The **welfare paramountcy + violence-screen** in Norwegian family law. Barnelova § 48 establishes that *avgjerder om foreldreansvar, kvar barnet skal bu fast og samvær* (decisions on parental responsibility, residence, and contact) shall be made *først og fremst* (first and foremost) on the basis of what is best for the child. The second paragraph imports the violence-and-abuse screen as a structural welfare consideration.

The provision is the Norwegian structural parallel to:
- [Sweden Föräldrabalken 6 kap. 2a (barnets bästa)](../sweden/foraldrabalken-kap-6-2a-barnets-basta-verbatim.md)
- [Denmark Forældreansvarsloven § 4 (barnets bedste)](../denmark/foraeldreansvarsloven-4-11-21-samvaer-verbatim.md)
- [Finland Lapsenhuoltolaki § 9 (lapsen etu)](../finland/lapsenhuoltolaki-1-9-10-tapaamisoikeus-verbatim.md)
- [Germany BGB § 1697a (Kindeswohlprinzip)](../germany/bgb-1697a-kindeswohlprinzip-verbatim.md)

Norway is doctrinally distinctive among the Nordic jurisdictions because § 48 second paragraph expressly mandates that *omsynet til at barnet ikkje må bli utsett for vald eller på anna vis bli handsama slik at den fysiske eller psykiske helsa vert utsett for skade eller fare* (regard for the child's protection from violence or other treatment endangering physical or mental health) be *taken into account* as a structural welfare factor — making Norway one of the few European jurisdictions with express welfare-statutory violence-screening in the paramountcy provision itself.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### § 48 — Det viktigaste omsynet til barnets beste

> Avgjerder om foreldreansvar, om kvar barnet skal bu fast og om samvær, og handsaminga av slike saker, skal først og fremst rette seg etter det som er best for barnet.
>
> Ved avgjerda skal det takast omsyn til at barnet ikkje må bli utsett for vald eller på anna vis bli handsama slik at den fysiske eller psykiske helsa vert utsett for skade eller fare.

## English working translation

### § 48 — The paramount consideration of the child's best interests

> Decisions on parental responsibility (*foreldreansvar*), on *where the child shall reside permanently*, and on *contact* (*samvær*), and the processing of such matters, shall *first and foremost* be directed by what is *best for the child* (*best for barnet*).
>
> In the decision, *regard shall be taken* (*takast omsyn*) to the fact that the child must not be subjected to *violence* (*vald*) or otherwise be treated in such a way that the child's *physical or mental health* is exposed to *injury or danger* (*skade eller fare*).

## Why this matters for PA

- **The først og fremst paramountcy formulation.** *Først og fremst rette seg etter det som er best for barnet.* Decisions shall *first and foremost* be directed by what is best for the child. This is the Norwegian welfare-paramountcy formulation — operationally equivalent to the UK *paramount consideration*, the Swedish *avgörande*, and the German *am besten entspricht*. The first-and-foremost phrasing positions welfare as the structural starting point of decision-making.
- **The triad of decisions (first paragraph).** § 48 covers three categories of decision:
  - *Foreldreansvar* — parental responsibility
  - *Kvar barnet skal bu fast* — residence (permanent residence)
  - *Samvær* — contact
- This is the structural Norwegian decision-framework — § 48 paramountcy applies to all three. In PA-pattern cases this means the welfare standard governs *both* residence transfer applications *and* contact restriction applications symmetrically.
- **The violence-and-abuse screen (second paragraph).** *Vald eller på anna vis bli handsama slik at den fysiske eller psykiske helsa vert utsett for skade eller fare.* Violence or treatment endangering the child's *physical or mental health*. This is doctrinally distinctive — the welfare provision itself expressly incorporates the violence-screen, including *psychological* harm (*psykisk helse … skade eller fare*).
- **The PA-pattern significance of the psykisk-helse screen.** In sustained PA-pattern cases the alienating conduct constitutes treatment endangering the child's *psykisk helse*. The Norwegian second-paragraph screen is therefore engageable in two complementary directions:
  - DV findings against the targeted parent can engage the screen (the standard application)
  - Sustained alienating conduct by the resident parent can engage the screen (the symmetric application — endangering the child's psychological health)
- The Norwegian Høgsterett has confirmed the symmetric application — see *HR-2019-1948-A* and the broader *Strand Lobben* line.
- **The procedural prong — handsaminga av slike saker.** *Handsaminga av slike saker* — the *processing of such matters*. This is doctrinally important: § 48 paramountcy applies not only to substantive decisions but also to the *handsaminga* (processing) of the case. In PA-pattern cases this means procedural choices (which expert to appoint, whether to engage *sakkunnig*, how to hear the child) must themselves be welfare-paramountcy-compliant.
- **The structural relationship with the Strand Lobben line.** The Norwegian Høgsterett's reception of *Strand Lobben v Norway* (ECtHR, GC 2019) reshaped the operational application of § 48 — particularly on the procedural-prong dimension.
