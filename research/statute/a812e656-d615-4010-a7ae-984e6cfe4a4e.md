# Graduated-remedy ladder for alienating-conduct cases — Italy, Germany, Austria, UK comparative

**Jurisdictions covered:** Italy · Germany · Austria · England & Wales

A comparative-law map of the **graduated remedy ladder** — the structural framework that escalates from intermediate measures (counselling, supervised contact, contact custodian) through tier-2 (sole custody / parental-responsibility restriction) to tier-3 (deprivation of parental responsibility / care order) in sustained alienating-conduct cases. The four jurisdictions covered here have all developed coherent graduated frameworks but operate with structurally distinct architectures.

## The four-tier remedy structure

| Tier | Italy | Germany | Austria | UK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1**<br/>*Counselling / mediation* | None codified (judicial *raccomandazione*) | § 156 FamFG *Erziehungsberatung* | § 107 AußStrG *Erziehungsberatung* | s. 16 Children Act *Family Assistance Order* |
| **Tier 2a**<br/>*Contact-arrangement modification* | art. 337-ter CC | § 1684 (4) BGB | § 187 (2) ABGB | s. 8 CA 1989 *contact order* |
| **Tier 2b**<br/>*Contact custodian / third party intervention* | (no direct equivalent) | § 1684 (3) BGB *Umgangspflegschaft* | (analogous via § 187 (2)) | s. 16 *Family Assistance Order* + *guardian ad litem* under s. 41 |
| **Tier 3**<br/>*Intermediate measures against alienating conduct* | **art. 333 CC** *condotta pregiudizievole* | § 1666 BGB *Kindeswohlgefährdung* (lower threshold) | (escalation via § 187 (2)) | s. 31 CA 1989 *supervision order* |
| **Tier 4**<br/>*Sole custody / residence change* | art. 337-quater CC *affidamento esclusivo* | § 1671 BGB *Alleinsorge* | § 177 ABGB *Obsorge-Übertragung* | s. 8 CA 1989 *residence order* (now *child arrangements order*) |
| **Tier 5**<br/>*Decadenza / deprivation of parental responsibility* | **art. 330 CC** *decadenza* | § 1666 BGB (severe Kindeswohlgefährdung) | § 181 ABGB *Entzug der Obsorge* | s. 31 CA 1989 *care order* |
| **Tier 6**<br/>*Rehabilitation / restoration* | **art. 332 CC** *reintegrazione* | § 1696 BGB *Abänderung gerichtlicher Entscheidungen* | § 182 ABGB *Wiederherstellung der Obsorge* | discharge of care order under s. 39 |

## Structural typology

### Italian model — explicit three-step graduated escalation

Italy is the cleanest example of an **express graduated framework**:

- art. 333 = intermediate measures (*provvedimenti convenienti*) for *condotta pregiudizievole*
- art. 330 = maximum-tier *decadenza* for *grave pregiudizio*
- art. 332 = rehabilitation route back from art. 330

The Cassazione has explicit graduated-escalation doctrine: art. 333 must usually be exhausted (or shown demonstrably inadequate) before art. 330 is sought. This is the most structurally coherent framework of the four.

### German model — Kindeswohlgefährdung as unified threshold

Germany operates a **threshold-based** framework rather than express tiers:

- § 1684 (3) BGB *Umgangspflegschaft* is available for breach of the *Wohlverhaltensklausel* without requiring a Kindeswohlgefährdung finding
- § 1671 BGB sole-custody-on-application is independent of the welfare-jeopardy threshold
- § 1666 BGB engages once the Kindeswohlgefährdung threshold is met — and offers the full spectrum from milder measures (e.g. supervised contact, prohibition on specific conduct) up to the most severe (full removal of parental authority)
- § 1666 (3) BGB explicitly enumerates measures available "in particular"

The doctrinal advantage: the German framework is highly flexible — once the welfare-jeopardy threshold is crossed, the court has wide discretion to fashion the remedy proportionate to the conduct. The doctrinal disadvantage: less structural transparency than the Italian three-step.

### Austrian model — Obsorge-based escalation

Austria's framework is anchored on the *Obsorge* (parental responsibility) concept:

- § 187 (2) ABGB — court must restrict or prohibit contact where the non-residential parent breaches § 159 *Wohlverhaltensgebot*
- § 177 ABGB — transfer of *Obsorge* to one parent
- § 181 ABGB — full *Entzug der Obsorge* (deprivation of parental responsibility) in the most severe cases

Austria's framework most closely resembles Italy's structurally: the three tiers (contact restriction → custody transfer → Obsorge deprivation) parallel the Italian art. 333 / art. 337-quater / art. 330 ladder.
